The average knowledge worker spends 6+ hours per week in meetings and retains roughly 40% of what was discussed. AI meeting transcription tools fix this — they join your calls, record, transcribe, summarize, and make every conversation searchable. But the four leading tools serve fundamentally different users, and picking the wrong one means paying for features you will never use.

We compared Otter.ai, Fireflies.ai, Fathom, and WhisperX across real-time capability, CRM integration, free tier generosity, and offline support. Here is which one fits your workflow.

## Otter.ai: For Real-Time Transcription

Otter.ai is the only major meeting assistant that transcribes in real time — words appear on screen as people speak, not after the meeting ends. This changes how you use the tool: you can search the transcript during the call, highlight decisions as they happen, and leave the meeting knowing the notes are already done.

The free tier is genuinely useful: 300 minutes per month, no credit card required. For a student recording lectures or a journalist recording interviews, this is enough for 10 half-hour sessions per month at zero cost. The mobile apps make in-person recording simple — put your phone on the table and Otter handles the rest.

The downsides are real. Pro plan minutes got cut from 6,000 to 1,200 in 2025 without a proportional price drop. File upload limits are the stingiest in the category: 3 lifetime uploads on Free, 10 per month on Pro. CRM integration exists only on the Enterprise plan, and there is no public API to access your own transcripts.

Otter is the best pick for general-purpose meeting documentation — product teams, journalists, students, operations, anyone who wants to watch transcription happen live and collaborate on shared meeting notes. It is not the pick for sales teams with CRM workflows.

**Best for: real-time transcription, cross-department teams, mobile recording.** [Read full Otter.ai review →](/reviews/otter-ai)

## Fireflies.ai: For Sales Teams and CRM

Fireflies.ai takes the opposite approach from Otter: transcripts arrive after the meeting, but what you do with them is where Fireflies excels. It auto-logs call notes to Salesforce, HubSpot, Pipedrive, or Zoho. It detects competitor mentions, scores rep performance against custom scorecards, and tracks talk-time ratios across your team.

AskFred — the cross-meeting AI assistant — is the feature that makes Fireflies worth $10/month. Ask "what objections did Acme Corp raise across our last three calls?" and Fred searches every transcript, timestamps its answers, and links back to the recordings. This is not a gimmick — it saves 5-10 minutes of manual transcript digging per question.

The catch is the AI Credits system. "Unlimited transcription" on Pro is real, but AskFred queries and conversation intelligence draw from a separate credit pool that runs out. A heavy user asking a dozen questions per call across 10 calls per week will burn through credits and need to buy top-ups ($5 per 50 credits).

Fireflies is the pick for sales teams that live in their CRM. If you are not on Salesforce or HubSpot, and you do not need call scoring or deal tracking, Fireflies is overkill — you are paying for infrastructure you will not use.

**Best for: sales teams, CRM auto-logging, conversation intelligence.** [Read full Fireflies.ai review →](/reviews/fireflies-ai)

## Fathom: For the Best Free Tier

Fathom's free plan is the strongest in the category: unlimited recordings, unlimited transcription, AI summaries, and highlights — all for one user at zero cost. No time caps, no credit systems, no forced upgrade. If you are a solo creator or individual contributor who just wants to stop taking notes during calls, Fathom is the place to start.

Post-meeting summaries arrive fast — typically within 30 seconds of the call ending. The UI is clean, the setup takes under 15 minutes, and the free tier is genuinely not a bait-and-switch. Fathom makes money on team plans ($19/user/month) with collaborative features, not by squeezing free users.

The trade-offs: no real-time transcription (transcripts arrive after the call), CRM integrations are improving but still basic compared to Fireflies, and the feature set is optimized for individual productivity rather than team-wide intelligence. If you need cross-meeting search across a team's full meeting history, Fathom is not built for that.

For solos, Fathom is the default recommendation. Start with the free tier. If you outgrow it, you will know exactly which features you need — and that is when Otter or Fireflies become worth paying for.

**Best for: solo users, unlimited free transcription, fastest setup.** [Visit Fathom](/reviews/fathom)

## WhisperX: For Offline and Privacy

WhisperX is the outlier in this group — it is not a meeting tool. It is an open-source transcription engine you run on your own hardware. No cloud, no subscription, no bot joining your calls. Audio stays on your machine.

It delivers 3.2% word error rate on standard English, speaker diarization (who said what), and word-level timestamps. It supports 99+ languages. It costs nothing to use — but you need a GPU, command-line comfort, and the patience to set up a Python environment.

The use case is specific: you have privacy requirements that forbid cloud transcription, or you transcribe 50+ hours of audio per month and the API costs of managed services exceed the cost of buying a GPU. For developers, researchers, and compliance-heavy industries, WhisperX is the only option that guarantees zero data leakage.

It is not a meeting assistant — it will not join your Zoom calls or send you email summaries. But if you need transcription without a third party listening in, nothing else comes close.

## FAQ

**Which tool is the most accurate?** WhisperX leads on open benchmarks at 3.2% WER. Among the cloud tools, all three deliver ~90-95% accuracy for clear single-speaker English. Accuracy drops across the board with accents, overlapping speech, and background noise. No tool handles these perfectly.

**Which free tier is best?** Fathom — unlimited recordings for one user with zero cost. Otter's 300 minutes/month is the next best for light users. Fireflies' 800-minute storage cap fills up in about 15 hours of meetings. WhisperX is entirely free if you have a GPU.

**Can these tools transcribe languages other than English?** Fireflies supports 60+ languages, WhisperX supports 99+. Otter supports English, Spanish, and French. Fathom is primarily English. If multilingual transcription matters, Fireflies or WhisperX is the pick.

**Do all of these join meetings as a visible bot?** Otter and Fireflies join as named participants ("Otter.ai Notetaker" / "Fireflies.ai Notetaker") that are visible to all attendees. Fathom also joins as a visible participant. WhisperX does not join meetings at all — you feed it audio files manually. There is no "invisible mode" on any of these tools.

**What is the best tool for a team of 10+?** Fireflies.ai for sales teams (CRM integration + coaching). Otter.ai for cross-functional teams (collaborative editing + shared workspace). Fathom Team at $29/user/month for lightweight team features without the CRM overhead.

**Can I use these for offline file transcription?** WhisperX is purpose-built for this. The cloud tools all support file uploads with varying limits: Fireflies charges extra per uploaded file, Otter caps free users at 3 lifetime uploads, Fathom handles uploads on paid plans. If offline file transcription is your primary use case, skip the meeting tools and use WhisperX.
