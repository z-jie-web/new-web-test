Every AI image generator can turn text into a picture in 2026. That problem is solved. The question that really matters now: can you get the same character across 20 images? Edit specific regions without regenerating the whole thing? Produce production-ready assets, not just impressive one-offs?

I compared the six major platforms against real creative workflows, not benchmark scores. Here is the honest answer.

## The 30-Second Verdict

- **If you make game assets or need creative control:** [Leonardo AI](https://leonardo.ai). Nothing else comes close on workflow.
- **If you want the most beautiful images with zero effort:** [Midjourney](https://www.midjourney.com/). Discord interface aside, the output is still the benchmark.
- **If you hate learning prompt syntax:** [DALL·E](https://openai.com/dall-e-3) via ChatGPT. Talk to it like a person.
- **If you want unlimited free generation and privacy:** [Stable Diffusion](https://stability.ai/). Self-hosted, no restrictions.
- **If commercial copyright keeps you up at night:** [Adobe Firefly](https://www.adobe.com/products/firefly.html). IP indemnification. Nobody else offers it.
- **If you need editable vector logos and brand illustrations:** [Recraft](/reviews/recraft). SVG export, Brand Kit, style training. Built for graphic designers.

## Leonardo AI: For People Who Really Make Things

Leonardo is not trying to be the prettiest. Midjourney wins on aesthetics. Leonardo wins on usefulness. If Midjourney's Discord workflow or pricing is a dealbreaker, we've rounded up the [best Midjourney alternatives for every budget →](/blog/midjourney-alternatives).

The platform ships 50+ fine-tuned models for specific aesthetics, cinematic realism, anime, game concept art, product photography. The Phoenix model (built from scratch, not fine-tuned from Stable Diffusion) hits roughly 95% prompt adherence. It renders readable text within images. Most AI models still struggle with that.

The real differentiators: AI Canvas (infinite workspace, inpainting, outpainting, real-time prompt editing) and 3D Texture Generation (albedo, normal, roughness maps ready for Unity and Unreal). If you are a game developer, concept artist, or marketer who needs consistent brand imagery, these are not gimmicks, they replace hours of manual work.

Free tier: 150 tokens/day, no credit card. Most generous in the category. Downsides: token consumption varies wildly by settings, and aggressive content filters occasionally block harmless prompts.

## Midjourney: Still the Prettiest

Midjourney understands composition, lighting, color theory, and artistic style better than anything else. Type a prompt, get something beautiful. The model's aesthetic judgment is the core product.

The catch is the interface. Discord-only (a web interface is slowly rolling out). No canvas. No inpainting. No direct integration with creative software. The Discord workflow works but feels like 2022 in a 2026 market.

$10/month, no free tier, no way to test before paying. Worth it if output quality is your only metric. Not worth it if you need creative control beyond prompting.

## DALL·E (ChatGPT): The One You Already Know How to Use

DALL·E's integration with ChatGPT is its real advantage. No prompt syntax to learn. No model quirks to memorize. You describe what you want, ChatGPT translates it, you refine through conversation: "make the background darker," "change to watercolor," "add a dog in the corner."

The trade-off is depth. Fewer controls than Leonardo. No custom model training. No LoRAs. For casual users, marketers needing quick visuals, and anyone frustrated by traditional prompting, DALL·E is the best starting point.

## Stable Diffusion: The Power User's Choice

Stable Diffusion is not a product, it is an ecosystem. Run it locally (no internet, full privacy). Fine-tune it on your own datasets. Extend it with thousands of community models and LoRAs from Civitai.

The cost is setup complexity. Python dependencies. A capable GPU. Choosing a front-end (Automatic1111, ComfyUI, InvokeAI). For developers, researchers, and privacy-first creators, the unlimited free generation and total control make it worth the effort. For everyone else, Leonardo or Midjourney.

## Adobe Firefly: The Lawyer-Approved Option

Firefly is trained exclusively on licensed and public domain content. That is its entire pitch, and for commercial work where copyright exposure matters, it is a strong one. Adobe offers IP indemnification for enterprise customers. No other major AI image generator does.

Firefly integrates into Photoshop, Illustrator, and Adobe Express. $5/month. The weakness: generative quality trails Midjourney and Leonardo. Firefly is not the most creative tool. It is the safest one.

## Recraft: The Vector Specialist

Most AI image generators produce raster images — pixels. Recraft produces vectors. SVG logos, brand marks, icon sets, illustrations that stay sharp at any size and open directly in Figma or Illustrator.

The Brand Kit feature is what makes Recraft different. You upload your logo, pick brand colors and fonts, and the model generates on-brand assets that match your visual identity. No more explaining your brand guidelines in every prompt. Style training lets you teach Recraft your illustration style from a few reference images, then generate new pieces that stay consistent.

Recraft is not a general-purpose image generator. It will not give you photorealistic portraits or cinematic concept art. It is built for one workflow: graphic designers who need production-ready vector output. If that is you, it solves a problem no other AI tool addresses well.

Free tier available. Paid plans start at $10/month for higher resolution exports and commercial licensing.

## FAQ

**Which is best for beginners?** DALL·E via ChatGPT, conversational iteration is the most forgiving learning curve. Leonardo's free tier (150 tokens/day, no credit card) is also excellent.

**Which produces the highest quality?** Midjourney for raw aesthetics. Leonardo's Phoenix model for prompt adherence and text rendering. [Flux](/reviews/flux) by Black Forest Labs rivals both on photorealism with the added benefit of open-weight models and per-image API pricing. For text-heavy images, logos, posters, quote graphics, [Ideogram](/reviews/ideogram) is the specialist with 93% typographic accuracy that no other generator matches. For vector logos, brand illustrations, and editable SVG output, [Recraft](/reviews/recraft) is purpose-built for graphic design workflows.

**Can I use AI images commercially?** Yes on paid plans. Adobe Firefly offers the strongest legal protection (IP indemnification). Free tiers almost never include commercial rights.

**Which is best for game development?** Leonardo AI. 3D texture generation, consistent character tools, and 50+ specialized models. No other platform matches its game asset workflow.

**What should I really do now?** Pick the tool that matches your primary use case. Use the free tier. Generate 20 images for a real project. If the output saves you time or makes you money, upgrade. If not, try the next one.
