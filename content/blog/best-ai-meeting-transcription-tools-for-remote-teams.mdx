Remote teams live and die by meetings. But meetings without documentation are just conversations that evaporate. Someone forgets a decision. Someone else claims they never agreed to a deadline. Action items get lost in the noise.

AI meeting transcription tools solve this by recording, transcribing, and summarizing every call automatically. You focus on the conversation. The tool captures what was said, what was decided, and who owns what.

I tested the three most popular options for remote teams: [Otter.ai](/reviews/otter-ai), [Fireflies.ai](/reviews/fireflies-ai), and [Fathom](/reviews/fathom). Each has a different philosophy about how transcription should work in a team context.

## The quick verdict

- **Best for solo users on a budget:** [Otter.ai](/reviews/otter-ai) — generous free tier, solid transcription quality, simple interface
- **Best for teams needing CRM integration:** [Fireflies.ai](/reviews/fireflies-ai) — pushes meeting summaries to Salesforce, HubSpot, and 40+ other tools
- **Best for zero-setup simplicity:** [Fathom](/reviews/fathom) — no configuration needed, works immediately, completely free for personal use

## Otter.ai: The budget-friendly option

Otter.ai has been in the meeting transcription space longer than most competitors, and it shows in the polish. The free tier gives you 300 minutes of transcription per month — enough for about 10 one-hour meetings. The Pro plan at $16.99/month gives you 1,200 minutes.

**What works for remote teams:**
- Real-time transcription during Zoom, Google Meet, and Microsoft Teams calls. You can see the transcript as the meeting happens.
- Automatic speaker identification. Otter learns to distinguish between team members after a few meetings.
- Shared workspaces for teams. Transcripts are organized by meeting and searchable across your entire history.
- The OtterPilot bot joins meetings automatically, even if you forget to start recording.

**What does not work:**
- Transcription quality drops with background noise or multiple people talking at once. This is a problem for lively team discussions.
- The free tier does not include team features like shared folders or admin controls. You need Pro or Business for real team collaboration.
- Action item extraction is manual — Otter transcribes what was said, but you have to identify the to-dos yourself.

For detailed pricing and features, see our [Otter.ai review](/reviews/otter-ai).

## Fireflies.ai: The crm-integrated option

Fireflies.ai is built for sales and customer success teams where meeting data needs to flow into other systems. Its standout feature is the 40+ integrations — Salesforce, HubSpot, Slack, Notion, Asana, and more. Every meeting summary can be pushed to the right place automatically.

**What works for remote teams:**
- Meeting summaries auto-sync to your CRM. After a customer call, the transcript, key topics, and action items appear in the contact record. No manual copy-paste.
- Conversation intelligence features: talk-to-listen ratio, sentiment analysis, topic tracking. For sales managers reviewing team performance, this is actionable data.
- Custom vocabulary and topic trackers. You can train Fireflies to flag specific terms or product names that matter to your business.
- The AskFred AI assistant lets you query meeting history in natural language. "What did the client say about budget last Tuesday?" gets a direct answer.

**What does not work:**
- The free tier is limited to 10 meetings. For a remote team with daily standups, you will hit the paywall in the first week. The Pro plan is $18/month per user.
- Setup is more involved than Fathom. You need to configure integrations, set up topic trackers, and train custom vocabulary. The ROI is there, but the onboarding takes a few hours.
- Transcription quality in non-English languages is weaker than English. For multilingual remote teams, this is a limitation.

For a full breakdown, see our [Fireflies.ai review](/reviews/fireflies-ai) and comparison of [Otter.ai vs Fireflies.ai](/compare/otter-ai-vs-fireflies-ai).

## Fathom: The zero-setup option

Fathom is the simplest meeting transcription tool I have used. You install it, it works. There is no configuration, no vocabulary training, no integration setup. It records, transcribes, and summarizes every Zoom call automatically.

**What works for remote teams:**
- Completely free for personal use. No minute limits, no feature gates. The free version does everything the paid version does, except for team features.
- Automatic AI summaries are genuinely useful. After every call, Fathom produces a structured summary with key decisions, action items, and follow-ups. Not a wall of text — a scannable document.
- One-click sharing. Send a meeting summary link to anyone, even people who were not on the call. The recipient sees the summary, full transcript, and recording in a clean interface.
- Works on Zoom, Google Meet, and Microsoft Teams without any per-platform configuration.

**What does not work:**
- Team features are only available on the paid plan ($19/month per user). The free version is single-user only.
- No CRM integrations. If your team needs meeting data in Salesforce or HubSpot, Fathom does not support it. You would need to use Zapier as a bridge.
- Limited to video calls. Fathom does not transcribe phone calls or in-person audio, unlike Otter.ai and Fireflies.

For the full evaluation, read our [Fathom review](/reviews/fathom).

## Which tool for which team

**Small team (2-5 people), casual meetings:** Fathom free. Zero setup, zero cost, summaries good enough for internal use.

**Sales or customer-facing team:** Fireflies.ai Pro. The CRM integration justifies the $18/month per user cost. Meeting data flowing into your pipeline automatically saves hours of manual work.

**Large team (10+ people), mixed use cases:** Otter.ai Business. The shared workspace, admin controls, and 1,200 monthly minutes scale better than the others for high-volume team usage.

## FAQ

**Do these tools work with phone calls, not just video meetings?**
Otter.ai supports phone call transcription through its mobile app. Fireflies supports it via dial-in numbers. Fathom only works with video calls (Zoom, Meet, Teams). If phone transcription is essential, Otter.ai is the safest choice.

**How accurate are the AI summaries?**
All three tools produce useful summaries for structured meetings. They struggle with informal conversations, brainstorming sessions, and meetings where people talk over each other. The summaries are a starting point, not a replacement for meeting notes — but they capture 80% of what matters.

**Can I use these tools for client-facing meetings?**
Yes, but check the notification settings. All three tools announce when recording starts (required by law in many jurisdictions). For client calls, this is standard practice and expected. Some teams use Fireflies' custom branding to make the recording notification look more professional.

**What about privacy and data security?**
All three tools encrypt data in transit and at rest. Fireflies is SOC 2 Type II certified. Otter.ai offers BAA for healthcare teams. Fathom is GDPR compliant. If your team handles sensitive data, check each tool's security documentation before adopting.
