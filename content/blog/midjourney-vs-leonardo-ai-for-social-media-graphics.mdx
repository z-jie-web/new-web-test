I needed 12 graphics for a LinkedIn carousel. Then 3 Instagram post images. Then a Twitter header. I tried doing all of them in Midjourney, got frustrated with the Discord-only interface, switched to Leonardo AI, liked the speed, but missed Midjourney's aesthetic quality.

[Midjourney](/reviews/midjourney) and [Leonardo AI](/reviews/leonardo-ai) are the two most popular AI image generators for social media content. They approach the problem differently enough that picking between them changes your workflow.

Here is what I learned from using both for a week of social media production.

## The Core Difference

Midjourney is an artist. You describe what you want, and it gives you something beautiful — often more beautiful than what you described. The aesthetic is distinctive. You can spot a Midjourney image in a feed. This is both its strength and its limitation for social media graphics.

Leonardo AI is a production tool. It has social media templates, aspect ratio presets, and a web interface designed for iteration. The output is good but not Midjourney-good. For most social media use cases, good is enough.

## Image Quality: Midjourney Wins

Midjourney produces more visually striking images. The lighting, composition, color harmony, and overall aesthetic polish are a clear tier above Leonardo AI. For social media graphics where visual quality drives engagement — Instagram feed posts, Pinterest pins, YouTube thumbnails — Midjourney output gets better results.

Midjourney v6.1 handles:
- Cinematic lighting with natural shadows and highlights
- Rich, complex color palettes that feel intentional
- Textures that look real (fabric, wood grain, skin, metal)
- Composition that follows photographic rules (rule of thirds, leading lines)
- Creative interpretation of prompts that adds visual interest you did not explicitly ask for

Leonardo AI's output is good enough for most social media. But "good enough" means you will occasionally need to generate 3-4 variations to get one that works, whereas Midjourney often delivers on the first or second try.

The gap narrows with Leonardo's newer models (Phoenix and Lightning), but Midjourney is still ahead on pure visual quality.

## Speed and Workflow: Leonardo AI Wins

Leonardo AI is faster and easier to use for social media production.

**Leonardo AI workflow advantages:**
- Web interface. Upload, prompt, generate, download — all in one window. No Discord commands to remember.
- Social media aspect ratio presets: 1:1 for Instagram, 16:9 for YouTube thumbnails, 9:16 for Stories, 4:5 for portrait feed posts
- 150 free daily tokens (about 30-50 images). For a social media manager producing daily content, this free tier is enough for most days.
- Image-to-image and inpainting on the free tier. Upload a rough reference image and Leonardo refines it. This is useful for maintaining visual brand consistency across posts.
- Batch generation: queue multiple prompts and pick the best results. For high-volume production, this saves real time.

**Midjourney workflow disadvantages:**
- Discord-first interface. You type `/imagine` commands in a chat window alongside thousands of other users generating images. The output scrolls past quickly. If you miss your generation, you have to scroll back.
- Pricing starts at $10/month. No free tier beyond a handful of trial generations. For a social media creator on a budget, this is a real cost.
- No built-in social media templates. You specify aspect ratios manually with `--ar` flags. Not hard, but an extra step every time.

## Free Tier Comparison

This is the deciding factor for many social media creators.

**Leonardo AI free tier:**
- 150 tokens per day (each image costs 3-5 tokens)
- Access to Phoenix, Lightning, and legacy models
- Image-to-image and inpainting included
- Alchemy (premium upscaling) on a limited basis
- No watermark on standard generations

**Midjourney:**
- No permanent free tier
- A small number of trial generations for new accounts
- $10/month Basic plan for approximately 200 images/month
- $30/month Standard plan for unlimited relaxed generations

For a creator who needs 5-10 social media images per day, Leonardo AI costs $0. Midjourney costs at least $10/month. Over a year, that is a $120 difference for roughly comparable output quality at the level most social media platforms require.

## Which Tool for Which Platform

| Platform | Best Tool | Why |

## FAQ

**Can I use Midjourney images commercially for client social media work?**
Yes, on paid plans. The free trial does not include commercial rights. The $30/month Standard plan includes a commercial license. Always check Midjourney's latest terms before using images for client work.

**Which tool is better for maintaining a consistent visual brand?**
Leonardo AI. Its image-to-image feature lets you upload a reference image (your brand colors, preferred style) and generate new images that match. Midjourney has style references but they are less precise. For brand consistency across posts, Leonardo AI gives you more control.

**Does the Discord interface actually matter?**
For occasional use, no. For daily professional use, yes — it gets frustrating. The scroll-to-find-your-generation problem alone adds friction to every session. Some Midjourney users switch to Leonardo AI just to escape the Discord interface, even though they prefer Midjourney's image quality.

**Can I use both tools together?**
I do. Midjourney for the hero images (main feed posts, thumbnails) where quality is all that matters. Leonardo AI for the supporting content (Stories, quick posts, variations of the same concept) where speed and volume matter more. The combined workflow is $10/month for Midjourney + $0 for Leonardo AI free tier.
