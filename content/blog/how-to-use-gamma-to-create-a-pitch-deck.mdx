## Before You Start: How Gamma Builds Decks

Gamma is not a slide editor in the traditional sense. It uses a card-based system where each "slide" is a flexible, scrollable card rather than a fixed-dimension slide. The practical implications:

- Cards adapt to content length (no cramming text into a fixed slide area)
- Web sharing works natively (send a link, not a file attachment)
- AI generation produces structured content, not just visual layouts

The trade-off is PowerPoint compatibility. If your investor specifically requests a .pptx file, Gamma's export loses some formatting. For web-based sharing (the norm in 2026), this is not an issue.

## Step 1: Write Your Prompt (2 minutes)

Gamma's output quality is determined by your prompt. Vague prompts produce generic decks. Specific prompts produce decks that match your company's voice.

**Bad prompt:**
```
Create a pitch deck for a startup
```

**Good prompt:**
```
Create a 10-slide pitch deck for an AI tutoring startup called StudyBot that helps K-12 students with homework using GPT-4. Include: problem (students struggle with homework help after school hours), solution (AI tutor available 24/7), market size ($12B online tutoring market), business model ($15/month subscription), traction (5,000 beta users, 40% monthly growth), team (2 ex-Google engineers), and ask ($2M seed round).
```

The good prompt produces a deck that is 70% content-ready. The bad prompt produces a deck that is 20% content-ready and requires rewriting every card.

**Prompt structure that works:**
1. Number of slides (8-12 is standard for pitch decks)
2. Company name and one-sentence description
3. Key sections to include (problem, solution, market, traction, team, ask)
4. Specific data points (numbers, percentages, dollar amounts)

Paste your prompt into Gamma's "Generate" button and select "Presentation" as the format.

## Step 2: Review and Edit the Outline (1 minute)

Gamma generates an outline before building the full deck. This is your chance to fix the structure before the AI commits to content.

**Check for these issues:**
- Missing sections (investors expect: Problem → Solution → Market → Traction → Team → Ask)
- Wrong order (Problem should come before Solution, always)
- Duplicate sections (Gamma sometimes creates two "About Us" cards)
- Unrealistic data (Gamma may invent market size numbers — replace with your real data)

Click on any outline item to rename, reorder, or delete it. This step takes 60 seconds but saves 15 minutes of post-generation editing.

## Step 3: Generate and Review the Deck (3 minutes)

Click "Generate" and Gamma builds the complete deck in 60-90 seconds. What you get:

- Full text content on every card
- AI-generated images matched to each section
- Consistent visual theme applied globally
- Card layouts optimized for content type (text-heavy vs. image-heavy)

**First pass review checklist:**
- Read every card's headline — does it tell a coherent story?
- Check data points — are your real numbers preserved, or did the AI invent new ones?
- Look at images — are any obviously wrong or generic stock photos?
- Check card length — any cards that are too long or too short?

At this stage, the deck is structurally complete but content-imperfect. Think of it as a rough draft that needs refinement, not a finished product.

## Step 4: Refine Content Card by Card (3 minutes)

The refinement pass is where you replace AI filler with your actual content. Go through each card and:

**For text content:**
- Replace AI-generated copy with your actual messaging
- Shorten paragraphs to 2-3 sentences maximum per card
- Add specific numbers and metrics (investors scan for data)
- Remove filler phrases and empty adjectives from every card

**For images:**
- Click any image and select "Generate new" to get a different AI image
- Or upload your own screenshots, product photos, or charts
- Product screenshots are more convincing than AI-generated illustrations for SaaS decks

**For layout:**
- If a card feels cluttered, split it into two cards
- If a card feels empty, add a supporting visual (chart, screenshot, team photo)
- Use the "Split card" button to break long content into digestible chunks

You are not aiming for perfection at this stage. A deck that tells your story clearly enough for a first meeting is sufficient — you can iterate after getting feedback.

## Step 5: Apply Your Brand (1 minute)

Gamma's default themes are clean but generic. For a pitch deck, you want at minimum:

1. **Change the color scheme** — Click "Restyle" and select colors that match your brand. If you do not have brand colors yet, pick one primary color and stick with it.

2. **Add your logo** — Upload your company logo and place it on the title card. Gamma does not have a global logo placement feature, so you add it manually to key cards.

3. **Adjust typography** — The default fonts are readable but not distinctive. Switch to a font that matches your brand personality (modern sans-serif for tech, serif for traditional industries).

On the Plus plan ($10/month) or above, you can save these settings as a custom theme for future decks. The free tier includes basic theme customization but applies Gamma branding to shared decks. Check [Gamma's official pricing](https://gamma.app/pricing) for the latest plan details and credit limits.

## Step 6: Export and Share (30 seconds)

Two options:

**Web link (recommended):**
Click "Share" → "Copy link" → Send to investors. The deck opens as a web page, tracks views, and works on any device. This is the fastest and most modern approach.

**PowerPoint export:**
Click "Export" → "Download as PPTX". Use this only if the investor specifically requests a file attachment. Expect some formatting simplification — card-based layouts do not map perfectly to fixed-dimension slides.

**PDF export:**
Click "Export" → "Download as PDF". Use this for email attachments or printing. PDF preserves the visual layout better than PPTX but loses interactivity.

## Common Mistakes to Avoid

**1. Trusting the AI too much**
Gamma generates plausible-sounding copy, but it does not know your business. Every data point, every claim, every metric needs to be your real data. The AI is a layout and structure tool, not a content authority.

**2. Too many cards**
A 20-card pitch deck is too long. Investors spend 3-5 minutes scanning a deck before deciding whether to take a meeting. Stick to 8-12 cards maximum. If you have more content, put it in an appendix deck that you share after the first meeting.

**3. Ignoring the outline step**
Skipping the outline review and going straight to generation wastes time. Fixing structural issues in the outline takes 60 seconds. Fixing them after generation takes 15 minutes.

**4. Using default images**
AI-generated images are fine for internal drafts, but investor-facing decks benefit from real product screenshots, actual team photos, and genuine customer logos. Replace at least the title card image and product card images with real visuals.

## When to Use Gamma vs. Beautiful.ai for Pitch Decks

Gamma is the right tool when:
- You need a draft fast (10 minutes vs. 30+ minutes manually)
- Your deck is primarily web-shared (link-based sharing)
- You are iterating quickly (multiple versions for different audiences)
- Budget matters (Gamma has a free tier)

[Beautiful.ai](/reviews/beautiful-ai) is the right tool when:
- Brand consistency is non-negotiable (enterprise brand enforcement)
- You need viewer analytics (track which slides investors spent time on)
- Multiple team members create decks (shared brand library)
- You have a budget for premium tooling ($12/month minimum)

Early-stage startups preparing a first deck should start with Gamma. Series A+ companies with locked brand guidelines will get more from Beautiful.ai.

## FAQ

**How long does it take to create a pitch deck with Gamma?**
10 minutes from first prompt to exportable deck, assuming you have your content (company description, metrics, team info) ready to paste in. The AI generates structure and layout in 60-90 seconds; the remaining time is spent refining content and images.

**Is Gamma good enough for investor pitch decks?**
Yes, for first-round meetings and initial outreach. The AI-generated structure covers all standard pitch deck sections. For later-stage fundraising (Series A+), you may want to invest more time in custom design or use a tool like [Beautiful.ai](/reviews/beautiful-ai) for brand enforcement.

**Can I use Gamma's free tier for pitch decks?**
Yes. The free tier includes 400 AI credits (roughly 10 decks) and full editing capabilities. The main limitation is Gamma branding on shared decks. For investor-facing work, the Plus plan ($10/month) removes branding and enables custom domains.

**How do I export my Gamma deck to PowerPoint?**
Click "Export" → "Download as PPTX". Some formatting simplifies during conversion — card-based layouts become fixed slides, and some interactive elements are lost. For best results, share the web link instead.

**What is the best prompt for generating a pitch deck?**
Include your company name, one-sentence description, number of slides (8-12), key sections (problem, solution, market, traction, team, ask), and specific data points. Specific prompts cut editing time in half compared to vague ones.
