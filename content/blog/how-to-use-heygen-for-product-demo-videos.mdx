## How HeyGen Creates Demo Videos

HeyGen turns a text script into a video of an AI avatar speaking. For product demos, this replaces the traditional workflow of hiring a presenter, booking a studio, and editing footage. The avatar reads your script with realistic lip-sync and facial expressions.

Three avatar options matter for demos:

- **Stock avatars** (Avatar III): 700+ pre-built presenters, included on all paid plans. Good enough for most demo videos.
- **Custom avatar** (digital twin): Record yourself for 30 seconds, get a photorealistic clone. Best for founder-led demos where personal brand matters.
- **Talking Photo**: Animate a still portrait. Lower quality, but fast for social media teasers.

For product demo videos, stock avatars work for 80% of use cases. Custom avatars are worth the setup time only if the presenter's identity is part of your brand (founder demos, CEO announcements).

## Before You Start: Prepare Your Script

HeyGen's output quality depends entirely on your script. The AI delivers whatever you write with consistent tone and pacing. A poorly structured script produces a poorly structured video, regardless of how good the avatar looks.

**Script structure that works for product demos:**

1. **Hook (15 seconds)**: State the problem your product solves. One sentence, no preamble.
2. **Feature walkthrough (90-120 seconds)**: Show 3 features maximum. Each feature gets a problem → solution → result structure.
3. **Social proof (15 seconds)**: One metric or customer quote.
4. **CTA (15 seconds)**: One clear next step (sign up, book a call, watch full demo).

Total target: 2-3 minutes. Longer demos lose viewers. If you have more to show, create a series of short demos rather than one long video.

Write your script in plain text, no formatting. HeyGen's editor accepts plain text and applies its own pacing. Avoid:

- Abbreviations the avatar might mispronounce (write "S A S" not "SaaS" if pronunciation matters)
- Complex numbers (write "two thousand" not "2,000" for natural delivery)
- Jargon your audience might not know

## Step 1: Choose Your Avatar (2 minutes)

Open HeyGen and select "Create Video." Choose between:

**For generic product demos**: Browse the stock avatar library. Filter by gender, age, ethnicity, and attire. For B2B SaaS demos, professional attire and neutral backgrounds perform best. Preview 3-4 avatars reading a sample sentence from your script before committing.

**For founder-led demos**: Upload your selfie video (30 seconds to 2 minutes of you talking to camera). HeyGen creates a custom avatar in 10-15 minutes. The quality depends on your source footage: good lighting, plain background, and steady camera produce the best results.

We tested both approaches. Stock avatars produce consistent, professional output every time. Custom avatars are more engaging when the audience knows the presenter, but quality varies with source footage quality.

## Step 2: Enter Your Script (3 minutes)

Paste your prepared script into HeyGen's editor. The editor displays a word count and estimated video duration. For a 3-minute demo, aim for 400-450 words.

**Settings that matter:**

- **Voice**: Each avatar has a default voice. You can swap voices from HeyGen's library of 300+ options. For demo videos, pick a voice that matches your brand tone (authoritative for enterprise, conversational for startups).
- **Speed**: Default is 1.0x. For demo videos, 0.95x sounds more natural. Faster speeds (1.1x+) sound rushed.
- **Pauses**: Add manual pauses between sections by inserting break markers. A 1-second pause between the hook and the feature walkthrough gives viewers time to process.

Generate a 30-second preview before committing to the full video. Listen for pronunciation issues and adjust the script text if needed (spell words phonetically if the avatar mispronounces them).

## Step 3: Add Visual Elements (5 minutes)

A talking-head-only demo video is boring. HeyGen lets you add:

- **Screen recordings**: Upload your product screenshots or screen recordings. The avatar appears in a picture-in-picture overlay while your product fills the screen.
- **Text overlays**: Add key metrics, feature names, or pricing information as text that appears alongside the avatar.
- **Background images**: Replace the default background with your product's UI screenshots or branded backgrounds.
- **Brand kit**: Upload your logo, colors, and fonts (available on Creator plan and above).

For product demos, the most effective layout is screen recording with avatar overlay. The viewer sees your product in action while the avatar explains what is happening. Record your screen using HeyGen's built-in screen recorder or upload existing footage from [OBS](https://obsproject.com/) or [Loom](https://www.loom.com/).

## Step 4: Generate and Review (5 minutes)

Click "Generate" and HeyGen renders the full video in 5-10 minutes depending on length. Review the output for:

- **Lip-sync accuracy**: Does the mouth movement match the words? Western European languages render well; some Asian languages show occasional misalignment.
- **Pacing**: Does the avatar speak too fast or too slow for your content?
- **Visual alignment**: Do text overlays and screen recordings appear at the right time?
- **Background**: Is the background appropriate for your brand?

If anything is wrong, edit the script or settings and regenerate. HeyGen charges credits per generation, so preview short sections before committing to the full video.

## Step 5: Export and Embed (2 minutes)

Export options:

- **MP4 download**: Standard video file, upload to YouTube, Vimeo, or your website.
- **Embed link**: HeyGen provides an embed code you can paste directly into your product page. The video streams from HeyGen's CDN.
- **Share link**: A direct URL you can send to prospects or include in email campaigns.

For product pages, the embed option is best because HeyGen tracks views and engagement. For email campaigns, use the share link. For social media, download the MP4 and upload natively (platforms prefer native video over embedded players).

## Localization for Global Audiences

HeyGen's killer feature for product demos is face-driven localization. Record your script in English, then use the translation feature to generate versions in 175+ languages with accurate lip-sync. The avatar's mouth movements adjust to match each language's phonetics.

For SaaS companies selling globally, this means one demo video becomes 10 language versions in under an hour. The cost is credits per minute of output, but this is dramatically cheaper than hiring presenters for each language.

Test your target language with a short clip before committing to a full production run. Lip-sync quality varies by language, and some require script adjustments for natural delivery.

## Common Mistakes

**1. Script too long**
A 5-minute demo video loses 60% of viewers. Keep it to 2-3 minutes. If you have more features to show, create a series of short, focused demos.

**2. No screen recording**
A talking head without product visuals is not a demo, it is a monologue. Always pair the avatar with screen recordings or product screenshots.

**3. Skipping the preview**
Generating a full 3-minute video costs credits. Always preview a 30-second section first to catch pronunciation and pacing issues.

**4. Generic stock avatar for founder content**
If the demo is supposed to come from the founder, use a custom avatar. Stock avatars feel impersonal for leadership-driven content.
