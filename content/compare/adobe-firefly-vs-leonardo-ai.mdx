## Safety vs. Production

Firefly is the legally safe image generator. Leonardo is the production-ready image generator.

If you are a game studio, concept artist, or marketing team producing assets at scale, Leonardo's 50+ fine-tuned models, AI Canvas with real-time editing, and 3D texture generation (albedo, normal, roughness maps) directly replace hours of manual work. But Leonardo offers no IP indemnification.

If you are an enterprise brand or agency where copyright exposure is a genuine concern, Firefly's IP indemnification and Creative Cloud integration are more valuable than Leonardo's production tools.

## Production Features

Leonardo's 3D texture generation and game-ready asset pipeline have no equivalent in Firefly. AI Canvas (infinite workspace, inpainting, outpainting, real-time prompt editing) offers production control Firefly cannot match.

Firefly's Generative Fill in Photoshop is excellent for image editing and extension, but it is not a production pipeline — it is an editing feature within an existing tool.

**Winner: Leonardo AI** for production workflows.
