## Developer vs Enterprise

[D-ID](https://www.d-id.com/) started as an API company. Its core product is making still photos talk — upload a face image, provide text or audio, get a talking-head video. The technology powers chatbots with faces, personalized video messages, and interactive kiosks. Developers embed D-ID into their own applications.

[Synthesia](https://www.synthesia.io?via=jie) started as a training video company. Its core product is a studio where you build multi-scene training videos with professional AI presenters. Enterprise features — team workspaces, brand governance, LMS integration — are built in from the ground up.

## Ease of Avatar Creation

D-ID requires one photo. Upload a headshot, type text, get a talking video. This simplicity is the product: no studio recording, no approval process, no training data requirements.

Synthesia requires either selecting from 150+ stock avatars or creating a custom avatar through a studio recording process. The result is more polished but the setup is heavier.

**Winner: D-ID** for quick setup. **Synthesia** for polished output.

## Enterprise Features

Synthesia's LMS integration (SCORM/xAPI), 140-language support, team workspaces, and brand kit enforcement are built for large organizations. D-ID offers none of these.

D-ID's API, webhook system, and embeddable player are built for developers integrating talking heads into their own products. Synthesia's API is secondary to its studio product.

**Winner: Synthesia** for enterprise L&D. **D-ID** for developer embedding.

D-ID's usage-based model suits developers who embed occasional video generation into applications — pay only for what you generate. Synthesia's subscription model suits organizations producing training videos regularly. For low-volume API use, D-ID is cheaper. For regular video production, Synthesia's predictable monthly cost is easier to budget.

## Who Should Choose D-ID

- Developers embedding talking-head video into apps or chatbots
- Companies needing lightweight, API-first avatar generation
- Projects where quick photo-to-video simplicity matters more than polish

## Who Should Choose Synthesia

- Corporate L&D teams deploying training at scale
- Multinational companies needing 140+ language support
- Organizations requiring LMS integration and enterprise governance

## FAQ

**Which is cheaper?** D-ID's usage-based model can be cheaper for low-volume API use. Synthesia's $22/month is better for regular video production.

**Can D-ID do multi-scene training videos?** Not well. D-ID is optimized for single talking-head clips. Synthesia is built for multi-scene course creation.
