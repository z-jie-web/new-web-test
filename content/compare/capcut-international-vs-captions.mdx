## Full Editor vs. Caption Specialist

[CapCut](https://www.capcut.com/) is a complete video editor with AI features. Multi-track timeline, effects, transitions, templates, and the TikTok ecosystem's trending formats. [Captions](https://www.captions.ai/) is a caption-first editor, the best tool for adding animated, word-by-word captions to social videos.

## FAQ

**Which is better for TikTok creators?** CapCut, it's owned by the same company as TikTok and integrates trending formats directly. Captions is better for Instagram Reels and YouTube Shorts where caption styling matters more.

**Can Captions do full video editing?** Captions is a caption-first editor. It has basic editing tools but can't replace a full editor like CapCut for multi-track projects.

**Are AI subtitles accurate?** Both tools have high accuracy for English. CapCut supports more languages. Always review AI-generated captions before publishing.

**Do free tiers leave watermarks?** CapCut's free tier is generous. Captions' free tier has limitations. Check each tool's current terms.
