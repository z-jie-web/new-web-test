## Generation vs. Enhancement

This is the clearest distinction in the AI video space. Runway creates. Topaz improves. They are not competitors — they are complementary tools in a professional video pipeline.

Runway Gen-3 generates new video from text descriptions. It creates footage that didn't exist before. The output can be creative and cinematic but may have AI artifacts, inconsistent textures, and temporal instability.

Topaz Video AI takes existing video — whether shot on a camera or generated by AI — and enhances it: upscaling 1080p to 4K/8K, removing noise, smoothing motion, increasing frame rate. It doesn't create anything new; it makes what exists look better.

## The Combined Workflow

The best AI video results come from combining both tools:

1. Generate raw clips in Runway Gen-3
2. Enhance and upscale the output in Topaz Video AI
3. Edit the polished footage in a traditional editor

This pipeline produces results that neither tool can achieve alone.

## FAQ

**Are they competitors?** No. They solve opposite problems: Runway creates, Topaz improves. Many professionals use both.

**Which should I buy first?** If you have no footage, Runway. If you have footage that needs improvement, Topaz. If you do both, eventually you will want both.
