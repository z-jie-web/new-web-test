## Easy vs. Capable

[DALL-E 3](https://openai.com/dall-e-3) removes the barrier between you and an image. No prompt syntax, no model selection, no parameter tuning. You describe what you want in plain English, ChatGPT translates it into an optimized prompt, and you refine through conversation. This is the most accessible AI image experience available.

[Leonardo AI](https://leonardo.ai) expects you to know what you want and provides the tools to execute it. 50+ models for different aesthetics, AI Canvas for precise editing, 3D texture generation for game assets. More capable but more demanding.

## Prompting Experience

DALL-E 3 via ChatGPT is a conversation. "Generate a product photo of a coffee mug on a wooden table, warm morning light." Then: "Make the mug blue." Then: "Change the background to a kitchen." Each refinement builds on the last.

Leonardo requires prompt crafting and model selection. The result can be more precise — you control every parameter — but the learning curve is real.

**Winner: DALL-E 3** for ease of use.

For image-only use, Leonardo at $12/month offers more value. For occasional image needs combined with AI chat, ChatGPT Plus at $20/month is the better bundle.

## Who Should Choose DALL-E 3

- Beginners who want to describe images in English, not prompt syntax
- ChatGPT Plus subscribers (image generation included)
- Anyone who occasionally needs quick, good-quality images
- Developers using the OpenAI API for image generation

## Who Should Choose Leonardo AI

- Game developers needing 3D textures and production assets
- Concept artists who need fine-grained creative control
- Anyone who wants to seriously test AI image generation with a generous free tier
- Production teams needing consistent output across 50+ specialized models

## FAQ

**Which is better for beginners?** DALL-E 3 — conversational prompting is the most forgiving learning experience.

**Which is better for professionals?** Leonardo AI — the production tools (AI Canvas, specialized models, 3D textures) address real professional workflows.
