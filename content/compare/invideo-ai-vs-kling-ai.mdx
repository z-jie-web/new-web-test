## Complete Video vs. Quality Footage

[InVideo AI](https://invideo.io/ai/) is a video factory: input a topic, get a finished video with script, scenes, voiceover, and music. The output is functional and publishable. [Kling AI](https://klingai.com/) is a footage generator: input a prompt, get a high-quality clip with realistic physics and motion. The output is visually impressive but needs assembly into a finished video.

## Pricing

|  | InVideo AI | Kling AI |
|--|-----------|----------|
| Free | Yes (limited exports) | Limited credits |
| Entry | $20/month | Credits-based |
| Mid-tier | $60/month (Business) | Volume credits |

InVideo's $20/month is better value for most users, it includes the entire production pipeline. Kling is better for creators who already have editing workflow and just need the best possible source footage.

## Who Should Choose InVideo AI

- Anyone who needs finished videos without editing skills
- Marketers producing video content at volume
- Internal communications and training videos

## Who Should Choose Kling AI

- Video editors who want the highest quality AI-generated source clips
- Projects where motion realism is the primary concern
- Creators who have established editing workflows

## FAQ

**Which is cheaper?** InVideo for end-to-end video. Kling for raw clip generation volume.

**Can I use both?** Yes. Use Kling to generate high-quality clips, import into InVideo or another editor for assembly.
