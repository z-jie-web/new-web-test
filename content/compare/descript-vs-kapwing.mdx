## Words vs. Timeline

Descript's transcript-based editing is a fundamentally different paradigm. Edit the words, and the audio/video follows. This changes how you work with speech-heavy content.

Kapwing uses a traditional timeline editor. Familiar to anyone who has used iMovie or Premiere. The collaboration features are the differentiator — shared workspaces, multi-user editing, version history.

Kapwing is cheaper. But Descript's Overdub (voice cloning for mistake repair) can save hours of re-recording — value that exceeds the price difference for podcasters.

## Who Should Choose Descript

- Podcasters and long-form content creators
- Anyone who edits speech-heavy content
- Creators who frequently fix spoken mistakes

## Who Should Choose Kapwing

- Teams collaborating on video with review workflows
- Short-form video creators comfortable with timeline editing
- Anyone who values price and collaboration over audio-editing innovation

## FAQ

**Which is better for short social videos?** Kapwing — timeline editing and team collaboration suit social content workflows better.

**Which is better for podcasts?** Descript — transcript-based editing and Overdub are built for audio-first content.
