## Mobile vs. Desktop

[Captions](https://www.captions.ai/) is built mobile-first. The app is optimized for shooting and editing short-form video on your phone — TikTok, Reels, Shorts. AI-powered eye contact correction makes selfie videos look more professional. Animated captions render word-by-word for maximum engagement.

[VEED](https://www.veed.io/) is built browser-first with a full timeline editor. More editing capabilities — trimming, transitions, overlays, multiple tracks — but requires a desktop workflow. Better for creators who edit on a computer and need more than caption styling.
