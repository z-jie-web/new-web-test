## Editor vs. Transcription

VEED makes videos look good on social media. Auto-captions with deep styling — fonts, colors, animations, emoji, word-by-word rendering. Full timeline editor for trimming, transitions, and overlays. Output is a finished video for TikTok, Reels, or YouTube.

Happy Scribe makes audio into text. Accurate transcription in 120+ languages. Clean subtitle exports in any format. Output is a transcript or subtitle file — you handle the video editing elsewhere.

## Who Should Choose VEED

- Social media creators making styled, captioned videos
- Teams collaborating on video with review workflows
- Anyone who needs video editing + captioning in one browser tool

## Who Should Choose Happy Scribe

- Journalists and researchers needing accurate transcripts
- Teams subtitling content in many languages
- Anyone who needs transcription only

## FAQ

**Which has better captions?** VEED for visual styling. Happy Scribe for accuracy and language breadth.

**Can I use both?** Yes. Transcribe in Happy Scribe, import the SRT into VEED for styling.
