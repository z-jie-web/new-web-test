## Real-time vs. Open-source

[Swapface](https://swapface.org) is built for live video — your face appears on stream instantly. [FaceFusion](https://github.com/facefusion/facefusion) is built for developers who want to own their face swap pipeline. Swapface optimizes for speed; FaceFusion optimizes for flexibility.

**Winner: Depends on use case.** Live streaming needs Swapface. Customization and privacy need FaceFusion.

## Quality Performance

Swapface delivers good quality at real-time speeds — impressive for live video but not pixel-perfect. FaceFusion can produce higher quality with proper configuration and enough processing time, but isn't optimized for real-time use.

**Winner: FaceFusion** for maximum quality. Swapface for real-time capability.

## FAQ

**Which has better quality?** FaceFusion can achieve higher quality with proper configuration. Swapface trades some quality for real-time speed.

**Are face swap tools safe to use?** Both process locally — your images never leave your machine. FaceFusion's open-source code adds an extra layer of trust.

**Do free tiers leave watermarks?** Swapface's free tier may include watermarks. FaceFusion never watermarks.

**Can FaceFusion do real-time video?** FaceFusion has limited real-time capability. Swapface is purpose-built for it.
