**Winner: Both.** These tools complement each other perfectly. Use Luma for generation, Topaz for enhancement.

## Quality Performance

Luma produces creative, visually appealing clips quickly — good enough for social media and most web content. Topaz elevates those clips to professional quality with upscaling, denoising, and frame interpolation.

**Winner: Luma** for speed and creativity. Topaz for technical quality enhancement.

## FAQ

**Can Luma upscale video?** No — Luma generates new video, it doesn't enhance existing footage. For upscaling, you need Topaz or a similar enhancement tool.

**Which is better for beginners?** Luma — it's cheaper and simpler. Generate clips in seconds with no technical knowledge needed.

**Are AI video tools safe to use?** Luma processes in the cloud. Topaz runs locally. Read each tool's privacy policy.

**Is the price gap as big as it looks?** Luma at $9.99/month costs $120/year. Topaz at $299 one-time costs $299 upfront but nothing ongoing. Over 3 years, Topaz is cheaper.
