## Two Mobile Entertainment Apps

Both do the same thing, put your face into videos and GIFs for social sharing, with different strengths. [FaceMagic](https://facemagic.ai) offers more creative effect variety. [Reface](https://reface.ai) has a larger library of famous movie and music video templates.

**Winner: Depends on preference.** FaceMagic for creative variety. Reface for recognizable templates and bigger library.

## Content Library Comparison

Reface's template library is one of the largest in the mobile face swap space, with famous movie scenes, music videos, and viral templates updated regularly. FaceMagic has a solid library with more creative and artistic effects, but fewer recognizable celebrity templates.

**Winner: Reface** for library size and recognizable content. FaceMagic for creative diversity.
