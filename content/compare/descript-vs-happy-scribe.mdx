## Editor vs. Transcription Service

[Descript](https://www.descript.com/) is an editor that happens to use transcription as its interface. Edit the transcript and the audio/video follows. Overdub lets you fix spoken mistakes by typing corrections, no re-recording needed.

[Happy Scribe](https://www.happyscribe.com/) is a transcription service. Upload audio/video, get accurate text back in minutes. Export subtitles in any format. For "I need this podcast transcribed accurately in French," Happy Scribe is the tool.

**If you edit audio/video and transcription is a means to that end: Descript. If transcription is the end goal: Happy Scribe.**

## Who Should Choose Descript

- Podcasters and video essayists who edit by working with words
- Creators who frequently need to fix spoken mistakes without re-recording
- Anyone who wants transcription and editing in one tool

## Who Should Choose Happy Scribe

- Journalists and researchers who need accurate transcripts
- Teams subtitling content in 120+ languages
- Anyone who needs transcription only, not an editor

## FAQ

**Which has better transcription accuracy?** Happy Scribe, transcription is its core product, not a feature. Especially for multi-language content.

**Can Descript replace Happy Scribe?** For English podcast editing: yes. For multi-language transcription: no.
