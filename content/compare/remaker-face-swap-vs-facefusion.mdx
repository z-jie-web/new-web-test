## Cloud vs. Local

[Remaker](https://remaker.ai) is instant and easy but processes images on its servers. [FaceFusion](https://github.com/facefusion/facefusion) runs locally — full privacy, no usage limits, but requires setup and a GPU. The tradeoff is convenience versus control.

**Winner: Depends on priority.** Remaker for speed and ease. FaceFusion for privacy and unlimited use.

## Quality Performance

Remaker delivers good-quality swaps with fast processing — ideal for casual and bulk use. FaceFusion's quality scales with your hardware and configuration; with a powerful GPU, it can produce excellent results with full control over parameters.

**Winner: FaceFusion** for maximum quality potential. Remaker for fast, consistent results.
