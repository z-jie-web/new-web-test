## Developer vs. Consumer

[FaceFusion](https://github.com/facefusion/facefusion) is for builders — clone the repo, configure the pipeline, integrate into your own workflows. [Reface](https://reface.ai) is for users — download, pick a template, share with friends. Different audiences, different tools.

**Winner: No contest.** These serve completely different users. If you're a developer, FaceFusion. If you want fun on your phone, Reface.

## Quality Performance

FaceFusion's output quality depends on your hardware and configuration. With a powerful GPU and proper setup, results can be excellent. Reface delivers consistent, template-optimized quality designed for social sharing — not photorealism, but reliably entertaining.

**Winner: FaceFusion** for maximum quality potential. Reface for consistent, share-ready output.
