## Image vs. Video Entertainment

[Remaker](https://remaker.ai) does image face swaps quickly and simply — upload two photos, get a swapped result. [FaceMagic](https://facemagic.ai) puts your face into videos, GIFs, and templates for social sharing. Different mediums, different use cases.

**Winner: Depends on medium.** For images, Remaker. For video and GIFs, FaceMagic.

## Quality Performance

Remaker delivers good-quality image swaps with fast turnaround. FaceMagic optimizes for video and animation — quality is template-driven and designed for social sharing rather than pixel-level realism.

**Winner: Remaker** for still image quality. FaceMagic for video entertainment.
