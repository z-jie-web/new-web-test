## Short-Form vs. Long-Form

[Captions](https://www.captions.ai/) is purpose-built for TikTok, Reels, and Shorts. Auto-captions with stylish animated word-by-word rendering. Eye contact correction for selfie videos. Templates designed for vertical formats and social engagement patterns.

[Descript](https://www.descript.com/) is purpose-built for podcasts, interviews, and video essays. Edit the transcript, delete words, rearrange sentences, remove filler, and the audio/video follows. This model is transformative for content where spoken word is the backbone.

**The length of your typical content determines which tool fits.**

## AI Features

Descript's Overdub voice cloning solves a specific problem: fixing spoken mistakes without re-recording. Train it on your voice, type corrections into the transcript, and Overdub generates the fix in your voice.

Captions' AI focuses on visual polish: auto-captions with word-by-word animation, eye contact correction for selfie videos, AI-powered editing suggestions for social formats.

**Winner: Descript** for audio repair. **Captions** for visual polish.

## Who Should Choose Captions

- Social media creators making short-form vertical video
- Anyone who needs polished, animated captions
- Selfie-video creators who want eye contact correction
- Mobile-first creators

## Who Should Choose Descript

- Podcasters and audio-first content creators
- Video essayists who build content around narration
- Anyone who edits by working with words, not waveforms
- Creators who frequently need to fix spoken mistakes

## FAQ

**Can Descript do animated captions like Captions?** Descript has caption features but Captions' animated word-by-word styling is superior for social content.

**Can Captions fix audio mistakes like Descript?** No. Overdub-style voice cloning for mistake correction is unique to Descript.
