## Complete vs. Component

[InVideo](https://invideo.io/ai/) produces a finished video: script, voiceover, music, and editing are all handled. Input a topic, output a publishable video. [Luma](https://lumalabs.ai/dream-machine) produces creative raw material: fast, good-looking clips that need assembly into a finished piece.

**Winner: Depends on your workflow.** InVideo if you need a finished video. Luma if you're an editor who wants source clips.

## Quality Performance

InVideo optimizes for coherent, publish-ready output — the AI handles structure, pacing, and narrative. Luma optimizes for visual creativity — each clip is a standalone piece with unique style and motion. InVideo is about completion; Luma is about creative possibility.

**Winner: InVideo** for finished output. Luma for creative clip quality.

## FAQ

**Which is faster?** Luma generates clips in seconds. InVideo takes minutes to produce a complete video with voiceover, music, and editing.

**Can I combine both?** Yes — generate raw clips in Luma, then assemble them with InVideo's editing tools for a finished video.

**Are AI video tools safe to use?** Both are reputable cloud-based services. Read each tool's privacy policy before uploading sensitive content.

**Do free tiers leave watermarks?** Check each tool's current terms. Most AI video tools include watermarks on free tier output.
