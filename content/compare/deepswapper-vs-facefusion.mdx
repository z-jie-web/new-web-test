## Cloud vs. Local

[DeepSwapper](https://deepswapper.com) is a polished web service: upload images, get results, no setup required. [FaceFusion](https://github.com/facefusion/facefusion) is open-source software you run on your own hardware: full privacy, unlimited customization, no usage limits. The tradeoff is convenience versus control.

**Winner: Depends on your needs.** DeepSwapper for instant results. FaceFusion for privacy and customization.

## Quality Performance

DeepSwapper produces more realistic swaps with better skin texture matching and lighting blending, the output looks more natural but takes longer to process. FaceFusion's quality depends on your hardware and configuration; it can match or exceed cloud services when properly set up, but requires more effort.

**Winner: DeepSwapper** for out-of-the-box quality. FaceFusion can match it with the right setup.

## FAQ

**Which tool produces more realistic swaps?** DeepSwapper generally produces better out-of-the-box results. FaceFusion can achieve comparable quality with proper configuration and a powerful GPU.

**Are face swap tools safe to use?** Reputable tools process images securely. DeepSwapper uploads images to its servers. FaceFusion keeps everything local, the safest option for sensitive photos. Read each tool's privacy policy before uploading.

**Do free tiers leave watermarks?** FaceFusion has no watermarks, it's fully open-source. DeepSwapper's free tier has usage limits. Check DeepSwapper's current terms for details.

**Do I need a powerful computer for FaceFusion?** Yes. FaceFusion requires a decent GPU for reasonable processing speed. DeepSwapper runs in the cloud, so any device with a browser works.
