## Complete vs. Component

[InVideo AI](https://invideo.io/ai/) is the "make me a video" button. Script, scenes, voiceover, music, text overlays, generated and assembled automatically. The result is functional and publishable, if not award-winning.

[Runway Gen-3](https://runwayml.com/) is the "generate the perfect shot" tool. Each clip is a component in a larger production. Quality is high enough for professional use. But you need to handle editing, voiceover, and assembly yourself.

**Winner: InVideo** for end-to-end speed. **Runway** for clip quality.

Runway at $15/month gets you high-quality clips, but you still need separate tools for voiceover, music, and editing. For professionals who already have those tools and just need the best raw footage, Runway is worth it.

## Quality Expectations

InVideo's output is functional, clean, watchable, and publishable, but not cinematic. The AI assembles stock footage, generated visuals, text overlays, and voiceover into a coherent video. It looks like a well-made template, not a custom production.

Runway's output is cinematic, each clip can be visually stunning with proper lighting, composition, and camera movement. But each clip is a raw component. The finished video's quality depends entirely on your editing skill.

## Who Should Choose InVideo AI

- Anyone who needs a publishable video without editing skills
- Marketers producing high-volume social ad variants
- Internal communications and training content

## Who Should Choose Runway Gen-3

- Professional editors and filmmakers
- Creators who prioritize clip quality over end-to-end automation
- Projects where visual polish is non-negotiable

## FAQ

**Which is cheaper?** InVideo at $20/month for complete video production is better value for most users. Runway at $15/month is better for professionals who need the clip quality.
