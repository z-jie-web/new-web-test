## Create vs. Improve

[Pika](https://pika.art/) creates footage that didn't exist. [Topaz](https://www.topazlabs.com/topaz-video-ai) makes existing footage look better. They are complementary, not competitive.

**Workflow:** Generate creative clips in Pika → improve and upscale in Topaz → edit in your preferred editor.

## FAQ

**Can Pika improve existing video?** No, Pika generates new video from text prompts. For improvement, you need Topaz or a similar tool.

**Which is better for beginners?** Pika, it's cheaper ($10/month) and simpler. Type a prompt, get a clip. Topaz requires existing footage and technical knowledge.

**Are AI video tools safe to use?** Pika processes in the cloud. Topaz runs locally. Read each tool's privacy policy.

**Is the workflow realistic?** Yes, generate in Pika, improve in Topaz is a practical pipeline used by creators who want both creative generation and professional output quality.
