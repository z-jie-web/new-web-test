## Solo vs. Team

VEED and Kapwing are the two leading browser-based video editors — and their core difference is who they are built for.

VEED is optimized for solo creators. The caption styling engine is the best in the browser category. The interface is streamlined for one person making social videos efficiently.

Kapwing is optimized for teams. Shared workspaces, multi-user editing, comment threads on specific timestamps, version history. If your video goes through review cycles with a manager, a brand team, and a legal reviewer, Kapwing's collaboration features are the differentiator.
