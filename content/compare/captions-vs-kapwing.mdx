## Mobile vs. Team

[Captions](https://www.captions.ai/) is for solo creators making short-form social content on their phones. [Kapwing](https://www.kapwing.com/) is for teams making videos together in a browser.

## FAQ

**Which is better for solo creators?** Captions — it's built for solo creators making social content. Kapwing's team features add complexity you don't need when working alone.

**Can Kapwing do animated captions?** Yes, Kapwing has caption tools, but Captions is the specialist with more animation styles and better mobile-optimized caption workflows.

**Are AI subtitles accurate?** Both have good accuracy for English. Always review AI-generated captions before publishing.

**Which free tier is better?** Captions' free tier is more generous. Kapwing's free tier adds a watermark to exports. For free use, Captions is the better choice.

**Can I switch between them?** Yes — they export standard video formats. You can create in Captions and bring into Kapwing for team review, or vice versa.
