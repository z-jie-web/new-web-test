## Solo vs. Team

CapCut is the best solo creator tool — powerful editing, AI effects, offline access, massive template library from the TikTok ecosystem.

Kapwing is built for teams — shared workspaces, comments, version history. If multiple people touch your video before publishing, Kapwing's collaboration features are the differentiator.
