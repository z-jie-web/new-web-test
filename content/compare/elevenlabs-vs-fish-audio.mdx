## Voice Quality: Benchmark vs Real-World

On standardized benchmarks, Fish Audio's S2-Pro model ranks #1 on TTS-Arena — ahead of ElevenLabs, OpenAI, and Google. That's a data point, not the whole story.

In our own A/B testing with 20 English passages, the results were nuanced. ElevenLabs' Eleven v3 model sounds slightly more natural on conversational narration — the kind of voice you'd want for a fiction audiobook or a casual YouTube explainer. Fish Audio handles technical terminology, multi-speaker dialogue, and non-English languages better. If you're generating Mandarin, Japanese, or Arabic voice content, Fish Audio's cross-lingual cloning is in a different league.

The practical gap between them has narrowed significantly. In 2025, ElevenLabs was the clear winner on quality. In 2026, the difference is marginal enough that price and flexibility become the deciding factors for most use cases.

**Winner: ElevenLabs** by a hair on English expressiveness. **Fish Audio** on multilingual and technical content.

## Developer Experience and Flexibility

ElevenLabs has the more polished developer experience. Better documentation, cleaner API design, more predictable pricing (even if the credit system takes getting used to). The platform handles 70+ languages with automatic detection, offers frame-accurate dubbing in 29 languages, and the Conversational AI SDK makes building voice agents straightforward.

Fish Audio counters with something ElevenLabs can't match: open-source model weights. You can download S2-Mini (400M params, runs on 12GB RTX 3060) or S2-Pro (4B params, needs 24GB) and run them entirely offline. No API calls, no credit counting, no vendor lock-in. For a startup building voice features into their product, this is the difference between a variable cost that scales with every user and a fixed infrastructure cost.

The emotion control system is Fish Audio's secret weapon. Typing `[whispering conspiratorially]` or `[angry, raising voice]` directly in your script is more intuitive than ElevenLabs' SSML-based approach. 15,000+ supported tags at 93.3% activation accuracy. Once you've directed a voice performance this way, going back to sliders and markup feels archaic.

Fish Audio's multi-speaker dialogue generation — 3 characters in a single API call, no stitching — is a feature ElevenLabs doesn't have a direct answer for. For audiobook chapters with dialogue, game NPC conversations, and podcast intros, this saves real production time.

**Winner: Fish Audio** on flexibility, control, and developer independence. **ElevenLabs** on polish and documentation.

If you have the GPU and the technical skills, the self-hosting math is compelling — the hardware pays for itself within months at any serious production volume.

## FAQ

**Is Fish Audio really better than ElevenLabs?**
On TTS-Arena benchmarks, yes — S2-Pro ranks #1. In everyday use, it depends on what you're doing. [ElevenLabs](/reviews/elevenlabs) still edges ahead on casual English narration and product polish. [Fish Audio](/reviews/fish-audio) wins on cost, emotion control, multi-speaker generation, and multilingual output. The gap is small enough that price and flexibility should tip the decision for most teams.

**Can I switch from ElevenLabs to Fish Audio easily?**
API migration is straightforward if you've abstracted your TTS calls behind a service layer — both have REST APIs with similar text-in, audio-out patterns. Voice cloning requires re-recording reference samples. The bigger cost is retraining your team on Fish Audio's emotion tag system (which is more powerful but different from ElevenLabs' approach).

**Which has better voice cloning?**
Fish Audio is faster (10 seconds vs 30 seconds minimum for instant cloning). ElevenLabs' Professional Voice Cloning (30-minute sample) produces near-indistinguishable results with breath patterns and emotional range — this is the tier audiobook publishers use. For most use cases, Fish Audio's cloning is good enough and significantly faster.

**What about Play.ht and Murf AI?**
[Play.ht](/reviews/play-ht) matches roughly on English voice quality with simpler per-word pricing, but trails on cloning and multilingual support. [Murf AI](/reviews/murf-ai) is better for presentation-style content with built-in video sync — it's a different use case. For raw TTS quality and API flexibility, ElevenLabs and Fish Audio are the top two in 2026.
