## Platform vs. Ecosystem

[Leonardo AI](https://leonardo.ai) is what you get when someone builds a great product on top of [Stable Diffusion](https://stability.ai/)'s technology. 50+ fine-tuned models curated for specific use cases. AI Canvas with polished inpainting and outpainting. 3D texture generation with one click. A free tier generous enough for real evaluation.

Stable Diffusion is what you get when you take the raw technology and build your own workflow. Thousands of models on Civitai ranging from stunning to unusable. ControlNet for pixel-perfect spatial control. The ability to train custom LoRAs on your specific characters or products. No platform rules, no content filters, no monthly fees — just you and the model.

## Workflow

Leonardo AI is ready in 2 minutes. Sign up, pick a model, start generating. AI Canvas, model switching, and 3D texture export are all accessible through a clean web interface.

Stable Diffusion requires hours of setup (Python, CUDA, model download, front-end selection) and weeks to master (prompt syntax, negative prompts, CFG scale, sampling methods, LoRA management). The learning curve is steep but the ceiling is unlimited.

**Winner: Leonardo AI** for immediate productivity. **Stable Diffusion** for maximum capability ceiling.

## Pricing

|  | Leonardo AI | Stable Diffusion |
|--|------------|-----------------|
| Free | 150 tokens/day (no credit card) | Unlimited (self-hosted) |
| Entry | $12/month (3,600 tokens) | Free (own hardware) |
| Mid-tier | $24/month (10,000 tokens) | ~$10/month (cloud GPU hosting) |
| Pro | $48/month (25,000 tokens) | ~$30-50/month (dedicated cloud GPU) |

Leonardo's free tier is the best for zero-risk evaluation. For moderate production use, Leonardo at $12-24/month provides a polished experience without hardware costs. Stable Diffusion's cost advantage emerges at high volume: once you own a GPU, the marginal cost per image approaches zero. Cloud GPU hosting bridges the gap for those without hardware.

## Who Should Choose Leonardo AI

- Production artists and game developers who want tools, not infrastructure
- Creators who value a polished workflow over unlimited customization
- Anyone who wants to start generating in minutes, not hours

## Who Should Choose Stable Diffusion

- Developers and researchers who need model-level access
- Privacy-focused users who need fully local generation
- Power users willing to invest time for unlimited capability
- Anyone generating content outside commercial platform policies

## FAQ

**Is Leonardo based on Stable Diffusion?** Leonardo's earlier models were fine-tuned from Stable Diffusion. Their Phoenix model is built from scratch. The platform abstracts the underlying technology.

**Which is truly free?** Both offer generous free options. Stable Diffusion is permanently free on your own hardware. Leonardo's free tier (150 tokens/day) is usage-limited but requires no setup.
