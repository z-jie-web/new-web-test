## Image vs. Live Video

[Remaker](https://remaker.ai) handles images quickly and affordably — ideal for batch processing. [Swapface](https://swapface.org) handles video in real time — the only tool in this category built for live streaming and video calls.

**Winner: Depends on medium.** Images? Remaker. Live video? Swapface.

## Quality Performance

Remaker delivers good-quality image swaps with fast processing. Swapface optimizes for real-time video — quality is good for streaming but trades some detail for real-time performance.

**Winner: Remaker** for image quality. Swapface for real-time video capability.
