## Beginner vs. Professional

[Pixverse](https://pixverse.ai/) is the best entry point for AI video generation. No cost, no watermark, fast results. If you are curious about AI video and want to try it without commitment, Pixverse is the answer.

[Runway](https://runwayml.com/) is the destination for professionals. Director Mode gives cinematography-level control. The timeline editor supports multi-clip projects. The output quality is the highest in AI video. But you pay for it — $15/month minimum.
