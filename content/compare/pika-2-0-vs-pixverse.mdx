## Creative vs. Practical

[Pika 2.0](https://pika.art/) built its reputation on Pikaffects, squish, inflate, melt, explode, creative transformations that make content stand out on social feeds. The tool is optimized for "make it look cool," not "make it look real."

[Pixverse](https://pixverse.ai/) is the practical alternative: fast generation, generous free tier, solid baseline quality. No unique creative effects, but also no friction. Generate, download, post.
