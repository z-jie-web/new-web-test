## Speed vs. Creativity

[Luma](https://lumalabs.ai/dream-machine) optimizes for speed: generate, iterate, generate again. The fast feedback loop encourages exploration. For "I need 10 variations to find the right one," Luma is efficient.

[Pika](https://pika.art/) optimizes for creativity: Pikaffects produce visuals that are genuinely novel — squish, inflate, melt, explode — effects no other tool can replicate. For "I need this to stop the scroll," Pika's creative toolkit gives you more options.
