## Timeline vs. Transcript

[CapCut](https://www.capcut.com/) uses a traditional timeline: drag, trim, layer, add effects and transitions. Familiar to anyone who has edited video before.

[Descript](https://www.descript.com/) uses a transcript as the editing interface: edit the words, the audio/video follows. Revolutionary for speech-heavy content, unnecessary for visual-first content.

## FAQ

**Which is better for podcast editing?** Descript — transcript-based editing is purpose-built for spoken-word content. Editing a podcast by reading and deleting text is dramatically faster than waveform editing.

**Can Descript do visual effects?** Descript has basic video editing but can't match CapCut's AI effects, transitions, and templates. Descript is for speech-heavy content, not visual effects.

**Are AI subtitles accurate?** Both have high accuracy. Descript's transcript-based approach means your captions are inherently accurate — the transcript IS the captions. CapCut generates captions separately.

**Which free tier is better?** CapCut's free tier is generous with basic features. Descript's free tier is limited to 1 project — essentially a trial. For free use, CapCut is the clear winner.
