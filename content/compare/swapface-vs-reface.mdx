Swapface handles live video in real time — your face on stream, in video calls, instantly. Reface puts your face into famous movie scenes and viral clips — polished, pre-made, shareable. Different tools for different video needs.

**Winner: Depends on use case.** Live video needs Swapface. Social entertainment needs Reface.

## Quality Performance

Swapface delivers real-time quality sufficient for streaming — the tradeoff is necessary for instant processing. Reface's template-based output is polished for social feeds and looks great in a feed, but has no live capability.

**Winner: Reface** for polished social content. Swapface for live performance.
