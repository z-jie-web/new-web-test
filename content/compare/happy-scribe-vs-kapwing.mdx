## Different Tools, Different Problems

[Happy Scribe](https://www.happyscribe.com/) does one thing: turns audio into accurate text. 120+ languages, clean subtitle exports, no video editing features.

[Kapwing](https://www.kapwing.com/) does video editing with team collaboration. Shared workspaces, comments, version history. Transcription is a feature within the editor, not the core product.

## Who Should Choose Happy Scribe

- Journalists and researchers needing accurate transcripts in many languages
- Teams that need transcription only, not a video editor

## Who Should Choose Kapwing

- Teams collaborating on video projects
- Anyone who needs editing first, transcription as a supporting feature
