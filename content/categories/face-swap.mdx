## What to Look For

**Photo vs video.** DeepSwapper and Remaker are photo-only. FaceFusion, Reface, FaceMagic, and Swapface handle video. If you only need photo face swaps, DeepSwapper is free and works in your browser with zero setup. If you need video, the decision tree branches into technical vs convenience.

**Technical skill required.** FaceFusion demands Python, a CUDA GPU (RTX 3060+), and command-line comfort. The setup takes 30-60 minutes for first-timers. The payoff is unlimited professional-grade face swaps with no watermarks, no subscriptions, and total privacy. Reface and FaceMagic work on your phone with no technical setup — but you trade quality and control for convenience.

**Privacy.** This is the conversation most comparison articles skip. FaceFusion and Swapface process everything locally — your face data never leaves your computer. Every other tool uploads your images to external servers. If you are swapping faces of clients, colleagues, or anyone who has not explicitly consented to their face data being uploaded to a third-party server, local processing is the only responsible choice.

**Real-time vs recorded.** Swapface is the only tool that does real-time face swap for live streaming. It integrates with OBS and Streamlabs for Twitch and YouTube. FaceFusion also supports real-time streaming in its latest versions, but with a steeper setup curve. All other tools process pre-recorded content.

**Custom footage support.** Reface locks you into its template library — you cannot upload your own videos in the mobile app. FaceMagic lets you use custom footage on mobile. FaceFusion handles any video you throw at it. If you film your own content and want to add face swap as a creative effect, Reface's template lock-in is a dealbreaker.

## FAQ

**Is there a completely free face swap tool?** Two: DeepSwapper (browser, photo only) and FaceFusion (desktop, photo and video). Both fully free, no watermarks, no usage limits.

**Which produces the most realistic results?** FaceFusion with HyperSwap 256px models. The quadrupled pixel resolution over older inswapper models captures eye reflections and skin texture that paid cloud tools struggle to match.

**Can I use face swap tools for commercial projects?** FaceFusion (open-source, Apache 2.0) — yes. Paid tiers of Reface, FaceMagic, and Remaker generally include commercial rights. Check individual terms before publishing.

**Are these tools safe to use?** FaceFusion and Swapface process locally — safest option. Web-based tools upload to external servers. Never upload photos of people without their consent. Review privacy policies before using any cloud-based face swap tool.

**What about legal concerns?** Face swapping real people without consent may violate privacy laws, defamation statutes, or platform terms of service. The software is legal — how you use it determines legality. Always obtain consent.
