# Face Swap Tools

AI face swap technology has gone from novelty to professional tool. Today's face swap tools can replace faces in photos and videos with stunning realism — for entertainment, creative content, live streaming, and even professional video production. The category has exploded in popularity, with millions of monthly searches across the globe.

## What to Look For

- **Output quality**: Professional-grade tools (FaceFusion, DeepSwapper) produce significantly better results than free mobile apps.
- **Technical skill required**: FaceFusion requires Python and GPU setup; Reface and FaceMagic work right in your browser or phone.
- **Use case**: Real-time streaming (Swapface), video face swap (FaceMagic, DeepSwapper), photo face swap (Remaker), or open-source full control (FaceFusion).
- **Privacy**: Local-processing tools like FaceFusion keep your media on your own hardware — important for sensitive content.

## Our Pick

For technically inclined users who want the best quality with no limits, **FaceFusion** is the clear winner — free, open-source, and professional-grade. For mobile-first creators, **Reface** offers the smoothest user experience. For live streamers, **Swapface** is unmatched in real-time performance.
