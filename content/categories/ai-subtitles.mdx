## What to Look For

**Your primary editing environment.** Browser-based (VEED, Kapwing), mobile-first (Captions, CapCut), or desktop with transcript-based editing (Descript). Pick the tool that fits where you already edit, switching environments adds more friction than any feature saves.

**Caption styling depth.** VEED leads on customization: fonts, brand colors, animated presets, positioning. Captions wins on animated word-by-word marking for short-form retention. CapCut has the largest template library with pre-built caption styles. If captions are a core part of your visual brand, VEED or Captions is worth the subscription.

**Accuracy requirements.** Happy Scribe's AI + optional human review achieves up to 99% accuracy, the gold standard for journalism, legal, and academic work. Descript delivers ~92-95% accuracy for clean single-speaker audio. CapCut and VEED are in the ~90% range, which is fine for social content but not for professional transcription.

**Team collaboration.** Kapwing is built for distributed teams: shared workspaces, timestamp comments, version history, role-based permissions. VEED has lighter collaboration features. Descript includes collaboration on Creator and above. If multiple people touch a video before it ships, Kapwing's collaboration depth is the differentiator.

**Pricing structure.** CapCut International at $8/month is the value leader. Captions Pro at $9.99/month is the best deal for short-form creators. Happy Scribe uses pay-as-you-go (€6/month for 720 AI minutes), ideal for occasional high-accuracy transcription. Descript at $24/month is the premium option, only worth it if you use the transcript-based editor as your primary editing workflow.

## FAQ

**Which AI subtitle tool is most accurate?** Happy Scribe with human review achieves up to 99% accuracy. Among automated-only tools, Descript's engine is most reliable at ~92-95%.

**Can these tools translate subtitles?** Yes. Happy Scribe supports 120+ languages. VEED supports 125+ languages for translation. Descript and Captions support 20-28 languages.

**Which is best for TikTok and Reels?** Captions (animated word-by-word effects) or CapCut International (all-in-one editor with templates at $8/month).

**Do I need a separate video editor?** Descript and CapCut are full editors with built-in captioning. VEED and Kapwing are browser-based editors. Happy Scribe is transcription-only, pair it with a separate editor.

**Are AI subtitles good enough to replace human captioning?** For social media and most content marketing, yes, AI accuracy is sufficient and the speed advantage is dramatic. For legal, medical, or broadcast content where errors have consequences, use Happy Scribe with human review or a professional captioning service.
