# AI Subtitles & Captions

AI subtitle and caption tools do more than add text to video — they boost viewer retention, improve accessibility, and make content discoverable across languages. With short-form video dominating social media, accurate and well-styled captions are no longer optional — they are essential for competitive content.

## What to Look For

- **Accuracy**: Transcription quality varies widely — tools like Happy Scribe lead on accuracy, while CapCut and Captions optimize for speed.
- **Editing experience**: Do you want transcript-based editing (Descript), browser-based editing (VEED, Kapwing), or mobile-first (Captions)?
- **Styling options**: Auto-captions are table stakes — look for tools with customizable caption styles that match your brand.
- **Collaboration**: For distributed teams, tools like Kapwing and VEED offer shared workspaces and commenting.

## Our Pick

For short-form creators, **Captions** and **CapCut** are the best dedicated tools for retention-optimized subtitles. For podcasters and video essayists, **Descript**'s transcript-based editing is a game-changer. For teams needing reliable transcription and translation, **Happy Scribe** is the gold standard.
