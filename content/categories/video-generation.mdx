## What to Look For

**Generation quality vs speed.** Runway produces the highest cinematic quality ceiling but takes 1-3 minutes per clip and charges premium credits. Pika generates in 30-90 seconds and costs less. Match the tool to the stakes of your project — high-stakes client work justifies Runway; daily social content does not need it.

**Resolution needs.** Kling AI outputs native 4K at $6.99/month. Most other tools cap at 1080p or charge enterprise pricing for 4K. If you deliver for large screens or broadcast, resolution is a hard requirement. If you publish to TikTok and Reels, 1080p is plenty.

**Editing integration.** Runway includes masking, rotoscoping, motion tracking, and a full timeline. Every other tool on this list is a generation engine — you generate clips, then edit elsewhere. If you want an all-in-one studio, Runway is the only option. If you already use Premiere or DaVinci Resolve, a pure generator like Kling or Pika fits better.

**Pricing model.** Credit-based pricing (Kling, Pika, PixVerse, Luma) means you pay per generation and unused credits expire monthly. Subscription-based (Runway, InVideo) gives predictable costs but lower ceilings. Topaz Video AI is the only one-time purchase option at $299 — but it is for enhancement, not generation.

**Use case fit.** InVideo assembles complete templated videos from prompts — best for marketers who never want to touch a timeline. Kling and Runway generate raw clips for editors to assemble. Topaz enhances existing footage. Pick based on whether you need a finished video or raw material.

## FAQ

**Which AI video generator is best for beginners?** InVideo AI — describe your topic and it assembles a complete video. Pika is the easiest for generating short creative clips from prompts.

**Can I use AI-generated video commercially?** Yes, on all paid plans across these tools. Free tiers typically restrict commercial use and watermark output. Check each tool's terms before publishing client work.

**How much should I budget?** $8-35/month covers a capable AI video tool. Start with Kling AI's free tier (66 credits/day) to test the technology. Upgrade to paid only when free limits constrain your output.

**Do I still need a video editor?** For most workflows, yes. AI generators produce clips. You still need an editor (CapCut, Premiere, Resolve) to assemble, color grade, add audio, and polish. Runway is the exception with its built-in editor.

**What is the difference between AI video generation and traditional editing?** AI generates new footage from text prompts. Traditional editing arranges and refines existing footage. Most professional workflows combine both — [read our full comparison](/blog/ai-video-vs-traditional-editing).
