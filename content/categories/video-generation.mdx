# AI Video Generation

AI video generation has transformed content creation — turning text prompts, images, and scripts into polished videos in minutes instead of days. Whether you need cinematic B-roll for a brand film, rapid social media shorts, or concept prototypes for client pitches, there is an AI video tool for the job.

## What to Look For

- **Output quality**: Cinematic realism (Sora, Runway) vs. stylized speed (Pika, PixVerse) — pick based on your use case.
- **Generation speed**: Social media creators need fast turnaround; filmmakers can wait for quality.
- **Control**: Some tools give you frame-by-frame motion control, others optimize for one-shot prompt-to-video.
- **Pricing model**: Credit-based (most tools) vs. subscription (Runway, InVideo) vs. one-time (Topaz).

## Our Pick

For professional creators, **Runway Gen-3** is the most complete AI video studio. For fast social content, **Pika 2.0** and **CapCut** are hard to beat on speed. For cinematic quality, **Sora** sets the benchmark — but availability is still limited.
