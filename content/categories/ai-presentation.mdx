A startup founder needs an investor deck by Friday. A sales team needs forty client presentations customized by next week. A marketing department needs every deck to use the same fonts, colors, and logo placement without hiring a designer to police it. Deck work is a permanent bottleneck, and it is exactly the kind of structured, repetitive design task AI handles well.

Two tools lead the category, and they optimize for opposite ends of the same problem. [Gamma](/reviews/gamma) gets you from a text prompt to a complete deck in under two minutes with a flexible card-based layout. [Beautiful.ai](/reviews/beautiful-ai) enforces design rules automatically, so every slide a team produces looks professionally formatted whether or not the author has design skills.

## What to Look For

**Brand control vs generation speed.** Beautiful.ai's brand kit enforcement locks fonts, colors, and logos across every deck a team produces, and it is the stronger choice when consistency is the pain point. Gamma's brand customization is limited on lower-tier plans, but nothing else matches its idea-to-deck speed: a full presentation from one text prompt in under two minutes. Pick by which problem costs you more hours.

**Where the deck goes next.** If your deck ends its life as a PDF going to investors, generation speed matters most. If it needs to be edited by clients or teammates in PowerPoint, test the round-trip first. Gamma exports to PowerPoint, but complex custom layouts can lose some native formatting on the way out.

**Data-heavy slides.** Both tools are built around structured, design-forward layouts, and both struggle with complex charts and financial models. If your deck is mostly spreadsheets and graphs, the smart-slide approach fights you rather than helps you. Traditional tools still win for data-dense work.

**Collaboration and analytics.** Beautiful.ai's collaboration features sit behind the $40/user/month Team plan, which also unlocks viewer analytics: opens, time-per-slide, and engagement patterns pushed into Salesforce. For sales teams, that is actionable pipeline data. Gamma offers real-time collaboration on lower tiers but no analytics layer.

## FAQ

**Can I build a pitch deck with AI for free?** Gamma's free tier covers the core prompt-to-deck workflow, with the $10/month Plus plan for heavier usage. Beautiful.ai has no permanent free plan, only a 14-day trial before the $12/month Pro tier. Our roundup of [free AI presentation tools for startup founders](/blog/best-free-ai-presentation-tools-for-startup-founders) covers the free options in detail.

**Do AI-generated decks export to PowerPoint?** Gamma exports to PowerPoint, Google Slides, and PDF. Complex custom layouts can lose some native formatting on export, so test your template with a real deck before committing a team workflow to it. For a step-by-step build, see our [Gamma pitch deck guide](/blog/how-to-use-gamma-to-create-a-pitch-deck).

**Will investors accept an AI-generated deck?** Investors evaluate the content, not the production process. A deck generated in two minutes with clear numbers and a sharp narrative beats a beautifully designed deck with a weak story. The tool determines how fast you iterate, not whether the deck persuades.

**Is Beautiful.ai's Team plan worth $40/user/month?** For marketing teams producing decks at volume, the brand enforcement and analytics justify the price.
