## Our Take

Captions is purpose-built for the short-form creator economy — specifically talking-head content where captions, eye contact, and visual polish directly impact viewer retention. The auto captions are best-in-class for TikTok, Reels, and YouTube Shorts, with animated word-by-word highlighting that has become the standard look for viral short-form content. The 2026 AI features — AI Eye Contact correction, AI B-roll insertion, AI Dubbing with Lipdub — transform what was once a caption tool into a full content production app. Pro at $9.99/month is strong value for any creator posting talking-head videos weekly. The free tier is effectively a demo (watermark on all exports). If short-form retention metrics are how you measure success, Captions is the best dedicated tool for the job.

## What Captions Actually Does

Captions starts with speech-to-text transcription in 100+ languages, generating word-level timed captions that animate in sync with speech. The caption styling system is the deepest in the category: 100+ animated templates, custom fonts and colors, emoji integration, and preset themes designed for different content types (educational, comedic, inspirational, product showcase).

Beyond captions, the AI features are what make Captions stand out in 2026. **AI Eye Contact** digitally corrects your gaze so you appear to be looking directly at the camera even while reading a script or glancing at notes — a feature that reviewers consistently describe as genuinely impressive. **AI B-Roll** automatically analyzes your talking-head footage and inserts relevant stock footage and visual overlays at natural cut points, saving hours of manual B-roll sourcing and editing. **AI Dubbing + Lipdub** translates your audio into 28+ languages and syncs the lip movements of the speaker to match the translated track — more polished than basic dubbing tools that only swap audio tracks. **AI Denoise** cleans background noise for cleaner audio and therefore more accurate captions.

For creators who want to scale beyond their own face, Captions offers **AI Twin** (creates a digital avatar from a single selfie that speaks any script with your cloned voice), **AI Actors** (stock AI avatars for when you don't want to be on camera), and **AI Voice Clone** for consistent voiceovers.

The mobile-first design is a deliberate choice — short-form creators live on their phones, and Captions' iOS and Android apps are more polished than the desktop experience. This is the right priority for the target audience but worth noting if you prefer editing on a laptop.

## Who Should Use Captions

**Captions is a strong fit if you:**
- Create talking-head short-form content for TikTok, Reels, or Shorts and care about retention optimization
- Want animated captions that look professionally designed without manual keyframing
- Need AI Eye Contact correction for scripted videos
- Release 3+ videos per week and want AI to handle repetitive tasks (B-roll insertion, denoising, dubbing)
- Edit primarily on mobile

**Captions is not the best choice if you:**
- Create long-form content over 10 minutes (use Descript or Premiere)
- Rarely use talking-head format — AI Eye Contact and B-roll features are less relevant
- Need a desktop-first editing experience
- Want a completely free tool — the free tier's watermark makes it unusable for publishing

## vs CapCut and VEED

Captions and CapCut overlap heavily in the short-form space. CapCut has a bigger template library and better desktop support. Captions wins on specialized short-form AI features: AI Eye Contact correction and AI B-roll insertion have no direct equivalent in CapCut. VEED is a more general-purpose browser editor better suited for teams and multilingual workflows. For the solo short-form creator optimizing for retention, Captions offers the most focused feature set.
