# Captions Review

Captions combines automatic subtitles, creator-focused editing, dubbing, and talking-head enhancement features, making it especially relevant for short-form video teams that care about retention and fast publishing.

## Key Features

- **Auto Captions**
- **Short-Form**

## Our Take

Captions is purpose-built for the creator economy — specifically short-form video creators optimizing for viewer retention. The auto captions are best-in-class for TikTok, Reels, and YouTube Shorts, with styling options that make videos feel polished and professional. The built-in editing tools and dubbing features make it more than just a captioning tool. The mobile-first design is a strength for its target audience, but desktop-first editors may find the workflow unfamiliar. For short-form creators who live on their phones and care about every percentage point of retention, Captions is the best dedicated tool.

Free tier with basic features. Pro at $9.99/month for premium captions, dubbing, and advanced editing. Team plans available for content teams.
