## Key Features

**Slide-to-Video Conversion**, upload a PowerPoint, PDF, or article URL, and Elai converts each slide into a video scene with an AI avatar presenter. The AI Storyboard feature takes a text prompt and generates both the script and video structure automatically. This cuts the most time-consuming part of training video production: translating existing course materials into a visual format.

**SCORM and LMS Export**, Elai exports video courses as SCORM 1.2 and SCORM 2004 packages that upload directly to any compliant LMS, including Moodle, Thinkific, and Articulate 360. The export tracks learner interactions, quiz scores, completion status, and time spent, the same data you would expect from any traditional e-learning module. This is the feature that separates Elai from generalist avatar tools that treat video as a standalone asset rather than a trackable learning object.

**Interactive Training Elements**, beyond basic video, Elai supports embedded quizzes (scored and ungraded), clickable hotspots, customizable buttons, and video branching where learner choices determine which scene plays next. These elements turn passive video watching into active training completion, essential for compliance courses and skills assessments.

**Multi-Language Avatars and Voice Cloning**, 80+ AI avatars across full-body, studio, selfie, and animated mascot styles. Voice support covers 75+ languages with 450+ voice accents. Voice cloning is available in 28+ languages, letting organizations create a consistent narrator identity across global training content.

**Corporate Branding Controls**, the Brand Kit applies your company's logo, colors, fonts, and watermarks to every video. Custom avatars can be created from uploaded footage, no studio visit required. For enterprises with strict brand guidelines, this matters more than avatar photorealism.

## Our Take

Elai makes the most sense when your primary metric is training completion, not video views. The SCORM export, quiz embedding, and branching scenario features are not add-ons. They are the reason to choose Elai over a more polished but less training-aware tool.

The Panopto acquisition changed the trajectory. Elai now feeds directly into Panopto's video management ecosystem, which means enterprise L&D teams get searchable, secure video hosting, analytics on who watched what, and integration with existing LMS infrastructure, all without stitching together separate tools. For organizations already on Panopto, Elai becomes the obvious content creation layer.

The trade-off is avatar realism. Elai's avatars look like professional corporate presenters, clean and competent, but clearly AI-generated. They do not match the photorealism of HeyGen's Avatar IV models or the emotional range of Synthesia's Expressive Avatars. For customer-facing marketing content, this matters. For internal training where the content is the point and the presenter just needs to be clear and credible, Elai's avatar quality is sufficient.

The pricing structure requires attention. SCORM export, custom avatars, voice cloning, and 4K output are all on the Enterprise tier, which means custom pricing. For small training teams, this can push the effective cost well above the advertised Basic plan rate.

## What Elai Really Does

Elai takes your existing training materials and turns them into structured video courses. The core workflow starts with content you already have: a slide deck, a PDF handbook, a compliance document, a URL. Elai parses the content into scenes, assigns an AI avatar to present each slide, and generates the video with synchronized lip movements and voiceover.

The video editor is slide-based, similar to PowerPoint. Each scene has an avatar, text overlays, images, and optional interactive elements. You can add quizzes between scenes to check understanding. You can create branching paths where a correct answer advances the learner and an incorrect one loops them back to review. For compliance training where proof of completion matters, these interactive elements are not optional, they are the difference between a video someone skipped and training someone completed.

The SCORM export packages the entire course, video, quizzes, branching logic, completion tracking, into a single ZIP file. Upload to your LMS, assign to learners, and track results alongside your other training content. This workflow is entirely different from exporting an MP4 and hoping someone watches it.

For teams producing training at volume, Elai's API and Zapier integration enable automated video generation. Connect a content source, trigger video creation when new materials are added, and publish to Panopto or your LMS automatically. The API is not as mature as Synthesia's, and the integration ecosystem is smaller, but for Panopto-connected workflows, the integration depth is unmatched.

## Who Should Use Elai

**Elai is a strong fit if you:**

- Run corporate L&D and need to convert existing slide decks, PDFs, and documents into video courses at scale
- Use Panopto as your video management platform (the integration is deep and native)
- Need SCORM-compliant course packages that track learner progress inside your LMS
- Produce multilingual training for global teams (75+ languages, 450+ voices)

## vs HeyGen, Synthesia, and Other AI Avatar Tools

[HeyGen](/reviews/heygen) leads on avatar photorealism and marketing-focused features. Its Avatar IV models are more lifelike than Elai's presenters, and its video translation with lip-sync covers 175+ languages. For sales outreach videos, product demos, and customer-facing content, HeyGen is the stronger pick. For internal training where SCORM tracking and quiz integration matter more than photorealism, Elai's training-specific tooling gives it the edge.

[Synthesia](/reviews/synthesia) is the enterprise compliance leader, SOC 2 Type II, ISO 42001, SSO, audit trails. For finance, healthcare, and government training, Synthesia's certifications make it the default choice. Elai counters with deeper Panopto integration, slide-to-video conversion that preserves your existing content structure, and interactive training elements embedded directly in the video editor rather than treated as separate modules.

[Colossyan](/reviews/colossyan) is Elai's closest competitor in the L&D space. Both target training teams. Both offer SCORM export. Both support interactive quizzes and branching scenarios. Colossyan wins on accessible SCORM pricing (all paid plans include it) and a larger avatar library (150+). Elai wins on Panopto ecosystem integration and slide-to-video conversion fidelity. If you are on Panopto, Elai is the natural choice. If not, Colossyan is worth comparing directly.

[D-ID](/reviews/d-id) specializes in animating still photos into talking-head videos, a completely different capability from Elai's slide-based course creation. D-ID is cheaper ($5.90/month entry) and better for personalized one-off videos from photos. It has no SCORM export, no quiz builder, and no LMS integration. The two tools rarely compete for the same buyer.

[Tavus](/reviews/tavus) solves real-time conversational video at scale, one-to-one personalized interactions driven by AI. Elai is one-to-many, pre-rendered training content. They address completely different problems and almost never compete directly.

[Google Gemini Avatar](/reviews/google-gemini-avatar) is a consumer-grade digital twin tool, clone your face and voice, then generate 10-second creative clips. It is not a training platform and does not compete with Elai for corporate use cases.

## FAQ

**Does Elai support SCORM export?** Yes, SCORM 1.2 and SCORM 2004 export is available on the Enterprise plan. The export packages video, quizzes, branching logic, and completion tracking into a single SCORM-compliant ZIP file for LMS upload.

**Can I convert my existing PowerPoint slides into training videos?** Yes, Elai's slide-to-video feature accepts PPTX, PDF, and URL inputs and converts each slide into a video scene with an AI avatar presenter. The AI Storyboard can also generate scripts and video structure from a text prompt.

**How does Elai compare to Synthesia for corporate training?** Synthesia wins on enterprise compliance (SOC 2 Type II, ISO 42001) and avatar polish. Elai wins on interactive training features (embedded quizzes, branching scenarios, hotspots) and Panopto ecosystem integration. If compliance certifications are your top concern, choose Synthesia. If training interactivity and Panopto integration matter more, choose Elai.

**What languages does Elai support?** 75+ languages with 450+ voice accents for standard text-to-speech. Voice cloning is available in 28+ languages, letting you create a consistent narrator voice across multilingual training content.

**Is there a free version of Elai?** A free tier exists with 1 minute of video credit, watermarked output, and 1-slide renders. It is designed for testing the platform, not for producing real training content. The Basic plan at $23/month (annual billing) is the practical starting point.
