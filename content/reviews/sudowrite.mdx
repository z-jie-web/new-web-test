## Key Features

- **Story Engine** ,  Sudowrite's flagship fiction-writing system. You provide a story concept (premise, characters, setting, tone), and Story Engine generates a structured beat outline, then writes scene-by-scene prose following that outline. It's not a "generate a novel in one click" button, it's a guided writing process where you review, revise, and approve each beat before prose generation. The output maintains consistent character voice, emotional arcs, and narrative tension because the engine writes from the outline, not from a single prompt. This is completely different from asking ChatGPT to "write a chapter," where quality degrades after 500 words as the model loses track of what happened earlier.
- **Canvas (Plot Visualization)** ,  A visual workspace where you map story elements as cards, characters, locations, plot points, themes, objects, and connect them with relationships (knows, wants, fears, betrayed by, lives in). The canvas auto-generates connections as you add elements, suggesting relationships you might not have considered. The value: seeing your entire story structure visually catches plot holes, orphaned characters, and pacing issues that are invisible in a text-based outline. Think of it as Scrivener's corkboard but with AI suggesting connections between your story elements.
- **Plot Braiding** ,  Imagine you're writing a novel with three subplots: the romance arc, the mystery arc, and the character growth arc. Plot Braiding analyzes your existing chapters and identifies where each subplot appears, what beats are covered, what's missing, and what's overdone. It then suggests where the next beat of each subplot should land and generates a draft that weaves them together. This is the feature that solves the hardest problem in novel writing: keeping multiple threads coherent across 80,000 words.
- **Character Voice Consistency** ,  You define a character's voice with sample dialogue, internal monologue, and description. Sudowrite analyzes the patterns, word choice, sentence rhythm, emotional range, and generates future dialogue and chapters that read like the same character. Across a 300-page novel, characters don't accidentally start sounding like each other. This is a problem that plagues general AI writers, Jasper's Brand Voice is for marketing tone, not for differentiating three point-of-view characters in a novel.
- **First Chapter Feedback** ,  Paste your first chapter, and Sudowrite analyzes it for pacing, character introduction, dialogue-to-narration ratio, sensory detail density, and hook strength. It gives specific, actionable feedback: "Your protagonist's motivation is implied but never stated explicitly, add a sentence in the opening paragraph." This catches the issues that make agents reject manuscripts in the first 5 pages, before you've written 300 pages.

## What Sudowrite Really Does

Sudowrite is a browser-based fiction writing platform. It's not a chatbot. You don't open it and start typing prompts. You open it, create a project, and work through a structured process.

**Story Engine workflow:**

1. **Brainstorm**: Describe your story concept. Sudowrite asks clarifying questions: Who is the protagonist? What do they want? What's stopping them? What changes by the end? This stage produces a one-page story synopsis.

2. **Outline**: Sudowrite generates a beat-by-beat outline. Each beat is a paragraph describing what happens in that story section. You can reorder, delete, add, or edit beats. This is where you control the story structure. The AI follows your outline, not the other way around.

3. **Prose**: With the outline approved, Sudowrite generates prose for each beat, one scene at a time. Each generation pulls from the full outline, character definitions, and established tone, so Chapter 12's prose is consistent with Chapter 3's characterizations. You can regenerate, edit, or rewrite any scene.

4. **Revision**: After a full draft, Sudowrite helps with revision passes: expand underwritten scenes, tighten pacing, sharpen dialogue, add sensory details, check character voice consistency.

**Canvas** is a parallel workspace. While Story Engine handles linear drafting, Canvas handles non-linear story development. You create cards for everything: characters with traits and motivations, locations with description and mood, plot points with cause and effect, themes with symbolic anchors. Sudowrite auto-suggests connections and flags contradictions ("Character A's motivation in Chapter 5 contradicts their stated goal in Chapter 2").

**Plot Braiding** analyzes the manuscript for subplot distribution. If the romance subplot hasn't appeared in 6 chapters, it flags it. If the mystery subplot is front-loaded and disappears in Act 2, it flags it. It generates bridging scenes that weave subplots back in at the right pacing intervals.

**Character Voice training** works like Jasper's Brand Voice but for fictional characters. You define each character with:
- Sample dialogue (50-200 words)
- Internal monologue style (stream-of-consciousness, analytical, emotional, detached)
- Vocabulary tier (colloquial, formal, technical, dialect-specific)
- Speech patterns (sentence length, preferred phrases, verbal tics)

Sudowrite applies these settings to every generation involving that character, so the gruff detective doesn't accidentally speak like the bubbly sidekick.
