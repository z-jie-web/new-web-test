## What Suno Actually Does

Suno is a web-based AI music generator. You start in one of two modes: **Simple** (describe a style and theme, Suno writes everything) or **Custom** (write your own lyrics, specify genre, tempo, and structure).

Type or paste lyrics into the Custom mode — verse, chorus, verse, bridge, chorus — and pick a genre. Suno generates two variations of the song with full instrumentation and vocals. Each generation takes 20-30 seconds. You can extend tracks (add an outro, double a chorus), remix sections, or generate new takes on the same lyrics with different styles.

Generated tracks open in **Suno Studio**, the in-browser DAW. You see 12 isolated stems in a timeline view. Mute the vocal stem to make an instrumental. Solo the drum stem to check the beat. Apply EQ to brighten the vocals or warm up the bass. Use warp markers to stretch or compress sections without changing pitch. Export stems individually as WAV files (Pro and Premier) or download the full mix as MP3.

**Voice cloning** (Premier) works from a 30-60 second clean vocal sample. The cloned voice appears as an option when generating new tracks. It takes 5-10 minutes to process the initial clone. The quality is surprisingly good — your voice's character comes through — but the AI doesn't perfectly replicate your phrasing or emotional delivery. Think of it as "your voice as an instrument" rather than "your voice as a singer."

## FAQ

**Can I use Suno songs commercially?** Yes, on paid plans (Pro and Premier). You own the songs you generate and can use them commercially. The free tier is for non-commercial use only. Note: the ongoing Sony/Universal lawsuits create uncertainty around the underlying training data — commercial rights are granted by Suno's terms, but the legal precedent is still being established.

**Can I copyright Suno-generated music?** Not fully, as of 2026. The US Copyright Office requires "meaningful human authorship." Suno-generated output alone is unlikely to qualify. If you substantially remix, re-record vocals, add live instruments, or restructure the song, you can claim copyright over your contributions. The raw AI output is not copyrightable on its own.

**How good is the audio quality?** 44.1kHz stereo — CD quality. The Pro and Premier tiers export lossless WAV. Suno Studio stems have some audible bleed between frequency bands, so professional producers will want to do final stem cleanup in a proper DAW.

**Does Suno work on mobile?** Suno has iOS and Android apps with the same generation capabilities as the web version. Suno Studio (DAW) is currently web-only.
