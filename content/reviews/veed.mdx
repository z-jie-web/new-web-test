# VEED Review

VEED offers browser-based editing with subtitle generation, caption styling, and social publishing support, making it a practical choice for creators and small teams producing fast-moving content across global channels.

## Key Features

- **Web Editor**
- **Caption Workflow**

## Our Take

VEED is the best browser-based video editor for teams that need to move fast. No downloads, no installs — just open a browser tab and start editing. The subtitle and caption editor is intuitive and polished, and the collaboration features make it a strong choice for distributed teams. It is not a replacement for professional NLEs like Premiere or DaVinci Resolve, but for the 80% of video tasks that do not need that level of complexity, VEED is faster and simpler. The free plan watermark is a fair limitation for a tool this capable.

## Pricing

Free plan with watermarked exports. Pro at $18/month for watermark-free, 4K export, and more features. Business at $30/month for teams with collaboration tools.
