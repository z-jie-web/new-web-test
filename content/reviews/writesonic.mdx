Brand Voice learns from sample content but doesn't match Jasper's depth — it captures tone (professional, casual, witty) but not industry-specific terminology or complex style patterns.

## What Writesonic Actually Does

Writesonic is a browser-based AI writing platform with two modes: template-based generation and long-form Article Writer.

**Template generation** works like any AI writer. Pick a template (Blog Intro, Product Description, Ad Copy, Social Caption), describe what you want, and Writesonic generates it. You can switch between AI models (GPT-3.5, GPT-4o, Claude) depending on your plan tier. The editor has one-click rewrite, expand, shorten, and change-tone buttons.

**Article Writer 6.0** is the differentiator. You input:
- Topic (e.g., "best CRM software for small business")
- Primary and secondary keywords (Writesonic suggests keywords via Ahrefs if you're unsure)
- Competitors to outrank (paste 3-5 competitor URLs, Writesonic analyzes their structure and keyword usage)
- Tone and point of view

Article Writer generates a full blog post outline, then writes each section. The output includes:
- H2/H3 heading structure optimized for featured snippets
- FAQ schema with structured Q&A (Google loves this)
- Internal linking suggestions (if you provide a sitemap)
- Meta description and title tag

Generation takes 3-5 minutes for a 3,000-word article. The output is 70-80% publish-ready — you'll need to fact-check, add specific examples, and adjust keyword density, but the structural work is done.

**SEO Writing Assistant** scores your draft in real-time:
- Target keyword usage (too little = won't rank, too much = keyword stuffing penalty)
- Heading structure (H2s should contain semantic variations of the main keyword)
- Readability (Flesch score, sentence length, paragraph length)
- Content length vs competitors (if competitors average 2,500 words and you wrote 1,200, Writesonic flags it)

**Chatsonic** is Writesonic's ChatGPT alternative. Web search enabled, which means it can pull current events and recent data (ChatGPT's knowledge cutoff is a weakness). Useful for research ("what are the top CRM tools launching in 2026?") and quick ad-hoc writing. Less useful than the Article Writer for SEO content.
