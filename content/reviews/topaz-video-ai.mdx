## What Topaz Video AI Really Does

Topaz Video AI uses machine learning models to improve video in ways that traditional filters cannot. It does not just sharpen pixels, it reconstructs missing detail by analyzing surrounding frames and understanding what hair, skin, fabric, and text should look like at higher resolutions.

The platform has four core capabilities. **Upscaling** increases resolution: SD to HD, 1080p to 4K, 4K to 8K or even 16K. The AI rebuilds edges, textures, and facial features rather than just stretching pixels. **Denoising** removes grain and digital noise without the waxy, over-smoothed look of traditional noise reduction. **Frame interpolation** creates new frames between existing ones for smooth slow motion, converting 24fps footage to 60fps or 120fps with natural motion. **Stabilization** corrects shaky handheld footage without the warping artifacts of basic stabilizers.

The model library is deep: 19+ specialized AI models as of version 3.26 (2026). **Proteus** handles general live-action upscaling as the default model. **Iris** specializes in face detail recovery from noisy or compressed sources. **Artemis** targets heavily compressed archival material, old DVDs, VHS rips, low-bitrate streaming downloads. **Gaia** is purpose-built for anime and cel animation, preserving clean line art at 4K and 8K. The **Starlight** family (Precise, HQ, Sharp, Mini, Fast) covers the full spectrum from degraded archival restoration to modern high-resolution improvement, now included in all paid plans. **Chronos** and **Aion** handle cinematic frame interpolation for slow-motion, with Aion producing the highest quality at the cost of heavy GPU usage.

Topaz also converts SDR to HDR (Hyperion model), deinterlaces old video sources (Dione), and supports batch processing with job queues for overnight rendering sessions.

## Performance and Hardware Reality

Processing speed depends entirely on your GPU. On an RTX 4070: Proteus runs at ~7-8 fps, Nyx at ~4-5 fps, and Aion at ~1-2 fps for 4K output. Upscaling a 1-hour video from 1080p to 4K takes roughly 1-3 hours. On older GPUs or integrated graphics, the same task can run overnight. Apple Silicon Macs are supported via Metal acceleration, M3/M4 Pro chips handle most models adequately, though CUDA on NVIDIA remains faster for the heaviest workloads.

Local rendering is unlimited on all plans, your footage never leaves your machine unless you opt into cloud rendering. Cloud rendering costs extra credits (Personal: 25/mo, Pro: 100/mo) but offloads processing to Topaz servers, useful for laptop users without a discrete GPU.

## Who Should Use Topaz Video AI

- Own a powerful NVIDIA GPU (RTX 4070+) and process video regularly

- Prefer a one-time purchase (all plans are subscription-only since 2025)
- Need a simple, one-click solution, the model selection and settings require learning

## vs Runway and Kling AI for Upscaling

Topaz Video AI is a dedicated improvement and upscaling tool, not an AI video generator. It upscales, denoises, and sharpens existing footage, you bring the video, Topaz improves it. Runway and Kling generate new AI video from scratch. These are complementary: generate clips in Runway or Kling, then upscale them in Topaz for 4K/8K delivery. For upscaling quality alone, Topaz has no equal at the consumer price point, Runway's upscaling is decent but not broadcast-grade, and Kling's native 4K generation is a different approach (generating at 4K vs upscaling existing footage).
