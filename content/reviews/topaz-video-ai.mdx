# Topaz Video AI Review

Topaz Video AI is a premium enhancement tool for upscaling, denoising, sharpening, and frame interpolation, often chosen by editors who need cleaner footage, better resolution, and stronger final delivery quality.

## Key Features

- **Upscaling**
- **Denoise**

## Our Take

Topaz Video AI is in a class of its own for video enhancement. The upscaling quality — especially for archival and low-resolution footage — is the best you can get without a professional post-production house. The trade-off is hardware: you need a powerful NVIDIA GPU and patience, as processing large files can take hours. For professional editors restoring old footage, upscaling for 4K/8K delivery, or cleaning noisy video, Topaz is worth every dollar. Casual users should look at cloud-based alternatives — the hardware requirements are no joke.
