# D-ID Review

D-ID enables talking avatars and face-animated videos from still images, making it useful for personalized outreach, lightweight digital presenters, and experimental branded communication formats.

## Key Features

- **Talking Avatar**
- **Personalization**

## Our Take

D-ID occupies a unique niche: animating still photos into talking avatars. It is not trying to compete with Synthesia or HeyGen on studio-quality presenter videos. Instead, it excels at personalized outreach — turning a customer's photo into a talking thank-you video, or creating lightweight digital presenters from a single image. The realism varies, and it is limited to talking-head format, but for personalized marketing at scale, D-ID does something no other tool does as easily. Think of it as a personalization engine, not a studio replacement.

## Pricing

Starter at $5.99/month (10 minutes of video). Lite at $18/month. Advanced at $60/month. Enterprise plans with API access for programmatic generation.
