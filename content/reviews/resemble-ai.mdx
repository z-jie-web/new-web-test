## What Resemble AI Really Does

**Voice cloning** comes in two tiers. Rapid clones cost ~$2/month per voice and work from short samples, good enough for internal prototyping, chatbots, and low-stakes content. Pro clones cost ~$5/month per voice and capture more nuance: vocal texture, breath patterns, micro-expressions. For brand voices, audiobook narrators, or any voice that represents your company publicly, Pro is the minimum.

**Speech-to-speech** is the feature that sets Resemble apart from the ElevenLabs/Play.ht/Murf AI pack. Instead of typing text and generating audio, you speak into a microphone and Resemble transforms your voice into the target voice in real time, preserving your pacing, intonation, and emotional delivery. For live applications (conversational AI agents, real-time dubbing, game character dialogue driven by a live actor), this is a completely different capability than TTS. It enables performance-driven voice generation rather than script-driven.

**Emotion control** works at the sentence level. You can mark individual sentences with emotional tags, excited, somber, urgent, calm, and the model adjusts delivery accordingly. This is not the same as "pick a happy voice from a dropdown." It is granular direction over how individual lines land. For audiobook narrators, game dialogue writers, and video producers who need voice to carry emotional weight, this is the key differentiator.

**Resemble Fill** is generative audio inpainting. Record a voiceover, realize you said the wrong product name at 0:45, select that segment, type the correction, and Resemble regenerates just that portion in the same voice with matching tone and pacing. No re-recording. For content teams that iterate heavily on scripts, this saves hours.

**Deepfake detection** analyzes audio, images, and video for AI-generated content. It provides an authenticity score and detailed analysis rather than a binary yes/no. The Chrome extension lets you check media on any webpage. The API supports batch processing for trust-and-safety teams screening user-generated content at scale. On-prem deployment is available for organizations that cannot send data to the cloud.

## FAQ

**How good is Resemble AI's voice cloning?** Strong, especially at the Pro tier. Emotional nuance, vocal texture, and pacing are well-preserved. Rapid clones are adequate for prototyping but Pro is the minimum for public-facing content. ElevenLabs cloning quality is comparable; the difference is Resemble's sentence-level emotional control.

**What is Resemble Fill?** Generative audio inpainting, you select a segment of existing audio, type new words, and Resemble regenerates that portion in the cloned voice with matching tone. Useful for fixing script errors without re-recording entire voiceovers.

**Does Resemble AI detect deepfakes from other platforms?** Yes. The detection system is multimodal (audio, image, video) and analyzes content regardless of which AI tool generated it. It provides an authenticity score, not just a binary verdict.

**Is there a free tier?** The Flex plan costs $0 to start, you only pay for usage beyond what your initial credits cover. The Creator plan at $19/month includes 15,000 seconds. Free credits on Flex never expire.

**Can I deploy Resemble AI on my own servers?** Yes. On-prem deployment is available for Enterprise customers, covering both voice generation and deepfake detection. This is a key differentiator for security-sensitive organizations.
