# Reface Review

Reface is the most downloaded face swap app globally, letting users swap faces in videos, GIFs, and images with realistic results.

## Key Features

- **Face Swap** — Swap your face onto celebrities, movie scenes, and viral clips
- **Video Swap** — Animate your face into short video templates
- **GIF Maker** — Create shareable face-swapped GIFs

## Our Take

Reface is the clear market leader in mobile face swap. With a massive template library and smooth UX, it is the best starting point for anyone exploring AI face swap content. The watermark on the free tier is expected — upgrading is worth it for serious creators.
