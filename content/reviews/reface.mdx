Reface is the most downloaded face swap app globally, letting users swap faces in videos, GIFs, and images with realistic results.

## Key Features

- **Face Swap** — Swap your face onto celebrities, movie scenes, and viral clips
- **Video Swap** — Animate your face into short video templates
- **GIF Maker** — Create shareable face-swapped GIFs

## Our Take

Reface is the most popular face swap app for a reason — it is fast, fun, and dead simple to use. With over 300 million downloads, it has the largest template library (updated daily with trending movie scenes, memes, and viral clips) and the most polished mobile UX in the category. The annual plan at $24.99/year is easily the best value — avoid the weekly subscription, which can quietly drain $150-200/year. The biggest limitation is that you cannot upload your own videos in the mobile app; you are locked into Reface's template library. For casual social content and meme creation, Reface is the best starting point. For custom video face swaps with your own footage, look at FaceFusion (desktop, free) or FaceMagic (mobile).

## What Reface Actually Does

Reface replaces faces in pre-made video templates, GIFs, and images. You take a selfie, pick a template — a movie scene, a viral dance clip, a celebrity moment — and Reface maps your face onto the character in seconds. The output loops as a short animated clip, ready to share to TikTok, Instagram, or messaging apps.

The template library is Reface's moat. Updated daily across categories (movies, music, sports, memes, seasonal), it gives you an endless feed of trending content to put your face on. The AI handles lighting matching, angle adjustment, and expression mapping — it is not photorealistic studio quality, but for a mobile app rendering in seconds, it is impressively good.

Reface has expanded beyond pure face swap into an AI content suite. **AI Avatars** generate stylized portraits (cinematic, fantasy, vintage). **AI Hairstyle** lets you preview different cuts and colors realistically. **AI Baby Generator** blends two parent photos. **Photo Animation (Revive)** brings still photos to life with motion. **Gender Swap** and **Age Changes** are playful extras. These features are solid but secondary — the core face swap remains the reason to download.

## Limitations

The mobile app restricts you to Reface's template library — you cannot upload a custom video and swap your face into it. For that, you need a desktop tool like FaceFusion or a web-based alternative. Free-tier exports are watermarked. The weekly subscription pricing model has generated user complaints about unexpected charges — always check your App Store subscriptions after signing up, and prefer the annual plan.

## Who Should Use Reface

**Reface is a strong fit if you:**

- Enjoy casual, fun face swaps without technical setup

## vs FaceFusion and FaceMagic

Reface is the most polished mobile face swap app with the largest template library (daily updates, trending clips). FaceFusion is the professional desktop option — unlimited, free, higher quality, but requires Python and a GPU. FaceMagic offers custom video face swap on mobile — upload your own videos, which Reface cannot do in the app. Most users start with Reface for its ease, then graduate to FaceMagic if they need custom video support, or FaceFusion if they need professional quality.

## FAQ

**Can I upload my own videos to Reface?** Not in the mobile app. Reface uses its own template library. The web version ("Unboring by Reface") offers more flexibility but costs extra.

**Is Reface safe?** The app collects facial data for processing. Review the privacy policy and terms before uploading sensitive content. Cancel via App Store/Google Play subscription settings — deleting the app does not stop billing.
