# FaceMagic Review

FaceMagic specializes in AI-powered face swap for videos, allowing creators to replace faces in video clips with natural-looking results.

## Key Features

- **Video Face Swap** — Replace faces in existing video clips
- **AI Swap** — Neural network-powered face mapping for realistic results
- **Social Media** — Optimized for short-form vertical video formats

## Our Take

FaceMagic is a solid mid-tier option for video face swap. It won't match high-end desktop tools for quality, but for social media creators needing quick face swaps in short videos, it delivers good results with minimal effort.

## Pricing

Freemium — limited free swaps per day, subscription for unlimited use.
