Notion AI is the AI writing tool that lives where you already work. Instead of copying text to [Jasper](/reviews/jasper) or [Copy.ai](/reviews/copy-ai), Notion AI generates content directly inside your docs, databases, and wikis — and it pulls context from your existing workspace. The killer feature: Autofill for databases, which tags and categorizes hundreds of rows in seconds. The catch: you need a Notion subscription ($10/month) plus the AI add-on ($10/month per user), making it more expensive than standalone tools, and there are zero SEO features.

## Key Features

- **Context-Aware Generation** — Notion AI reads your workspace. Ask it to "write a product update based on last week's sprint notes," and it pulls from your actual sprint doc. Ask it to "summarize this project," and it scans linked pages and mentions. Jasper and Copy.ai start from blank prompts. Notion AI starts from what you've already written. This is the workflow advantage — less copy-paste, more "generate from what's here."
- **Autofill for Databases** — The feature that justifies the $10/month cost for database-heavy users. Select a column in a Notion database, hit "Autofill," and Notion AI fills every row based on a prompt. Use cases: auto-tag 200 support tickets by sentiment (Positive, Neutral, Negative), auto-categorize 500 leads by industry, auto-generate SEO meta descriptions for 100 blog post entries. This turns 3 hours of manual tagging into 30 seconds of AI work.
- **Q&A Across Workspace** — Ask Notion AI a question, and it searches your entire workspace, then cites specific pages and blocks in the answer. Better than Notion's native search because it synthesizes information rather than just returning keyword matches. Example: "What were the main blockers in Q3?" → Notion AI scans retrospectives, meeting notes, and project docs, then returns a 3-bullet answer with citations.
- **Writing Assistance In-Line** — Highlight any text block and Notion AI can: improve writing, fix grammar, translate, make it longer/shorter, change tone (professional, casual, direct). Same features as Grammarly or Jasper, but inside Notion's editor instead of a separate tool. The UX is seamless — no switching tabs, no copy-paste.
- **Meeting Notes → Action Items** — Paste raw meeting transcript, ask Notion AI to "extract action items," and it outputs a bulleted list with assignees and deadlines. Works with voice-to-text from Otter.ai or Fireflies.ai transcripts pasted into Notion.

## What Notion AI Actually Does

Notion AI is an add-on feature inside Notion's workspace. It's not a separate app. You enable it, and every Notion page gains an "Ask AI" command and a floating AI menu.

**Writing assistance** works in-line. Highlight any text, press Space, and Notion AI offers: improve writing, fix spelling/grammar, translate (50+ languages), make longer/shorter, change tone, simplify language. The output replaces your selection or appears below it. Same workflow as Grammarly, but inside Notion.

**Content generation** starts with a prompt. In any empty text block, type Space and Notion AI offers templates: brainstorm ideas, write a blog post, create a to-do list, write a job description, draft an email, summarize meeting notes. Pick a template, describe what you want, and Notion AI generates a draft. The generation pulls context from your workspace — if you're in a project doc, Notion AI knows the project name and recent updates because it reads the page you're on and linked pages.

**Database Autofill** works on any Notion database property. Select a column (e.g., "Category" or "Sentiment"), click the Autofill button, write a prompt ("Tag each row by sentiment: Positive, Neutral, or Negative based on the feedback text"), and Notion AI fills every row. It processes 100+ rows in under a minute. You can preview results before applying.

**Q&A** searches your workspace. Ask a question ("What were the main blockers in Q3?"), and Notion AI scans all pages you have access to, then returns a synthesized answer with citations. Each citation links to the specific page and block where the information came from. This is faster and more useful than Notion's keyword search for open-ended questions.

## FAQ

**Is Notion AI free?** No. There's a limited trial (20-40 AI requests depending on your Notion plan), then you pay $10/month per user. The trial is enough to test, not enough for regular use.

**Can Notion AI write SEO blog posts?** It can write blog posts, but it has no SEO features — no keyword research, no optimization scoring, no readability analysis. If you're writing content to rank on Google, use [Writesonic](/reviews/writesonic) instead. Notion AI is for internal docs and team wikis, not public-facing SEO content.

**Does Notion AI work if I don't use Notion?** No. It's an add-on inside Notion. If you don't have a Notion workspace, Notion AI is useless. For standalone AI writing, use [Copy.ai](/reviews/copy-ai), [Jasper](/reviews/jasper), or [Writesonic](/reviews/writesonic).

**What AI model does Notion AI use?** Notion doesn't disclose the model, but output quality suggests GPT-4 or equivalent. You can't switch models — whatever Notion picked is what you get. [Copy.ai](/reviews/copy-ai) and [Writesonic](/reviews/writesonic) let you choose between GPT-4, Claude, and Gemini per task.
