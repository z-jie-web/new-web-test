# CapCut International Review

CapCut International combines creator-friendly editing, auto captions, subtitle styling, and short-form publishing tools, making it one of the most searched AI-assisted video editors for global social media growth.

## Key Features

- **Auto Captions**
- **Social Editing**

## Our Take

CapCut is the most popular AI video editor for short-form content, and for good reason. The auto captions are fast and accurate, the template library is enormous, and the mobile app is genuinely excellent. For TikTok, Reels, and YouTube Shorts creators, it is hard to beat the speed and convenience. The desktop experience lags behind mobile, and the watermark on free exports is annoying — but at the Pro price point, it is one of the best values in video editing. Privacy-concerned users in certain regions may want to consider alternatives like Descript or VEED.

## Pricing

Free with watermark on exports. Pro plan at $7.99/month for watermark-free exports, premium templates, and cloud storage. Team plans available.
