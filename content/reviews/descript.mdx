## Our Take

Descript is the most innovative video editor for spoken-word content. The core idea — edit video by editing text, and the video follows — sounds gimmicky until you use it, and then you cannot go back. For podcasters, video essayists, tutorial creators, and anyone whose content revolves around speech, Descript eliminates the most tedious parts of editing: finding the right moment on a timeline, cutting filler words, rearranging segments. The 2026 additions — Underlord AI co-editor, AI clip generation for social repurposing, eye contact correction — have made Descript more than a transcript editor. It is now a full content production hub. The Creator plan at $24/month (annual) is the practical entry point. Free tier is a demo with one-time AI credits.

## What Descript Actually Does

Descript's transcript-based editing is the headline feature. Import a video or recording, and Descript auto-generates a transcript. Edit the text — delete words, rearrange paragraphs, copy-paste segments — and the video edits itself accordingly. Click any word to jump to that exact moment in the recording. This paradigm shift makes editing spoken content feel more like editing a document than operating a timeline.

Beyond the transcript editor, Descript includes a full screen recorder with webcam picture-in-picture, making it a one-stop tool for creating tutorials, demos, and video messages. The audio tools are particularly strong: **Studio Sound** applies one-click noise reduction, echo removal, and EQ that makes amateur recordings sound professional. **Filler Word Removal** detects and deletes "um," "uh," "like" across an entire recording in one click — no manual scrubbing required.

The AI features added in 2025-2026 are significant. **Underlord AI** is an agentic co-editor that handles complex tasks via natural language: "remove all silent gaps longer than 3 seconds," "flag sections where I stumble," "suggest B-roll placements for the section about pricing," "generate show notes and chapter markers." **AI Clip Generation** automatically identifies shareable moments and creates short-form social clips with captions, saving hours of manual clipping for repurposing long-form to social. **Eye Contact Correction** uses AI to shift your on-screen gaze so you appear to be looking at the camera even while reading a script. **Overdub** creates an AI voice clone that can type corrections in your own voice — fix a mispronounced word without re-recording.

Transcription accuracy runs ~92-95% for clean single-speaker English audio across 25 supported languages. Accuracy drops to ~80% with 3+ speakers or significant background noise. Regenerate (basic word correction without voice cloning) is available on all plans including Free.

## Who Should Use Descript

**Descript is a strong fit if you:**
- Create spoken-word content: podcasts, tutorials, video essays, webinars, interviews
- Repurpose long-form content into social clips regularly
- Edit on a timeline but find traditional NLEs overkill for speech-heavy content
- Want an all-in-one tool: recording, editing, transcription, subtitles, publishing
- Collaborate with a team on video projects

**Descript is not the best choice if you:**
- Edit music videos, action content, or heavily visual projects (use Premiere or Resolve)
- Need advanced color grading or VFX compositing
- Create short-form social content as primary format (CapCut is more optimized)
- Want a one-time purchase — Descript is subscription-only

## vs CapCut and VEED

Descript's transcript-based editing has no direct equivalent in CapCut or VEED — this is the reason to choose Descript over either. CapCut is better for short-form, template-driven social content with its massive template library. VEED is simpler for quick browser-based caption work. But for long-form spoken content — especially podcasts and tutorials where editing the words is the primary task — Descript's paradigm shift saves more time than any feature in CapCut or VEED.

## FAQ

**Is Descript free?** The Free tier gives 60 minutes of media and 100 one-time AI credits. It is a trial, not a sustainable free plan. For real use, Hobbyist ($24/mo) or Creator ($35/mo) is needed.

**How accurate is the transcription?** ~92-95% for clean single-speaker English. Drops with multiple speakers and background noise. Supports 25 languages.

**Can I clone my voice with Descript?** Yes, via Overdub on Creator plan ($35/mo) and above. The AI voice clone lets you type corrections that sound like you. Ethical considerations apply — only clone your own voice.
