# Descript Review

Descript blends transcript-based editing, subtitle workflows, screen recording, and voice tools into one creator-friendly platform, making it a strong fit for tutorials, podcasts, webinars, and repurposed video content.

## Key Features

- **Transcript Editing**
- **Content Repurposing**

## Our Take

Descript's transcript-based editing is genuinely innovative — edit video by editing text, and the video follows. For podcasters, video essayists, and tutorial creators who work with spoken content, this paradigm shift is transformative. The all-in-one nature (record, edit, transcribe, publish) eliminates tool-switching friction. The learning curve is real — it takes time to unlearn timeline-based editing habits — but once it clicks, you will wonder how you worked any other way. The AI voice cloning feature is powerful but raises ethical considerations that creators should think through before using.

Free plan with limited exports. Hobbyist at $24/month. Business at $40/month. Enterprise plans with SSO and dedicated support.
