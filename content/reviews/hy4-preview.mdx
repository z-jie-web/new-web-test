Is Tencent Hy4 preview good for coding agents? Tencent open-sourced Hunyuan Hy4 preview on August 28, 2026, and the model is a different animal from anything the company has shipped before: 770B total parameters with 49B active, a 1M context window, and DeepSWE that jumped from 28.0 on Hy3 to 64.3. Tencent's internal blind test of 163 experts across 203 engineering tasks rated it above GLM-5.3 and Kimi K3, and hands-on tests show the model fixing its own physics bugs mid-build. It is also explicitly a preview, with known long-thinking and over-verification habits. This review covers the numbers, the hands-on evidence, the pricing, and what a preview label should mean for your decision.

## Key Features

- **Frontier-scale open MoE**: Hy4 preview more than doubles Hy3's size (770B total versus 295B) and doubles active parameters to 49B, with a 1M context window (up from 256K), attention rebuilt from GQA into Gated DSA plus MLA plus IndexCache, four parallel residual streams, and native FP8 precision. This is Tencent's AI-team post-reorg statement model.
- **Productivity-first design**: co-developed with CodeBuddy and WorkBuddy through a co-design process with Tencent's internal software engineering, gaming, finance, and security experts supplying high-quality data. The model is a first-party companion to Tencent's agent products, not a standalone experiment.
- **Coding results (vendor-reported)**: DeepSWE 64.3 (from 28.0 on Hy3), Terminal-Bench 2.1 at 85.4 tying Claude Opus 5 and clearing DeepSeek V4 Pro, Code Arena WebDev ranked ~5th overall and 3rd among open models. The internal 163-expert blind test scored 2.99/4.00 versus GLM-5.3's 2.92 and Kimi K3's 2.94.
- **Hands-on self-correction**: media tests through CodeBuddy show the model checking its own environment before acting, fixing its own JavaScript crashes and color-rendering bugs during a complex frontend build, and iterating a 3D pinball game until physics bugs (ball escaping the table, launch failures) were resolved on its own. A 19-page investment research deck was produced from a single prompt in office mode.
- **Open weights and serving**: Hugging Face, GitHub, ModelScope, and Gitcode hosts; API on Tencent TokenHub and OpenRouter. A recursive self-improvement loop (the model helping optimize its own training, data, evaluation, and operators) and a 31.8% end-to-end throughput gain over baseline round out the release.

## Our Take

Hy4 preview is the model that finally puts Tencent in the China-frontier conversation rather than next to it. The numbers justify the claim: DeepSWE more than doubling to 64.3, Terminal-Bench 2.1 at parity with Claude Opus 5, and an internal blind test that places it marginally above GLM-5.3 and Kimi K3, the two models our own reviews rate as the strongest Chinese coding options of August. The hands-on self-correction behavior (fixing its own physics bugs rather than papering over them) matches what DeepSWE-class scores should feel like in daily agent use.

The caveats are the word "preview" and the word "internal." Tencent itself lists long thinking and over self-verification as known issues on complex tasks: agents will run slower and burn more tokens than the flagship class until a follow-up release lands. The 163-expert blind test is an internal evaluation with no published methodology for independent replication, and the Code Arena and DeepSWE figures need third-party confirmation. The ecosystem is also the youngest of the Chinese majors: fewer hosting providers, less community tooling, and CodeBuddy's free window was two weeks.

The verdict: Hy4 preview is the strongest open-model option from a Chinese lab with full commercial backing, and the first Tencent model we would put in the same sentence as [GLM-5.3](/reviews/glm-5-3) and [Kimi K3](/reviews/kimi-k3). If you need open weights with frontier-adjacent engineering scores, test it now. If you need production predictability, wait for the non-preview release or stick with the closed frontier. ## What Hy4 Preview Does in Practice

Tencent released Hy4 preview on August 28, 2026, per [the company's announcement](https://www.tencent.com/zh-cn/tencent-releases-and-open-sources-tencent-hy4-preview/), the same 48-hour window as GLM-5.3-Flash and Qwen3.8-Flash-Next, and immediately surfaced it in WorkBuddy, CodeBuddy (domestic and international), Yuanbao, and ima. The release included open weights across four hosting communities and same-day multi-chip adaptation covering ten domestic accelerator families.

In daily use through CodeBuddy, the pattern that stands out is self-correction. In one hands-on test, the model was asked to build an Art Deco blog site; it checked the environment first, planned the approach, then found and fixed its own JavaScript crash and accent-color rendering errors before delivering. In another, a 3D pinball game with physics and scoring: the ball escaping the table, the launcher failing, and the flippers missing were all diagnosed and fixed by the model itself after it ran and observed its own output. A separate test had the model generate a playable shooting-game demo through an Unreal Engine 5 MCP connection from pure conversation. These are the behaviors DeepSWE 64.3 predicts, and they are visible in practice. Independent hands-on coverage from [zhidx](https://www.zhidx.com/p/588800.html) confirms the pattern across longer sessions, including the model fixing its own bugs during a 3D open-world demo build.

Office work is the other pillar. The 19-page investment research deck produced from a single prompt (scoring a robotics company across growth, earnings quality, and cash-flow safety) is representative of the WorkBuddy positioning. For engineering teams, the 1M context window and the CodeBuddy integration matter most: repository-scale sessions fit without summarization, and the agent product is first-party rather than community-built.

The rough edges match the preview label. Long, complex tasks show extended thinking and repeated self-verification, which costs time and tokens. The ecosystem is young: OpenRouter and TokenHub serve the API, but third-party hosting options and community integrations are thinner than DeepSeek's or Qwen's. And as a first post-reorg release, the model's roadmap (Tencent says a follow-up Hy4 is coming soon) is the main source of version risk.
