## What Fish Audio Really Does

Fish Audio is a full-stack voice AI platform built around the S2 model family. The core TTS pipeline converts text to speech with sub-150ms latency on server hardware. Voice cloning extracts a speaker embedding from 10-15 seconds of reference audio and applies it to any language the model supports.

The emotion control system is what sets it apart from ElevenLabs. Rather than fiddling with SSML tags or sliders, you write natural language directions directly in the script: `[whispering conspiratorially]`, `[shouting over crowd noise]`, `[sarcastic, eye-rolling tone]`. The model interprets these and adjusts delivery. It works about 93% of the time, the other 7%, the tag is ignored or misinterpreted.

The multi-speaker feature deserves attention. Upload 3 voice samples, write a script with speaker labels, and generate the full dialogue in one pass. Timing, turn-taking, and emotional context carry across speakers. For audiobook production, podcast intros, and game dialogue, this single feature replaces a workflow that previously required multiple tools and manual stitching.

The companion tools fill out the platform: stem splitter for isolating audio elements, sound effect generator from text prompts, and speech-to-text that handles 100+ languages. These are solid but not category-leading, they're useful add-ons, not reasons to choose Fish Audio over alternatives.

## FAQ

**Is Fish Audio really better than ElevenLabs?**
On standardized benchmarks (TTS-Arena), yes, S2-Pro ranks #1 overall. In real-world use, it depends on the voice and language. [ElevenLabs](/reviews/elevenlabs) still sounds slightly more natural for casual English narration. Fish Audio wins on technical content, multi-speaker scenes, and non-English languages.

**How fast is the voice cloning?**
10-15 seconds of reference audio for instant cloning, about 5 minutes for the high-quality mode. Instant clones are usable for testing and demos. Production use (audiobooks, voiceovers) benefits from the longer mode. Both are faster than ElevenLabs' 30-second minimum.

**Can I run Fish Audio models locally?**
Yes. S2-Mini needs 12GB VRAM (RTX 3060 minimum). S2-Pro needs 24GB (RTX 4090 recommended). Runs on Linux, WSL, or Docker. No native macOS support. Model weights are free for non-commercial use; commercial use requires a license.

**What's the catch with pricing?**
UTF-8 byte billing. English text is roughly 1 byte per character. Chinese, Japanese, Arabic, and Hindi use 3-4 bytes per character. A 1,000-word Chinese script costs 3-4x more than the same length in English. If you primarily generate non-Latin scripts, budget accordingly or use the Pro plan with fixed monthly minutes.
