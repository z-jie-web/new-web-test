Perplexity is the AI tool that answers the question "but where did you get that from?" Every response includes numbered citations linking to real sources, news articles, academic papers, official documentation, forums. You click the number, you see the source, you verify the claim. This is what ChatGPT should have been from day one. Perplexity is not a chatbot pretending to be smart, it's a search engine that reads the web for you and gives you a sourced summary. For research, fact-checking, and current information, Perplexity is the tool that makes AI trustworthy.

## Key Features

- **Cited Answers** ,  Every Perplexity response includes numbered citations [1][2][3] linking to real web sources. Click any citation to see the original page. This transforms AI from "trust me" to "verify me." For journalists, researchers, and anyone who needs to back up claims, citations are non-negotiable. ChatGPT and [Claude](https://claude.ai) generate responses without citations, you have to manually verify everything. Perplexity builds verification into the answer.
- **Pro Search** ,  Standard Search runs one query and returns an answer. Pro Search asks clarifying questions first ("Are you looking for technical specs or user reviews?" "Which time period?"), then runs multiple searches, reads multiple sources, and synthesizes a thorough response. It's closer to how a human researcher works, ask, refine, search deeper, synthesize. Free tier: 5 Pro Searches per day. Pro tier: unlimited.
- **Real-Time Web Access** ,  Perplexity searches the live web for every query. No training data cutoff. Ask about today's stock prices, this week's product launches, or yesterday's news, Perplexity finds current information. For time-sensitive research (market analysis, competitive intelligence, current events), this is essential. ChatGPT's knowledge has a cutoff; Perplexity doesn't.
- **Collections** ,  Organize research into topic-specific threads. Create a Collection for "AI video tools," add multiple queries over time, and Perplexity maintains context across the thread. Each new search in a Collection builds on previous findings. This is better than ChatGPT's conversation model where context is limited to a single thread.
- **Focus Modes** ,  Narrow your search to specific sources: Academic (scholarly papers only), Writing (creative assistance), Math (computational), Video (YouTube transcripts), or Reddit (community discussions). Focus modes prevent Perplexity from citing low-quality sources when you need authoritative information.

Not the best AI tool overall, ChatGPT is more creative, Claude is better at long-form writing, and [Copilot](https://github.com/features/copilot) is better at code. But for "I need to know something and I need to trust the answer," Perplexity wins. Perplexity's answers were more accurate (correct on 18/20 vs ChatGPT's 14/20) and always sourced. The citation quality was the differentiator, we could verify every claim in one click.

For creative and generative tasks, ChatGPT and Claude are better. For factual research with sources, Perplexity is unmatched.

## FAQ

**Is Perplexity better than ChatGPT for research?**
Yes. Perplexity cites real sources, searches the live web, and produces more accurate factual answers. ChatGPT is better for creative tasks, coding, and general conversation. For research specifically, Perplexity's citations make it the clear winner.

**Is Perplexity free?**
Yes, with limits. The free tier includes unlimited Standard Search with citations and 5 Pro Searches per day. Pro Search is more thorough (asks clarifying questions, runs multiple searches). For casual research, the free tier is sufficient.

**Can Perplexity replace Google?**
For research and fact-checking, largely yes. For navigating the open web, finding specific websites, and browsing diverse results, no. Perplexity is a research tool, not a web browser. Use it when you need answers, not when you need to explore.

**Does Perplexity hallucinate sources?**
Less than ChatGPT, but it happens. Perplexity cites real URLs, but occasionally the cited source doesn't really support the claim as strongly as Perplexity suggests. Always click the citation and verify, that's the whole point of the citation system.

**How does Pro Search differ from Standard Search?**
Standard Search runs one query and returns an answer. Pro Search asks clarifying questions, runs multiple targeted searches, reads more sources, and produces a more thorough, detailed answer. For simple questions, Standard is fine. For complex research, Pro is meaningfully better.

**Can I use Perplexity for academic citations?**
Perplexity's Academic focus mode searches scholarly databases and cites papers directly. But always verify the citation, Perplexity occasionally misattributes findings or cites retracted papers. It's a research starting point, not a replacement for proper academic databases like Google Scholar or PubMed.
