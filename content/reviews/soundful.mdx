For producers who have tried stem separation on AI music and gotten muddy results, this is the feature that makes Soundful worth paying for.

Iterate through 50 variations before picking the right one with no credit anxiety. The free tier gives you the full generation engine — only download count and licensing restrict you from commercial use.

## What Soundful really does

Soundful uses a template-driven workflow. You pick a genre from 150+ options (EDM, hip-hop, lo-fi, ambient, house, techno, trap, R&B, and more), set the BPM and key, and hit generate. The AI composes a full instrumental track within your project constraints — drums, bass, melody, harmony, FX — with no warping or pitch-shifting needed afterward. Generation takes a few seconds for a three-minute track, with song structure markers (intro, verse, chorus, bridge, outro) preserved.

On the Pro plan, downloading a track gives you the full multitrack package: individual WAV stems, MIDI note data, pre-master and master stereo files, and an Ableton Live .als project file with all tracks on the timeline. The MIDI export lets you replace Soundful's built-in synth sounds with your own VST instruments — the composition is Soundful's, the sound design is yours.

For video editors, Soundful offers a **Filmora integration** for timeline-synced music generation. For developers, there is an **API** (Business plans) for integrating AI music into apps, games, and platforms.

## FAQ

**Can I use Soundful music commercially?**
Yes, on paid plans. Premium gives you a social media commercial license covering YouTube, TikTok, Instagram, and similar platforms with perpetual monetization rights. Pro adds an exclusive license — your generated tracks are only usable by you during the subscription. The free tier is personal use only, no commercial rights.

**Does Soundful generate music with vocals?**
No. Soundful is instrumental-only. If you need songs with vocals, [Suno](/reviews/suno) generates full vocal tracks from text prompts. If you need cinematic scoring with choir elements, [AIVA](/reviews/aiva) handles orchestral arrangements.

**How good is Soundful's stem separation?**
Soundful's stems are generated at the source level — each instrument is synthesized separately rather than separated from a mixed stereo file. This means zero bleed-through between instruments. It is cleaner than [Suno](/reviews/suno)'s 12-stem separation and [Soundraw](/reviews/soundraw)'s stem export. Professional producers will still want final mixing in their own DAW, but the raw stems are genuinely usable.

**What DAWs does Soundful work with?**
Soundful exports standard WAV stems and MIDI files compatible with any DAW: Ableton Live, Logic Pro, FL Studio, Pro Tools, GarageBand, Cubase, Studio One, and Reaper. The .als Ableton project file (Pro plan) opens directly in Ableton Live with song structure markers and individual tracks on the timeline. Non-Ableton users can import WAV stems and MIDI in about 30 seconds.

**What is the maximum track length?**
Three minutes. For longer content, generate multiple tracks and arrange them in a DAW, or loop sections using the MIDI data. For most social media content (15 seconds to 2 minutes), three minutes is more than enough.
