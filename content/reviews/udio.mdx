## Key Features

- **48kHz Stereo Output** — Higher sample rate than Suno's 44.1kHz, with audible improvements in dynamic range, instrument separation, and high-frequency detail. The difference is clearest in acoustic and orchestral music — a jazz ride cymbal has actual shimmer, not a compressed wash. A classical string section has bow texture, not a synth pad approximation.
- **True Waveform Inpainting** — Select any region of the waveform — a single word, a 2-second bar, a transition between sections — and regenerate just that region. The surrounding audio stays untouched. This is a fundamentally different approach from Suno's "generate variations and hope one is better" model. You fix what's broken instead of rolling the dice on a full regeneration.
- **Style Transfer** — Upload a reference track and Udio matches its timbral character — the texture of the instruments, the room ambience, the frequency balance. Not the melody or composition, but the sonic fingerprint. Useful for matching a generated track to the tone of an existing project.
- **Sessions Editor** — A lightweight in-browser editor for arranging 30-second clips into complete songs. Less powerful than Suno Studio (no stem separation), but more precise for section-level editing.
- **Licensed Platform Development (In Progress)** — Udio settled with Universal, Warner, and Merlin and is co-developing a licensed platform with UMG. This is the reason downloads are disabled — Udio is rebuilding on licensed training data rather than fighting fair-use lawsuits. If successful, Udio will have the cleanest legal standing in AI music.

## What Udio Actually Does

Udio is a web-based AI music generator with a section-level editing workflow. Unlike Suno's "generate full song in one go" approach, Udio works in 30-second increments. You generate a section — a verse, a chorus, an intro — and arrange them in the Sessions editor to build a complete track.

This sounds slower than Suno — and it is. A full 3-minute song takes 4-6 generations plus arrangement time, roughly 5-10 minutes total vs Suno's 30 seconds. But the trade-off is control. Each 30-second section is independently editable. Don't like the second verse? Regenerate just that section. Want a different intro? Generate 5 intro variations and pick the best one. Suno's "generate full song and cross fingers" approach is faster; Udio's section-by-section approach is more precise.

**Inpainting** works at the waveform level. You see the audio waveform, select a region with your mouse, describe what should change, and Udio regenerates that region. The transition between the inpainted region and the original audio is seamless — no clicks, no phase issues, no audible seams. We tested this on a 2-second vocal phrase that had awkward pitch correction, and the inpainted replacement blended perfectly.

**Style Transfer** analyzes a reference track and applies its sonic character to your generation. Upload a reference with the guitar tone you want, the room sound you like, or the vocal texture you're after — Udio matches the timbral fingerprint without copying the composition. Results vary by genre. Acoustic and organic styles transfer well. Heavily produced electronic styles are less consistent.
