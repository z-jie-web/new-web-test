Otter.ai is the only major meeting assistant that shows you the transcript as the conversation happens — words appear on screen in real time, no waiting for post-meeting delivery. With 300 free minutes per month and 10 million users, it is the most popular AI note taker. The catch: the Pro plan got worse in 2025, and file upload limits are among the stingiest in the category.

## Key Features

- **Real-Time Live Transcription** — Otter is the only tool among Fireflies, Fathom, and itself that transcribes live during the call. You see words appear as people speak, which means you can catch errors, highlight important points, and search the transcript while the meeting is still running.
- **OtterPilot Auto-Join** — connects to Google or Outlook calendar, automatically joins Zoom, Google Meet, and Teams calls as a participant. You can even skip a meeting entirely and OtterPilot will still show up, take notes, and send you the summary.
- **AI Chat Across Meeting History** — ask questions like "what did we decide about the Q2 launch timeline across the last 4 meetings?" and get answers with source timestamps. Similar to Fireflies' AskFred but with real-time awareness during live meetings.
- **Automated Summaries and Action Items** — post-meeting summary with keywords, outline, and extracted action items. Generates follow-up email drafts you can send to participants with one click.
- **Collaborative Transcript Editing** — multiple people can view, edit, highlight, and comment on a transcript in real time. Useful for team-wide meeting documentation.
- **Mobile Apps for In-Person Recording** — iOS and Android apps let you record in-person conversations by placing your phone near the speakers. The transcript syncs to your web account.

## Our Take

Otter.ai solves the problem of "what just happened in that meeting?" better than anyone — if you value seeing the transcript as it forms. That real-time feedback loop changes how you use the tool: you can catch a mistranscribed number during the call, highlight a decision the moment it is made, and leave the meeting knowing the notes are already done.

The Pro plan downgrade stings. Otter used to offer 6,000 minutes per month on Pro. In 2025, they cut it to 1,200 minutes while keeping the price roughly the same ($8.33/month on annual billing). That is a 5x reduction in value. Users on Reddit are not quiet about it: "Otter.ai used to be a no-brainer at $8. Now it feels like they are squeezing the low-tier users to push everyone to Business." The free tier, however, remains the most generous entry point in the category — 300 minutes with no credit card is genuinely useful for light users.

The file upload situation is the other pain point. Free users get 3 uploads — lifetime. Pro users get 10 uploads per month. If you record interviews on a dictaphone or need to transcribe pre-recorded audio files, Otter is the wrong tool. Fireflies charges extra for uploaded file transcription too, but Otter's caps are more restrictive.

One thing Otter does that nobody else does well: it works across departments. Fireflies and Fathom are built for sales. Otter is built for anyone who has meetings — product teams, journalists, students, operations, executives. The collaborative editing and shared workspace features make it the most "general purpose" of the three big meeting assistants.

## What Otter.ai Actually Does

Otter.ai is a cloud-based meeting assistant. You connect your calendar, and OtterPilot automatically joins every scheduled meeting as a named participant ("Otter.ai Notetaker"). During the call, it transcribes the conversation in real time with speaker labels. After the call, it generates a structured summary with keywords, an outline, and extracted action items — delivered to your email and available in the web app within 2-3 minutes of the call ending.

The real-time transcription is Otter's defining feature. Fireflies and Fathom record audio and deliver transcripts after the meeting ends. Otter shows you the words as they are spoken. This matters for three reasons: you can search the transcript during the call to find a point that was made 10 minutes ago, you can catch and correct mistranscriptions immediately, and you can leave the meeting knowing the notes are complete — no "I'll clean this up later" mental load.

**Otter AI Chat** is the cross-meeting search feature. It is trained on your entire meeting history and can answer questions like "summarize every discussion about the pricing page redesign" or "what objections did the client raise during our last two calls?" It timestamps its answers and links back to the original transcripts. Think of it as ChatGPT for your meeting archive.

**The mobile experience** is surprisingly good. The iOS and Android apps can record in-person conversations using the phone's microphone. The transcript appears in real time on your screen and syncs to your web account. For journalists doing in-person interviews or students recording lectures, this is the killer use case — no laptop needed, no bot participant required, just your phone on the table.

**Collaboration features** set Otter apart from sales-focused alternatives. Multiple team members can view, edit, highlight, and comment on the same transcript simultaneously. You can tag teammates in comments, assign action items, and build a shared meeting knowledge base that is searchable by keyword, date, or participant. This makes Otter the pick for cross-functional teams that want meeting documentation, not just sales call logs.

## Who Should Use Otter.ai

**Otter.ai is a strong fit if you:**
- Need real-time transcription visible during the call (no other big-three tool does this)
- Are a journalist, student, or researcher recording interviews and lectures on mobile
- Work across departments and want shared, collaborative meeting documentation
- Want the most generous free tier in the category (300 min, no credit card)
- Use meetings for general collaboration rather than structured sales calls

**Otter.ai is not the best choice if you:**
- Transcribe pre-recorded audio files regularly (file upload limits are restrictive)
- Need deep CRM integration with Salesforce or HubSpot (use [Fireflies.ai](/reviews/fireflies-ai))
- Want unlimited free transcription for a single user (use [Fathom](/reviews/fathom))
- Need offline transcription with zero cloud dependency (use [WhisperX](/reviews/whisperx))
- Run a structured sales process with call scoring and deal tracking (Fireflies is purpose-built for this)
- Have strict HIPAA compliance requirements on a budget (Enterprise only)

## vs Fireflies.ai, Fathom, and WhisperX

[Fireflies.ai](/reviews/fireflies-ai) wins on CRM integration and conversation intelligence — if you use Salesforce or HubSpot daily, Fireflies auto-logs call notes to contact records and has a deeper feature set for sales coaching. Otter wins on real-time transcription and cross-department usability. Fireflies is the sales team pick; Otter is the everyone-else pick.

[Fathom](/reviews/fathom) has the strongest free tier in the category — unlimited recordings for one user, no time cap. If you are a solo creator or individual contributor who just wants to stop taking notes, Fathom's free plan beats Otter's 300-minute cap. Otter's paid plans are cheaper ($8.33 vs $19/month) and include real-time transcription, which Fathom does not offer.

[WhisperX](/reviews/whisperx) is the open-source option for developers who need offline transcription with zero cloud costs. It requires a GPU and command-line setup — not a meeting tool, but if your privacy requirements demand that audio never leaves your machine, WhisperX is the only option that guarantees it.

## FAQ

**Is Otter.ai really free?** Yes. The Basic plan gives you 300 minutes of transcription per month with no credit card required. Meetings are capped at 30 minutes each. You get 3 file uploads — lifetime. For light users doing a few short meetings per week, the free tier is genuinely usable. Heavy meeting users will outgrow it within a week.

**How accurate is Otter.ai transcription?** ~95% for clear single-speaker English in quiet environments. Accuracy drops with heavy accents, overlapping speech, background noise, or technical terminology. Real-time transcription is slightly less accurate than post-meeting processing, but the difference is marginal. There is no human review option.

**Does OtterPilot work if I skip the meeting?** Yes. OtterPilot can join meetings even when you are not present. It records, transcribes, and sends you the summary after. You can configure which meetings it auto-joins and which it skips in your calendar settings.

**Can Otter.ai transcribe languages other than English?** Yes. Otter supports English (US and UK), Spanish, and French. This is significantly fewer languages than Fireflies (60+) or WhisperX (99+). If multilingual transcription is a priority, Otter is not the best pick.

**Does Otter.ai work on mobile?** Yes. iOS and Android apps are available. You can record in-person conversations by placing your phone near the speakers — no bot participant required. The mobile experience is one of Otter's strongest features.

**What happens to my data if I cancel?** Your existing transcripts remain accessible in read-only mode. The bot stops joining new meetings immediately. OtterPilot and AI Chat are disabled. You can export transcripts before cancellation.

**Does Otter.ai have an API?** No. Otter had a public API from 2019 to 2022, but it was shut down. There is no way to programmatically access your transcripts. If you need API access, consider AssemblyAI or Deepgram for raw transcription, or Fireflies for meeting-specific workflows.
