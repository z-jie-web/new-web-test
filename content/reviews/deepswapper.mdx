## What DeepSwapper Actually Does

DeepSwapper is a web-based face swap tool. You upload a source face photo and a target photo, click swap, and the tool replaces the face in the target image with the source face. The entire process happens in the browser with no account required.

The output quality depends entirely on input quality. Well-lit, front-facing photos with similar angles produce decent results. Side profiles, low-resolution images, or mismatched lighting produce obvious artifacts. There are no adjustment controls — no blending strength, no mask refinement, no enhancement pass. What you get is the base model's best attempt, which is adequate for casual use but not for anything you would publish professionally.

DeepSwapper is photo-only — no video face swap, no GIF support, no batch processing. The tool is best understood as a free, frictionless entry point to face swap technology rather than a production tool.

## vs Reface and FaceFusion

DeepSwapper is the simplest and most accessible option — free, no download, no signup, browser-based. Reface offers a far better mobile experience with a massive template library updated daily, but requires app installation and has watermarks on the free tier. FaceFusion offers professional-grade quality with HyperSwap 256px models, real-time streaming, and full creative control, but requires Python, a GPU, and technical setup. DeepSwapper occupies the ultra-low-friction niche between them: it is the tool you use when someone says "show me what face swap can do" and you want to demonstrate in 10 seconds without installing anything.

For most users, DeepSwapper is an entry point, not a destination. Start here to understand face swap technology. Graduate to Reface if you want mobile convenience and templates. Graduate to FaceFusion if you want professional results.
