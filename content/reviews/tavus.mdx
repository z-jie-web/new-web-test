## What Tavus Really Does

Tavus operates on three layers, each corresponding to a different API product.

**Replica API, Personalized Video at Scale.** You record a base video once. Tavus extracts your face, voice, and delivery style into a digital replica. You write scripts with variable placeholders. Tavus generates unique videos where your replica delivers the script with each recipient's actual name, company, and custom details inserted naturally, not robotically spliced, but spoken as part of the original delivery. For a sales team sending 500 outreach videos, this turns a week of manual recording into an afternoon of script writing.

**Lip-Sync API, Dubbing and Localization.** Provide an audio track in any of 30+ languages, and Tavus animates your replica's mouth to match. The lip-sync accuracy is good enough for professional use, not YouTube-deepfake perfect, but better than the misaligned mouth movements you see on most AI-dubbed content. Combined with a TTS provider like ElevenLabs, you can create a single base video and localize it into 30 languages without re-recording.

**Conversational Video Interface, Real-Time AI Avatars.** This is the most technically ambitious layer. A user starts a video call with your AI replica. Behind the scenes, an LLM generates responses, Tavus renders the replica speaking those responses in real-time, and the Raven-1 model manages turn-taking, interruptions, and conversation flow. Sub-second latency means the interaction feels like a slightly laggy Zoom call rather than a buffering video. Persona Memories (RAG-based) give the avatar contextual awareness, it remembers what was discussed earlier in the conversation. Function Calling lets the avatar trigger backend actions: schedule a meeting, look up an order status, escalate to a human agent.

## FAQ

**How good is the face and voice cloning quality?**
Face cloning is excellent, Phoenix-4 captures micro-expressions, head movements, and eye contact better than any other B2B avatar platform we've tested. Voice cloning is good but not great, it sounds like you, but the emotional range is limited. For sales outreach, the quality is more than sufficient. For branded video content where voice performance matters, pair Tavus with [ElevenLabs](/reviews/elevenlabs) professional voice cloning.

**How long does it take to create a replica?**
2 minutes of training footage, about 10-15 minutes of processing time. You record yourself talking naturally to a camera in good, even lighting. No studio, no green screen, no professional equipment needed. A laptop webcam in a well-lit room works.

**What's the real cost for a sales team of 5?**
At the Growth tier ($300-600/month), plus per-minute streaming costs for any conversational video usage. Expect $500-1,000/month for a team of 5 sending a few hundred personalized videos per week. The ROI math: if those videos generate even 2-3 additional closed deals per month, the tool pays for itself many times over.

**Is Tavus safe from a deepfake perspective?**
Tavus requires explicit on-camera consent during replica creation, you cannot create a replica of someone else from uploaded footage. All replicas are tied to the account that created them. The platform is SOC 2, GDPR, and HIPAA compliant. That said, the technology is inherently dual-use, the safeguards are procedural and contractual, not technical watermarks like Google's SynthID.
