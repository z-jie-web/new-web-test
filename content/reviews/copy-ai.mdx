## What Copy.ai Actually Does

Copy.ai is a browser-based content generation and workflow automation platform. You start in one of two modes: direct generation or workflow builder.

**Direct generation** works like any AI writer — pick a template (Blog Post, Social Caption, Email, Ad Copy), describe what you want, and Copy.ai generates it. You can switch between GPT-4, Claude, and Gemini per generation to compare output quality. The editor lets you refine, shorten, expand, or change tone with one-click buttons.

**GTM Workflows** are the differentiator. You build automated pipelines: a trigger (manual input, Zapier event, scheduled time) fires content generation across multiple templates. Example: "New blog published" trigger → generates 3 tweet variations, 2 LinkedIn post drafts, 5 email subject lines, and 1 Instagram caption. Everything lands in your Copy.ai inbox, ready to copy-paste or schedule.

The workflow builder has a visual drag-and-drop interface, but it takes 2-3 hours of setup to build your first working pipeline. Once configured, it runs with one click or automatically via Zapier triggers.

**Integrations** via Zapier connect Copy.ai to 2,000+ apps. A HubSpot "new contact" trigger → Copy.ai generates a personalized follow-up email → Gmail sends it. A Google Sheets "new row" trigger → Copy.ai generates social captions → Buffer schedules them. The value is in removing manual copy-paste between tools.
