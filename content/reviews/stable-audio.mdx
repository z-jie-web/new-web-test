## Key Features

- **Stems and Loops, Not Full Songs** — Stable Audio generates instrumental building blocks: drum loops, bass lines, melody stems, chord progressions, ambient pads. You specify genre, mood, BPM, and instruments. Stable Audio outputs a clean loop or stem, ready to drop into Ableton, Logic, or FL Studio. This is fundamentally different from Suno and Udio, which generate complete songs with vocals and arrangement. Stable Audio is for producers who want raw materials, not finished tracks.
- **Variable Length Generation (10s to 3min)** — Need a 16-bar drum loop? Generate exactly 30 seconds. Need a 2-minute ambient pad for a video intro? Generate exactly 120 seconds. Suno and Udio work in fixed 30-second or full-song increments. Stable Audio lets you specify exact durations, which is critical for fitting music to video timelines or DAW projects.
- **Royalty-Free Commercial License** — Stable Audio is trained on licensed music from the AudioSparx library, not scraped major-label recordings. Paid tiers ($11.99/month Professional and up) grant full commercial rights with no attribution required. This is the clearest legal standing in AI music — no active lawsuits, no copyright ambiguity. Suno and Udio face major-label litigation. Stable Audio sidesteps it entirely.
- **Genre and Mood Control** — Text prompts like "lo-fi hip-hop drum loop, 90 BPM, dusty vinyl texture" or "cinematic orchestral strings, epic, 120 BPM, legato" produce genre-accurate results. Stable Audio understands production terminology (attack, release, reverb, sidechain) better than Suno, which is trained on song-level descriptions.
- **44.1kHz Stereo Output** — Standard sample rate for music production. Quality is good but not exceptional — Udio's 48kHz output has more headroom and dynamic range. Stable Audio's output is clean and usable, but audio engineers will notice it's not mastering-grade.

## What Stable Audio Actually Does

Stable Audio is a web-based AI music generator with a single workflow: text prompt → audio file.

You start with a text prompt describing what you want:
- Genre and style ("lo-fi hip-hop," "cinematic orchestral," "deep house")
- Instruments ("808 drums," "analog bass," "mellotron strings")
- Mood and texture ("chill," "aggressive," "dreamy," "gritty")
- Technical details (BPM, key, duration)

Example prompt: "Ambient techno loop, 128 BPM, deep sub-bass, analog pads, minimal kick, 60 seconds"

Stable Audio generates a stereo audio file (44.1kHz WAV or MP3) matching your prompt. Generation takes 10-30 seconds. You can regenerate with the same prompt to get variations, or tweak the prompt to refine the output.

**No built-in editor.** No stem separation. No arrangement tools. Stable Audio outputs raw audio files — you download them and import into your DAW. This is intentional. Stable Audio is a generator, not a production suite. Suno Studio (Suno's built-in DAW) tries to be both. Stable Audio stays in its lane.

**Commercial use workflow**: Generate a loop, download the WAV, import into Ableton/Logic/FL Studio, layer with your own instruments, mix, master, release. Stable Audio is one ingredient in your production, not the whole meal.
