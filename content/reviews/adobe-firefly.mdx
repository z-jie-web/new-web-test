## What Adobe Firefly Actually Does

Firefly is three things in one: a standalone web app (firefly.adobe.com), a set of AI features embedded in Creative Cloud apps, and an API for enterprise customers.

**Generative Fill (Photoshop)**: This is Firefly's killer feature. Select any region in a Photoshop document, type what you want ("add a coffee cup on the desk," "extend the sky upward," "remove the person in the background"), and Firefly fills it in seamlessly. It matches lighting, perspective, depth of field, and color grading automatically. For photo retouchers and compositing artists, this single feature saves hours per project. No other AI image tool integrates this deeply into a professional editing workflow.

**Generative Expand**: Crop an image wider or taller than the original, and Firefly fills in the new areas realistically. This is transformative for repurposing assets — a vertical product shot can become a horizontal hero banner without a reshoot. Available in Photoshop and as a standalone feature.

**Text to Image (Web App)**: The standalone Firefly web app generates images from text prompts. Quality is good but not great — competent for social media graphics, blog illustrations, and quick concept mockups, but not at Midjourney's level for high-stakes creative work. The web app includes style reference (upload an image and match its look), compositional reference, and aspect ratio controls from square to widescreen.

**Text Effects and Vector Recoloring (Illustrator)**: Firefly generates stylized text effects (metallic, neon, 3D, etc.) from descriptions, and can recolor entire vector designs with a single prompt. These features are genuinely useful for designers but have a narrower audience.

## Commercial Safety — The Real Differentiator

Every other AI image generator is in legal gray territory. Did Stability AI have permission to train on every image in LAION-5B? Are Midjourney's training images properly licensed? Multiple class-action lawsuits are still making their way through courts. Enterprise legal teams do not like uncertainty.

Adobe's training data is clean — 100% Adobe Stock images (where contributors are compensated), openly licensed content, and public domain works. Adobe offers IP indemnification for Firefly-generated images, meaning if a legal claim arises from Firefly output, Adobe covers it. No other AI image generator offers this. For any business where legal risk is a real consideration, Firefly's commercial safety is worth the quality trade-off.

## Speed and Workflow

Generation is fast — typically 5-15 seconds per image on the web app. Photoshop integration means you never leave your editing workspace: select, prompt, generate, refine, all within the Photoshop interface. This is the most frictionless AI image workflow for existing Adobe users.

The web app is less polished. It lacks the canvas editing tools that Midjourney's web editor offers — no inpainting, no region variations, no pan/zoom. It is essentially a prompt box with style controls. For serious creative work, you will use Firefly inside Photoshop, not in the browser.

## FAQ

**Is Adobe Firefly free?** There is a free tier with 25 credits/month. Output has a content credential watermark. Paid plans start at $4.99/month for watermark-free output.

**Can Adobe Firefly images be used commercially?** Yes. Unlike most AI image generators, Firefly includes IP indemnification for commercial use — Adobe covers legal claims arising from Firefly-generated content.

**Is Firefly as good as Midjourney?** No, for pure aesthetic quality. Midjourney produces more visually striking and artistically sophisticated images. Firefly's advantage is legal safety and Creative Cloud integration, not image quality.

**How does Generative Fill work?** Select any area in Photoshop, type what you want added or removed, and Firefly generates it. It automatically matches lighting, perspective, and color grading of the surrounding image.
