Gamma is an AI-native presentation and document platform that ditches the traditional slide canvas for a flexible card-based layout. Instead of dragging boxes around a static slide, you describe what you want and Gamma builds it — text, images, structure, and all.

## Key Features

- **AI Deck Generation** — Type a topic or paste an outline, and Gamma produces a complete multi-slide presentation with text, layout, and images in under 2 minutes
- **Card-Based Layout** — Replaces the rigid slide metaphor with nestable, scrollable cards that work better for web-native sharing
- **AI Image Generation** — Built-in DALL·E-style image generation directly inside cards, no need to leave the editor
- **One-Click Restyle** — Change the entire visual theme of a deck with one click; layouts, fonts, and colors update globally
- **Real-Time Collaboration** — Multiple editors, comments, and live cursors, similar to Google Docs
- **Embed and Share** — Decks are web-native pages that can be embedded anywhere, not just downloaded as files

## Our Take

Gamma occupies a distinct niche in the AI presentation space: it is the fastest path from idea to presentable deck. Where [Beautiful.ai](/reviews/beautiful-ai) enforces strict design rules and PowerPoint demands manual labor, Gamma sits in the middle. You get AI speed without sacrificing editability.

The card-based layout is the real differentiator. Traditional slides force a one-screen-at-a-time mental model. Gamma's cards are more like sections of a web page, nestable and scrollable. This works exceptionally well for web-native sharing (send a link, not a .pptx attachment) but creates friction when you need to export to PowerPoint for a board meeting.

For startup founders preparing investor pitch decks, Gamma's AI generation saves 4-6 hours of manual layout work per deck. The "describe your deck" prompt produces a surprisingly coherent first draft. Not perfect, but a strong starting point that needs content refinement rather than structural rebuilding.

The main limitation is brand control. If your company has strict design guidelines (specific fonts, locked color palettes, logo placement rules), Gamma's free-form AI approach fights against those constraints. Enterprise teams with rigid brand systems will find Beautiful.ai or Pitch.com more suitable for compliance-heavy environments.

## What Gamma Does Best

Gamma's core workflow is prompt-to-deck. You type something like "10-slide pitch deck for an AI tutoring startup targeting K-12 students" and the system generates a complete outline with card layouts, placeholder text, and AI-generated images. The output is not presentation-ready, but it is 70% there — enough to skip the blank-canvas problem entirely.

The AI image generation is integrated directly into the card editor. Click an image placeholder, describe what you want, and Gamma generates it in-place. No switching to [Midjourney](/reviews/midjourney), downloading, cropping, and re-uploading. This saves 15-20 minutes per deck for image-heavy presentations.

The restyle engine is another standout. One click changes the entire visual language: fonts, colors, card shapes, background textures. For teams producing multiple deck variants (one for investors, one for customers, one for partners), restyle lets you create three versions from one content base in minutes.

Collaboration works like Google Docs: real-time cursors, inline comments, and suggestion mode. Unlike PowerPoint's co-authoring (which still operates on a file-based model), Gamma's web-native architecture means no "who has the latest version" problems.

If you're also creating video content alongside presentations, tools like [Runway Gen-3](/reviews/runway-gen-3) can generate B-roll footage, while [VEED](/reviews/veed) handles quick video edits with auto-captions for social promotion.

## Pricing Breakdown

Gamma uses a credit-based AI system combined with traditional subscription tiers. Credits are consumed when generating new content with AI; manual editing does not cost credits.

| Plan | Monthly Price | AI Credits/Month | Key Features |
|------|-------------|-----------------|--------------|
| Free | $0 | 400 credits (~10 decks) | Basic AI generation, Gamma branding, unlimited editors |
| Plus | $10 | Unlimited AI | Remove Gamma branding, custom domains, PDF/PPTX export |
| Pro | $20 | Unlimited AI | Advanced analytics, priority support, SSO, enhanced brand kit |

Annual billing saves about 20% across all plans. The Plus plan at $10/month is the practical entry point for anyone producing decks for external audiences, because it removes the Gamma branding and enables export.

For comparison, Beautiful.ai starts at $12/month (Pro) and Pitch.com at $16.67/month (Pro). Gamma undercuts both on price while offering more generous AI features on the free tier. The 400 free credits are enough to evaluate the tool thoroughly before committing.

## Who Should Use Gamma

**Gamma is a strong fit if you:**
- Build pitch decks, sales presentations, or marketing decks regularly
- Need to go from idea to presentable deck in under 30 minutes
- Work in a startup or small team without a dedicated designer
- Share decks primarily via web links rather than file attachments
- Want AI to handle layout and image selection so you can focus on content

**Gamma is not the best choice if you:**
- Work in an enterprise with strict brand guidelines and design system enforcement
- Need pixel-perfect PowerPoint compatibility for board meetings
- Require offline editing capabilities (Gamma is cloud-only)
- Produce highly technical presentations with complex diagrams (tools like Lucidchart or Miro integrate better)

## FAQ

**Is Gamma free to use?** Yes. The free tier includes 400 AI credits (enough for roughly 10 decks), unlimited editors, and full editing capabilities. The main limitation is Gamma branding on shared decks.

**How does Gamma compare to PowerPoint?** Gamma is faster for creating new decks from scratch (AI generates structure and layout), but PowerPoint is better for complex, data-heavy slides with embedded charts and strict formatting requirements. Many users create in Gamma and export to PowerPoint for final delivery.

**Can I use Gamma for client-facing presentations?** Yes, on the Plus plan ($10/month) or above. The free tier includes Gamma branding, which may not be suitable for professional client work. The Plus and Pro plans remove branding and enable custom domains.

**Does Gamma work for non-presentation documents?** Yes. Gamma also supports docs (long-form text documents), webpages, and one-pagers using the same card-based system. It is a general-purpose content creation tool, not just a slide deck builder.
