Soundraw is the AI music generator that skips the text prompt entirely. Instead of typing "upbeat lo-fi hip-hop beat with jazzy piano," you click through menus: genre → mood → tempo → instruments. The result is a beat that matches your selections in about 10 seconds. It's faster than [Suno](/reviews/suno)'s text-to-song approach for content creators who know what they want but can't describe it in words. The trade-off: Soundraw only makes beats. No vocals, no full songs, no orchestral compositions. If you need a background beat for a YouTube video or podcast, Soundraw is purpose-built for exactly that job.

## Key Features

- **Mood-Based Generation** ,  Select from 15+ genres (hip-hop, lo-fi, pop, electronic, trap, cinematic, etc.), choose a mood (energetic, chill, dark, happy, dramatic), set tempo (slow to fast), and pick instruments (piano, guitar, synths, drums). Soundraw generates a beat matching your parameters in ~10 seconds. No prompt engineering required. This is faster and more predictable than Suno's text-based approach for creators who know their creative direction.
- **STEM Downloads** ,  Export individual tracks: drums, bass, melody, synths, FX. Load them into Ableton, Logic Pro, FL Studio, or any DAW for mixing, layering, and customization. This is what separates Soundraw from one-shot generators, you get production-ready stems, not just a stereo mix. Producers use STEM exports to replace the AI bass with their own recording, add live instruments over the AI drums, or remix the melody with different effects.
- **Beat-Maker (Song Structure Editor)** ,  Arrange your beat by adding, removing, and reordering sections: intro, verse, pre-chorus, chorus, bridge, outro. Adjust section lengths. Control the energy arc of the track. This gives you compositional control that [Suno](/reviews/suno) and [Udio](/reviews/udio) don't offer, those tools generate a complete song structure that you accept or regenerate. Soundraw lets you design the structure yourself.
- **Royalty-Free Commercial License** ,  All paid plans include full commercial rights. Use generated beats in YouTube videos, podcasts, ads, films, games, and social media without attribution or royalty payments. Soundraw's training data is original compositions, not scraped music, there's no copyright ambiguity like the lawsuits facing [Suno](/reviews/suno) and [Udio](/reviews/udio). See [YouTube's music licensing policies](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/3309399) for details on using royalty-free content.
- **Unlimited Downloads (Paid Plans)** ,  Generate as many beats as you want and download unlimited tracks. Unlike per-credit systems (Suno's 50 credits/day on free tier), Soundraw's paid plans let you iterate freely without worrying about burning credits.

## Our Take

Soundraw does one thing and does it well: generates royalty-free beats for content creators. It's not trying to be [Suno](/reviews/suno) (full songs with vocals) or [Udio](/reviews/udio) (audiophile-quality instrumentals) or [AIVA](/reviews/aiva) (orchestral compositions). It's a beat machine. You pick the parameters, it makes the beat, you download the stems, you use it.

The quality is solid but not exceptional. Soundraw's beats sound professional, clean mixes, decent dynamics, appropriate genre conventions. A lo-fi beat sounds like a lo-fi beat. A trap beat has the 808s and hi-hats you'd expect. But after generating 50+ tracks, you start hearing patterns: the same chord progressions, similar drum fills, predictable arrangements. It's polished formulaic, great for background music, not for tracks where the music is the main attraction.

Where Soundraw genuinely excels is the workflow. The mood-based interface is faster than text prompts for creators who know their direction. The STEM export means you can really use the output in professional production. The Beat-Maker gives you structural control that other tools don't offer. If you produce YouTube content and need 5 background beats per week, Soundraw's workflow saves hours compared to browsing stock music libraries or generating and re-generating on Suno.

## What Soundraw Really Does

Soundraw's generation flow is menu-driven. You start by selecting **genre** (hip-hop, lo-fi, pop, electronic, trap, cinematic, R&B, reggae, rock, jazz, funk, and more). Then **mood** (energetic, uplifting, chill, dark, romantic, dramatic, mysterious). Then **tempo** (60-180 BPM with a slider). Then **instruments** (drums, bass, guitar, piano, synths, strings, brass, and more).

Hit generate. Soundraw produces 5-10 beat variations in about 10 seconds. Preview them. Pick the one closest to your vision. Open it in the Beat-Maker to arrange sections, add an intro, extend the chorus, remove the bridge, adjust lengths. Export as MP3, WAV, or individual STEMs.

The **customization depth** is Soundraw's differentiator. After generation, you can swap individual instruments, replace the piano with a guitar, change the bass line, adjust the drum pattern. You can't do this with [Suno](/reviews/suno) or [Udio](/reviews/udio) ,  those tools generate a complete mix and you either accept it or regenerate the whole thing.

The **Beat-Maker** works like a simplified DAW timeline. You see song sections as blocks. Drag to reorder. Click to add or remove sections. Resize to change section lengths. It's not as powerful as Ableton, but it's faster for arranging a 2-minute YouTube background track.

## FAQ

**Can I use Soundraw beats on YouTube without copyright strikes?**
Yes. All paid plans include royalty-free commercial license. Soundraw generates original beats (not samples from existing songs), so there's no Content ID match risk. You can monetize YouTube videos with Soundraw beats without attribution or royalty payments.

**Does Soundraw generate vocals or singing?**
No. Soundraw is instrumental-only. It generates beats, loops, and instrumentals. If you need songs with vocals, [Suno](/reviews/suno) is the better choice, it generates complete songs with AI vocals from text prompts.

**How does Soundraw compare to stock music libraries?**
Soundraw generates unique beats on demand. Stock music libraries (Epidemic Sound, Artlist) offer pre-made tracks that other creators may also use. The advantage of Soundraw: your beat is unique, no other creator will have the same track. The advantage of stock libraries: human-composed music with more emotional depth and nuance.

**Can I edit Soundraw beats in my DAW?**
Yes, on the Creator plan ($29.99/month). STEM downloads export individual tracks (drums, bass, melody, synths) that you import into Ableton, Logic Pro, FL Studio, or any DAW. Replace the AI bass with your own recording, add effects, layer live instruments, and remix the beat however you want.

**Is Soundraw better than Suno for background music?**
For content creators who want predictable, genre-specific background beats: Soundraw is better. The mood-based interface gives you more control over the output, and STEM downloads let you customize in a DAW. For creators who want variety and are willing to experiment with text prompts: Suno generates more diverse output at a lower price.

**Is there a free version of Soundraw?**
There's a free tier that lets you generate and preview beats. But you cannot download anything without a paid plan. It's a demo, not a usable free tier. If you need free AI music, [Suno](/reviews/suno)'s free tier (50 credits/day) is more generous.
