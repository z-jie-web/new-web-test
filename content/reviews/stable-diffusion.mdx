## What Stable Diffusion Really Does

Stable Diffusion is a family of latent diffusion models that generate images from text prompts. The current flagship is SD4, released in early 2026, with significant improvements in prompt adherence, text rendering, and anatomical accuracy.

Unlike Midjourney or DALL-E, you do not send prompts to an API and receive images back. You run the model yourself, on your GPU, on a cloud instance, or through a third-party service. This changes everything about the workflow:

**Local Generation**: Run SD4 on an RTX 3060 or better and generate unlimited images with zero cost per image, zero content moderation, and zero privacy concerns. A 512x512 image takes 2-5 seconds on an RTX 4090. This is a massive advantage for high-volume use cases, game studios generating thousands of texture variations per day, e-commerce sites creating product imagery at scale, researchers training on custom datasets.

**Custom Models and LoRAs**: The Civitai community hosts over 100,000 fine-tuned models and LoRA (Low-Rank Adaptation) files. Want a model trained exclusively on Studio Ghibli frames? Anime character designs? Photorealistic product renders? There is a fine-tuned checkpoint for it. A LoRA file is typically 10-200MB and can be loaded on top of any base model to add specific styles, characters, or concepts, no full retraining needed.

**ControlNet**: This is the killer feature that no closed-source competitor offers. ControlNet adds spatial conditioning layers to Stable Diffusion, letting you control image composition with: Canny edge maps (draw edges and the model fills them in), depth maps (control 3D spatial relationships), pose skeletons (exact human pose control, essential for character design), normal maps (surface detail and lighting direction), and segmentation maps (what goes where in the scene). A designer can sketch a rough wireframe, and ControlNet ensures the final generation matches the intended composition exactly. No amount of prompt engineering achieves this level of spatial precision.

**Interfaces**: Automatic1111 WebUI is the most popular, a Gradio-based web interface with every slider and setting exposed. ComfyUI uses a node-based workflow similar to Blender's shader editor, favored by professionals who need reproducible pipelines. Fooocus simplifies the experience to Midjourney-like simplicity while running locally. InvokeAI offers a polished canvas editor with layer-based workflow.

## Image Quality

SD4 closes the gap with Midjourney considerably. Prompt understanding is now much better, complex multi-subject scenes with spatial relationships ("a cat sitting on a table, a dog under the table, a window behind both") render correctly more often than not. Text rendering within images, a historic weakness, is now usable for short phrases (1-5 words reliably).

The quality ceiling depends heavily on which model you use. A well-curated fine-tuned checkpoint from Civitai can produce images that rival Midjourney for specific styles. The base SD4 model is competent but not exceptional, it is a foundation, not a finished product. The community is the product.

## Who Should Use Stable Diffusion

**Stable Diffusion is a strong fit if you:**
- Need unlimited generation volume without per-image costs
- Require API access or programmatic control
- Want to train custom models on your own dataset
- Have strict privacy or content compliance requirements
- Need precise spatial control (ControlNet) for professional work

**Stable Diffusion is not the best choice if you:**
- Want the best out-of-the-box aesthetic quality with zero setup (use Midjourney)
- Need photorealistic output without complex prompting (use DALL-E 3)
- Are not technical and do not want to learn node-based workflows
- Only generate images occasionally, the setup cost is not worth it

## vs Midjourney and DALL-E

Stable Diffusion's open-source nature is its moat. The community ecosystem (Civitai, Automatic1111, ComfyUI, ControlNet, thousands of LoRAs) is something no closed-source product can replicate. For aesthetic quality out of the box, Midjourney still leads. For photorealism and prompt simplicity, DALL-E 3 is better. For designers producing logos, icons, and brand illustrations, [Recraft](/reviews/recraft) offers native SVG vector generation that Stable Diffusion requires external tracing tools to approximate. For flexibility, control, and ecosystem depth, Stable Diffusion is in a category of its own. [Flux](/reviews/flux), built by the same founding engineers who left Stability AI, is the spiritual successor, better photorealism with the FLUX.2 family and open-weight klein 4B under Apache 2.0.

## FAQ

**Is Stable Diffusion really free?** Yes. The model weights are free to download and use locally. You only pay for hardware (GPU, electricity) or cloud hosting.

**What GPU do I need?** Minimum 8GB VRAM (RTX 3060 or better). 12GB+ is comfortable. 24GB (RTX 3090/4090) for training custom models.

**Can I use Stable Diffusion for commercial projects?** Yes. Stability AI's license permits commercial use of SD3.5 and SD4 outputs. Some community models have their own licenses, check before using commercially.

**SD4 vs SDXL vs SD3.5, which should I use?** SD4 is the current best for general use. SDXL remains popular for community model compatibility. SD3.5 is a good balance if SD4 is too heavy for your GPU.
