## What AIVA Actually Does

AIVA is a web-based AI music generator with a composition-focused workflow.

**Preset workflow** (easiest):
1. Pick a preset style (Epic, Dramatic, Mysterious, etc.)
2. Set duration (30 seconds to 5+ minutes)
3. Set tempo and key
4. Generate

AIVA outputs a full orchestral composition. You can regenerate to get variations or tweak the style settings.

**Custom workflow** (advanced):
1. Upload a reference MIDI or audio file
2. Set "influence level" (how closely AIVA should follow your reference)
3. Choose instruments (strings, brass, woodwinds, percussion, piano)
4. Generate

AIVA analyzes your reference and generates a new composition with similar harmonic structure, orchestration density, and emotional arc. The output is not a copy — it's a new piece "in the style of" your reference.

**MIDI editing workflow**:
1. Generate music in AIVA
2. Download as MIDI (Standard plan and above)
3. Open MIDI in your DAW (Logic, Cubase, Ableton, etc.)
4. Edit notes, swap instruments, adjust timing, rewrite sections
5. Export final audio from your DAW

This is the workflow for professional composers who want AI to draft the foundation and then refine it manually.
