## What Midjourney Actually Does

Midjourney takes text prompts and reference images and generates high-quality images in seconds. But the real power is in the control system.

**Style References (--sref)** let you upload any image and Midjourney will match its color palette, lighting, texture quality, and composition style to your new generations. A designer can upload their brand's moodboard and every generated image will match the visual language. This alone makes Midjourney viable for commercial brand work in a way that prompt-only generators cannot match.

**Character References (--cref)** solve the hardest problem in AI image generation: making the same character appear consistently across multiple images. Upload a reference image of a character, and Midjourney will maintain their face, build, and clothing across different scenes, lighting conditions, and compositions. Game studios and comic artists rely on this for concept art and visual development.

**The Web Editor** (midjourney.com) now offers a full canvas experience: generate images, select regions to re-generate (inpainting), extend images beyond their original borders (outpainting/pan), and create zoom-out variations. The 2026 V7 model added real-time preview generation, letting you see a rough sketch before committing to a full render — dramatically reducing wasted GPU time.

Midjourney V7 (released late 2025) brought three major improvements: better text rendering within images, more accurate hand and finger anatomy, and a 2x speed improvement over V6. The model now generates 1024x1024 images by default with optional 2x and 4x upscaling.

## Image Quality and Aesthetic Control

Midjourney's quality advantage is most visible in five areas where other generators consistently fall short:

- **Lighting**: Midjourney understands light direction, diffusion, color temperature, and bounce light. A prompt like "golden hour backlight through sheer curtains" produces physically plausible lighting, not a flat approximation.
- **Composition**: The model has an innate sense of the rule of thirds, leading lines, negative space, and focal depth. You do not need to specify composition rules in your prompt — Midjourney applies them automatically.
- **Texture**: Fabric, skin, metal, wood, glass, water — Midjourney renders surface textures with tactile realism. This is why fashion and product designers prefer it.
- **Color harmony**: Analogous, complementary, triadic — Midjourney's color palettes feel intentional. Competitors often produce images where colors clash or feel muddy.
- **Atmosphere**: Mist, haze, volumetric light, particle effects — Midjourney excels at creating mood. This matters more than resolution for emotional impact.

Where Midjourney is weaker: strict photorealism (DALL-E 3 is better at "looks like a photograph"), text rendering within images (improved in V7 but still imperfect), and precise object placement (Stable Diffusion with ControlNet gives more spatial control).

## Speed and Workflow

V7 generates 1024x1024 images in about 15-30 seconds on the Pro plan, with upscaling adding another 10-15 seconds. The web editor's real-time preview cuts iteration time significantly — you see a rough composition in 3-5 seconds and can adjust before committing.

The Discord-based workflow requires learning Midjourney's command syntax (`/imagine`, `--ar 16:9`, `--sref`, `--cref`, `--stylize`), which has a learning curve. The web editor simplifies this with GUI controls, but power users still prefer Discord for batch generation and community prompt sharing.
