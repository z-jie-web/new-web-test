# Synthesia Review

Synthesia is one of the best-known AI avatar video platforms for training, onboarding, explainers, and multilingual presentation content, helping companies scale professional communication without studio production costs.

## Key Features

- **AI Avatars**
- **Business Video**

## Our Take

Synthesia is the enterprise standard for AI avatar video. The avatar quality, language support (140+ languages), and compliance certifications make it the default choice for large organizations. For corporate training, onboarding, and internal communications at scale, nothing else comes close. The main drawback is cost — it is priced for teams and enterprises, not solo creators. If you are an individual or small team, HeyGen or D-ID offer more accessible entry points. But for Fortune 500 companies that need SOC 2 compliance and enterprise security, Synthesia is the answer.

## Pricing

Starter plan at $22/month (1 editor, 10 video credits). Creator plan at $67/month. Enterprise plans with custom pricing for teams and organizations.
