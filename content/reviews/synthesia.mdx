# Synthesia Review

Synthesia is one of the best-known AI avatar video platforms for training, onboarding, explainers, and multilingual presentation content, helping companies scale professional communication without studio production costs.

## Key Features

- **AI Avatars**
- **Business Video**

## Our Take

Synthesia is the enterprise standard for AI avatar video — and for regulated industries, it is effectively the only choice. With SOC 2 Type II and ISO 42001 certifications (the only platform in its category with both), 240+ stock avatars, and 140+ language support, Synthesia dominates the corporate L&D and internal communications market. The 2026 additions — Expressive Avatars with gestures, AI Playground with integrated Sora 2 and Veo 3.1 for B-roll, AI Script Assistant — have closed some of the realism gap with HeyGen. However, the pricing model remains enterprise-biased. SCORM export and 1-click translation are locked behind custom Enterprise pricing, and custom avatars cost an additional ~$1,000/year each. For Fortune 500 companies that need compliance, Synthesia is the default. For solo creators and small teams, HeyGen offers better value.

## What Synthesia Actually Does

Synthesia creates videos of AI avatars speaking your script. You type or paste text, select an avatar, choose a language and voice, and Synthesia renders a video of that avatar delivering your script with realistic lip-sync and facial expressions. There is no filming, no studio, no talent to schedule.

The editor is slide-based, similar to PowerPoint — add slides with text, images, screen recordings, and AI-generated B-roll. The 2026 AI Playground integration gives direct access to Sora 2 and Veo 3.1 for generative video B-roll, letting you supplement avatar scenes with AI-generated background footage. The AI Script Assistant converts rough notes or bullet points into structured video scripts.

The avatar library has grown to 240+ stock avatars spanning diverse ages, ethnicities, and professional attire. Expressive Avatars (2026) added gesture capabilities and emotional range — they still feel slightly stiff compared to a real presenter in highly emotional content, but for the business use cases Synthesia serves (training, onboarding, product demos, internal comms), the quality is more than sufficient. Custom Digital Twins require an Enterprise plan and a studio capture session, costing ~$1,000/year per avatar on top of the subscription.

Voice support covers 140+ languages with 400+ voice options. AI voice cloning is available at the Enterprise tier. Output is 1080p full HD — not 4K, but adequate for LMS platforms, corporate portals, and web embedding.

Template coverage is strong for business use cases: 250+ professionally designed templates for training modules, sales pitches, HR announcements, product walkthroughs, and compliance explainers.

## Enterprise Compliance

This is Synthesia's moat. SOC 2 Type II certification covers security, availability, and confidentiality controls. ISO 42001 certification covers AI governance — how the models are trained, what data is used, how outputs are managed. SAML SSO, audit trails, and role-based access controls are standard on Enterprise. For financial services, healthcare, government, and any industry with regulatory oversight, these certifications are not optional — they are table stakes. No other AI avatar platform holds both certifications as of 2026.

However, Synthesia does not have CCPA compliance, which creates a gap for organizations handling California consumer data.

## Pricing Breakdown

| Plan | Monthly (Annual) | Video Minutes | Key Limitations |
|------|-----------------|---------------|-----------------|
| Free | $0 | 3 min/mo | 6-9 avatars, branded output |
| Starter | $18-22 | 120 min/yr (10 min/mo) | 70-125+ avatars, 1 editor + 3 guests |
| Creator | $59-67 | 360 min/yr (30 min/mo) | 90-180+ avatars, personal avatars, API |
| Enterprise | Custom | Unlimited | SCORM export, 1-click translation, SSO/SAML, custom digital twins |

Important caveats: SCORM export (for LMS platforms) and 1-click translation are locked behind Enterprise, which requires a custom quote. Custom avatars add ~$1,000/year per avatar. Video minute caps on Starter and Creator are annual, not monthly — once you hit the limit, you wait until the renewal date.

## Who Should Use Synthesia

**Synthesia is a strong fit if you:**
- Work in a regulated industry (finance, healthcare, government) requiring SOC 2/ISO 42001 compliance
- Run corporate L&D at scale — onboarding, compliance training, product certification
- Need 140+ language support for global workforce communications
- Value enterprise features: SSO, audit trails, role-based access
- Have budget for custom avatars and Enterprise-tier features

**Synthesia is not the best choice if you:**
- Are a solo creator or small business on a budget (use HeyGen at $24/mo)
- Need photorealistic avatars for social media or marketing (HeyGen Avatar IV is more realistic)
- Need SCORM export without Enterprise pricing (HeyGen includes SCORM on Creator plan)
- Want real-time conversational avatars (Synthesia is pre-rendered video, not live interaction)
- Operate under CCPA jurisdiction (no CCPA compliance)

## vs HeyGen

HeyGen offers more photorealistic avatars (Avatar IV), more accessible SCORM export (included on Creator at $24/month vs Synthesia Enterprise), and a credit-based system that can be cheaper for low-to-medium volume. Synthesia counters with enterprise governance (SOC 2 Type II + ISO 42001), deeper LMS integrations, and a more structured corporate workflow. For regulated enterprises, Synthesia is the safe choice. For everyone else, HeyGen offers better value and realism.

## FAQ

**Can I create a custom avatar of myself?** Yes, via Custom Digital Twin on Enterprise plans. Requires a studio capture session and costs ~$1,000/year per avatar on top of your subscription.

**What languages does Synthesia support?** 140+ languages with 400+ voice options. AI voice cloning available on Enterprise.

**Is Synthesia suitable for marketing content?** Yes, but HeyGen typically delivers more photorealistic results for customer-facing marketing. Synthesia excels at internal corporate content where compliance matters more than photorealism.
