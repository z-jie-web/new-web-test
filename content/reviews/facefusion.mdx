# FaceFusion Review

FaceFusion is the leading open-source face swap and enhancement tool, offering professional-grade face replacement with no usage limits, watermarks, or paywalls.

## Key Features

- **Open Source** — Full access to source code, completely free
- **Face Swap** — High-quality face replacement with fine-tuning controls
- **Face Enhancement** — Built-in AI enhancement to improve output quality

## Our Take

FaceFusion is the best face swap tool for technically inclined users. The setup barrier is real — you need Python and a decent GPU — but once running, the output quality rivals and often exceeds paid alternatives. For professional work where quality and privacy matter, it is the clear winner. Not for casual users.

## Pricing

Free — fully open source. No paid tiers, no watermarks, no usage limits.
