## Our Take

Fathom's free tier is the strongest in the AI meeting transcription category — not close. Otter gives you 300 minutes per month with a 30-minute-per-meeting cap. Fireflies gives you 800 minutes of storage total. Fathom gives you unlimited everything, no credit card, setup in under 2 minutes. If you are a solo founder, consultant, or individual contributor who just wants to stop taking notes, install Fathom today. There is no reason not to.

The 5-summary cap on the free tier is the business model. Advanced AI summaries (the ones that structure the meeting into topics, decisions, and action items) stop after 5 per month. After that, you get "basic" summaries — essentially a shorter transcript rather than a structured breakdown. For someone doing 20+ meetings a month — which is a normal week for most sales reps and consultants — this cap makes Premium ($15/mo) a near-certainty by Wednesday. For sales teams, Fathom is a legitimate Fireflies competitor at a lower price point. For teams running structured sales methodologies (MEDDIC, BANT), Fireflies' field-level mapping still wins.

## What Fathom Actually Does

Fathom is a meeting bot. You connect your Google or Outlook calendar, and it automatically joins every scheduled Zoom, Google Meet, or Microsoft Teams call as "Fathom Notetaker." It records the call, transcribes the conversation with speaker labels, and delivers a structured summary within 30 seconds of the meeting ending.

During the call, you can click a highlight button to bookmark key moments. These highlights appear in the summary and are auto-tagged by context. You can also create video clips — select a segment of the recording, and Fathom generates a shareable clip without requiring video editing software.

After the call, the summary lands in your inbox and Slack (if connected). It includes: a high-level overview, key topics discussed, decisions made, action items with owners, and a full timestamped transcript. You can search across all past meetings by keyword, participant, or date. The "Ask Fathom" AI chatbot (Premium+) can answer questions about your meeting history.

For teams, the workspace provides a shared view of all team meetings. Managers can see which calls were attended, review summaries, and track deal progress (Team Pro). View-only users — execs who want to browse but not record — are free.

**The CRM integration** on paid plans auto-logs meeting summaries to the contact or deal record in Salesforce, HubSpot, or Close. This happens automatically after each call — no manual copy-paste. Team Pro adds Deal View: a dashboard that shows which deals had meetings, what was discussed, and whether next steps were committed.

## FAQ

**Is Fathom really free?** Yes. The free tier includes unlimited recordings, unlimited storage, and unlimited transcription length with no time caps. You get 5 advanced AI summaries per month. After that, summaries are "basic" — shorter, less structured, but still functional. No credit card required.

**How accurate is Fathom's transcription?** ~90-95% for clear single-speaker English. Accuracy drops with heavy accents, overlapping speech, background noise, or technical jargon. Users report occasional timestamp inaccuracies since recent updates — a 3-hour call might show as 6 minutes in the recording timeline.

**Does Fathom work on mobile?** No. Fathom is designed for desktop video calls only — Zoom, Google Meet, and Microsoft Teams. There is no mobile app for recording in-person conversations. If you need mobile recording, use Otter.ai's iOS/Android apps.

**Can I hide the Fathom bot from participants?** No. Fathom always joins as a visible participant ("Fathom Notetaker"). All participants are notified that the meeting is being recorded. There is no invisible or stealth mode.

**What happens when I cancel?** Your existing recordings and transcripts remain accessible. The bot stops joining new meetings immediately. Premium features (Ask Fathom, CRM sync) are disabled.

**How many languages does Fathom support?** Transcription supports 25 languages. This is fewer than Fireflies (100+) and WhisperX (99+), but covers the major business languages.
