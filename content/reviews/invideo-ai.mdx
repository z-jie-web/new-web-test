# InVideo AI Review

InVideo AI turns prompts into complete videos with scripts, visuals, and voice layers, making it especially useful for marketers, publishers, and business users who want fast production without advanced editing skills.

## Key Features

- **Script to Video**
- **Marketing**

## Our Take

InVideo AI is the closest thing to "type a topic, get a publishable video" — and in 2026, it has evolved far beyond basic template assembly. With 200+ integrated AI models (including Sora 2 as its first official partner), AI Twins for digital avatars, and Agent One for production planning, InVideo has become a video creation platform rather than just a template tool. The output can still feel template-driven on lower plans, but the Plus plan at $25/month ($17 annual) offers strong value for marketers who need video volume. The dual credit system (video minutes vs AI credits) is the main friction point — it is easy to underestimate usage. For marketers, publishers, and business users who want to turn ideas into videos without learning editing, InVideo is the most complete all-in-one solution.

## What InVideo AI Actually Does

InVideo AI generates complete videos from a single prompt. You describe the topic, target audience, platform, and tone — InVideo writes the script, selects stock footage and images from a 16M+ asset library (iStock, Storyblocks, Shutterstock), generates AI voiceover, adds background music, and assembles everything into a timeline. The entire process takes minutes from prompt to draft.

The 2026 **Agent One** release shifts the paradigm from "prompting" to "directing." Instead of typing a one-line prompt and hoping for the best, Agent One builds a production deck with storyboards, scene descriptions, and animatics before rendering. This is aimed at filmmakers and marketing teams who need more creative control than "AI, surprise me."

**AI Twins** create digital avatars from a YouTube link or uploaded recording — useful for consistent brand presence across videos without filming each one. Voice cloning uses a 30-second audio snippet via ElevenLabs integration. 200+ AI models are integrated, including Sora 2, Veo 3.1, Kling 3.0, PixVerse, and Hailuo, selectable per scene.

The **Magic Editor** lets you refine with natural language: "make the intro faster," "swap the third scene's background music," "add captions in Spanish." Template support covers 7,000+ options across YouTube, Instagram, TikTok, and LinkedIn formats.

Export ranges from 720p (Free, watermarked) to 1080p (Plus) to 4K (Max+). Frame rates from 24-120fps. Commercial license is included on all paid plans.

## Pricing Breakdown

InVideo uses a dual-credit system: **video minutes** for template-based rendering and **AI credits** for generative AI features. Different models cost different amounts — a Kling 2.6 clip might use 0.5 credits while a Veo 3.1 4K video can consume 10+.

| Plan | Monthly (Annual) | AI Minutes | AI Credits | Resolution |
|------|-----------------|-----------|------------|------------|
| Free | $0 | ~2-10 min/week | 1/week | 720p, watermarked |
| Plus | $25 ($17 annual) | 50 min/mo | 10/mo | 1080p |
| Max | $60 ($40 annual) | 200 min/mo | 40/mo | 4K |
| Generative | $96-$120 | 200 min/mo | 100/mo | 4K |
| Team | $899 | 2,000 min/mo | 1,000/mo | 4K |

Credits do not roll over. Regeneration counts as new usage. The Plus plan at $17/month (annual) is the practical entry point for individual creators making 5-15 videos per week. Max at $40/month (annual) is needed for 4K export and serious volume.

## Who Should Use InVideo AI

**InVideo is a strong fit if you:**
- Want to turn blog posts, articles, or ideas into videos at scale
- Are a marketer or small business owner without video editing skills
- Need to produce video ad variants for A/B testing
- Want an all-in-one solution: script, media, voiceover, editing in one tool
- Run a faceless YouTube channel requiring consistent video output

**InVideo is not the best choice if you:**
- Need manual timeline control and fine-grained editing
- Want cinematic-quality original footage (AI generators are supplementary)
- Produce primarily short-form social content (CapCut or Captions are more optimized)
- Need offline editing capability

## vs Runway and CapCut

InVideo, Runway, and CapCut serve different video creation approaches. InVideo assembles complete templated videos from prompts — script, stock footage, voiceover, music — making it best for marketers who need publishable videos without editing skills. Runway generates original AI video clips from scratch with cinematic quality, best for creative professionals who want raw AI-generated footage to edit. CapCut is the mobile-first editor for short-form social content with a massive template library. If your goal is turning text into a complete video without touching a timeline, InVideo is the most efficient path.

## FAQ

**Is InVideo free?** The free tier has ~10 AI minutes per week, 720p, and a hardcoded watermark. It is a trial, not a usable free plan. Plus at $17/month (annual) is the minimum for publishing.

**What is the difference between InVideo and Runway?** InVideo generates complete templated videos from prompts (script, stock footage, VO). Runway generates original AI video clips from scratch. They serve different needs: InVideo for complete video assembly, Runway for original AI-generated footage.
