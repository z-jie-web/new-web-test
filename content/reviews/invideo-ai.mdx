# InVideo AI Review

InVideo AI turns prompts into complete videos with scripts, visuals, and voice layers, making it especially useful for marketers, publishers, and business users who want fast production without advanced editing skills.

## Key Features

- **Script to Video**
- **Marketing**

## Our Take

InVideo AI is the closest thing to "type a topic, get a publishable video." It handles script writing, media selection, voiceover, and editing in one flow. The output can feel template-driven — you will not win cinematography awards — but for marketers, small businesses, and publishers who need volume, it is incredibly efficient. If your goal is to turn blog posts into videos or create ad variants at scale, InVideo is one of the best tools for that specific job.

## Pricing

Free plan with watermarked exports. Paid plans start at $20/month for HD exports, stock media access, and no watermark. Business plan at $60/month for teams.
