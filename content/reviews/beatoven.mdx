Beatoven takes a different approach to AI music generation: instead of making standalone tracks, it reads your video and composes a score around it. Upload a product launch video with fast cuts and dramatic reveals, and Beatoven builds tension with swelling synths and percussive hits timed to your edits. Upload a calm interview podcast, and the music stays in the background, subtle pads and sparse piano that support the conversation without competing. Where [Suno](/reviews/suno) generates complete songs and [Soundraw](/reviews/soundraw) cranks out beats, Beatoven's output is a soundtrack, music that changes because the picture changes.

## Key Features

- **Video-Aware Mood-Based Generation** ,  Upload your video file and Beatoven analyzes scene transitions, color grading, motion intensity, and pacing. The AI composes a music track that follows the emotional trajectory: quiet during intimate dialogue, swelling during reveals, energetic during montages. [Soundraw](/reviews/soundraw) asks you to pick genre and mood from menus. Beatoven reads your content and responds, no menu navigation required.
- **Multi-Track Stem Export** ,  Download your generated music as separated stems: melody, harmony, bass, drums, and effects. Import these into Premiere Pro, Final Cut, DaVinci Resolve, or any DAW. Adjust levels per stem, replace individual elements, remix the composition. Stem export is what separates Beatoven from one-shot generators, you get editable components, not a locked stereo file.
- **Text-to-Music with Scene Description** ,  Generate music without video by describing the scene: "30-second intro for a tech podcast, energetic but professional, synth-forward." Beatoven matches the described context. Handy for podcast intros, outros, and transitions where you know the emotional need but have no video file. See [Beatoven's help center](https://help.beatoven.ai/) for guides on text-to-music workflows and stem export configuration.
- **Context-Aware Dynamic Composition** ,  Beatoven's engine adjusts tempo, instrument density, and harmonic tension based on what it detects in your content. Rapid cuts get more percussion and energy. Slow landscape pans get ambient pads and sparse piano. The output feels scored to picture, not pasted underneath. According to [ASCAP's guide on production music licensing](https://www.ascap.com/help/ascap-licensing), scored music that responds to visual timing needs different rights treatment than background tracks, and Beatoven's output maps to the production music model.
- **Royalty-Free Commercial License** ,  All paid plans include full commercial rights for films, advertisements, YouTube, podcasts, corporate videos, and client work. No attribution, no additional licensing fees. Beatoven trains on original compositions and licensed material, sidestepping the copyright gray zone that dogs tools trained on scraped music.

## Our Take

Beatoven is narrow. It doesn't try to make songs, beats, or standalone tracks. It scores video, and it's the only AI tool that does this well. Upload your edit, wait 1-3 minutes, get back music that rises and falls with your cuts. That's the whole product.

The quality depends heavily on what you feed it. Drop in a brand film with dramatic reveals and color-graded transitions, you get orchestral swells and tension builders that sound like a human composer spent an afternoon on them. Drop in a vlog with jump cuts and handheld footage, the results are less impressive, Beatoven wasn't designed for that kind of content. [Mubert](/reviews/mubert) handles beat-driven casual content better. [Soundraw](/reviews/soundraw) gives you more control over genre and structure. Beatoven's lane is narrow: cinematic, emotional, narrative-driven scoring where the music needs to follow the picture.

The generation wait is real. 30 seconds is fine for a social clip. 2 minutes for a 5-minute scene is workable. But if you're scoring a 20-minute documentary segment, you're looking at several minutes of processing, and the output might need regeneration if the AI misreads a scene's emotional intent. This isn't a dealbreaker for film work where you're already spending hours on a single scene. It's a bigger issue for content creators who need music fast and frequently.

The free tier's 3 monthly downloads are too few to evaluate the tool properly. One short film, one test. [Suno](/reviews/suno) gives you 50 credits daily on its free tier. [Soundraw](/reviews/soundraw) lets you preview unlimited beats. Beatoven's trial is the stingiest in AI music.

## What Beatoven Really Does

Beatoven's workflow starts with a video upload or text description. Upload a video and Beatoven runs scene detection: it finds cuts, measures motion intensity, checks color temperature, maps pacing. This takes 30-60 seconds.

Next, pick a **mood profile**: emotional (warm, hopeful, melancholic), energetic (driving, uplifting, tense), atmospheric (ambient, mysterious, serene), or custom. Adjust **instrumentation**: piano-forward, synth-based, orchestral, guitar-driven, or electronic. Beatoven composes a track matching both the video's dynamics and your preferences.

The composition lands in Beatoven's timeline view, where you see how the music maps to each scene. Tweak the intensity curve. Lengthen or shorten sections. Regenerate segments that miss the mark. Export as a full mix (MP3, WAV) or separated stems for your DAW.

For **podcast production**, skip the video and describe your show: "45-minute interview podcast about technology, conversational tone, need intro music (30 seconds, energetic), transition stings (5 seconds), and background bed (subtle, continuous)." Beatoven generates each element with consistent instrumentation, so your intro, transitions, and bed share a sonic identity.

## FAQ

**Can I use Beatoven music in commercial projects like client videos and ads?**
Paid plans (Creator, Pro, Business) all include a commercial license covering client work, ads, films, YouTube monetization, and corporate content. The license is perpetual, your generated tracks stay licensed even if you cancel. Free tier downloads are non-commercial only.

**How does Beatoven's video analysis work for music scoring?**
Beatoven scans your video for scene transitions, motion intensity, color grading, and pacing, then maps an emotional trajectory. Quiet scenes get sparse arrangements. Reveals get swells. Montages get energy. If the AI misreads a scene's emotional intent, you can adjust the intensity curve manually after generation.

**Why does Beatoven take longer to generate music than other AI music tools?**
The extra time goes into scene analysis. Before composing, Beatoven processes your video for 30-60 seconds to understand the structure. Composition adds another 30 seconds to 2 minutes depending on track length. [Suno](/reviews/suno) skips video analysis entirely (30-second generation for any track). [Mubert](/reviews/mubert) streams in real time with no analysis step. You're trading speed for context-awareness.

**Can I edit Beatoven's generated music after export?**
Creator and Pro plans export separated stems: melody, harmony, bass, drums, effects. Load them into Ableton, Logic Pro, FL Studio, Premiere Pro, Final Cut, or DaVinci Resolve. Adjust levels, swap instruments, add effects, remix to fit your project.
