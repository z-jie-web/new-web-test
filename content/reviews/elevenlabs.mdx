- **Text to Speech** — Convert text to natural-sounding speech with 40+ voice models

## What ElevenLabs Actually Does

ElevenLabs converts text into spoken audio using deep learning voice models trained on real human speech. Unlike robotic TTS of the past, ElevenLabs voices have natural prosody, emotion, breath patterns, and micro-inflections — they sound like a person reading, not a machine reading.

The platform is built on a two-tier model system. **Flash v2.5** delivers near-instant output (~75ms time-to-first-audio) at lower quality — suitable for voice agents, IVR systems, and real-time applications. **Turbo v2.5** balances speed and quality for most use cases. **Multilingual v2** and **Eleven v3** produce the most natural output with emotion, pacing, and 70+ language support, at the cost of slower generation (1 credit per character for the highest quality).

**Instant Voice Cloning** (Starter plan, $5/month) works from 30 seconds to 2 minutes of audio — enough for quick narration projects. **Professional Voice Cloning** (Creator plan, $22/month) requires 30 minutes of clean audio and produces near-indistinguishable results, capturing breath patterns and emotional range. This is the tier audiobook publishers and branded content creators use.

ElevenLabs expanded into multimodal territory: **Sound Effects** generates audio effects from text prompts, **AI Music** produces background tracks, and **Dubbing Studio** provides frame-accurate video dubbing with voice preservation across 29 languages. The **Conversational AI** platform supports building voice agents with sub-200ms latency on the Business tier.

## Pricing Breakdown

ElevenLabs uses a credit system where 1 character of generated speech = 1 credit on premium models, and ~0.5 credits on Flash/Turbo. Credits reset monthly; unused credits roll over for up to 2 months on paid plans.

| Plan | Monthly Price | Credits/Month | Key Features |
|------|-------------|---------------|--------------|
| Free | $0 | 10,000 (~10 min) | Basic TTS, no commercial license, no voice cloning |
| Starter | $5 | 30,000 (~30 min) | Commercial license, Instant Voice Cloning |
| Creator | $22 ($11 first mo) | 100,000 (~100 min) | Professional Voice Cloning, 192 kbps API, overage billing |
| Pro | $99 | 500,000 (~500 min) | 44.1 kHz PCM via API, reduced overage rates |
| Scale | $330 | 2,000,000 (~2,000 min) | 3 team seats, shared credit pool, lower overage |
| Business | $1,320 | 11,000,000 (~11,000 min) | Low-latency TTS (~75ms), 3 PVCs included |

Annual billing saves roughly 17% across all plans. Creator ($22/month annual) is the practical entry point for professionals — it unlocks Professional Voice Cloning and 192 kbps output quality. The free tier watermark requirement makes it unsuitable for commercial publishing.

## Who Should Use ElevenLabs

**ElevenLabs is a strong fit if you:**
- Produce audiobooks, podcasts, or narrated YouTube content regularly
- Need voice cloning for branded content or recurring characters
- Build voice AI applications or conversational agents
- Localize content across multiple languages with natural-sounding output
- Need commercial licensing for voice content (Starter plan and above)

**ElevenLabs is not the best choice if you:**
- Only occasionally need TTS for personal projects (free tier suffices)
- Need real-time streaming on a budget (latency-optimized tier starts at $1,320/month)
- Want a simple pay-per-use model without monthly commitments (PAYG is available but requires subscription)
- Need offline text-to-speech (ElevenLabs is cloud-only)

## vs Murf AI and Play.ht

ElevenLabs leads in voice quality and cloning accuracy, but competitors offer different strengths. **Murf AI** ($19/month Creator) provides a more polished studio interface with built-in video sync and timeline editing, making it better for presentation-style content. Its voice quality trails ElevenLabs but the workflow is more beginner-friendly. **Play.ht** ($39/month Creator) matches ElevenLabs on voice quality for English and offers a simpler per-word pricing model that some users find easier to budget. For open-source TTS with top-tier benchmarks, [Fish Audio](/reviews/fish-audio) is a strong alternative at 70% lower cost. For reading and accessibility rather than content creation, [Speechify](/reviews/speechify) is the better fit — it's built for consuming text, not producing voiceovers. For serious voice AI work, ElevenLabs is the industry standard.

## FAQ

**Can I use ElevenLabs for commercial projects?** Yes, on any paid plan (Starter and above). Free tier output requires attribution and cannot be monetized.

**How good is voice cloning?** Professional Voice Cloning (Creator plan, $22/month) produces near-indistinguishable results from 30 minutes of clean audio. Instant Voice Cloning (Starter, $5/month) is good for quick projects but lacks the emotional range of the professional tier.

**How many languages does ElevenLabs support?** 70+ languages on the Multilingual v2/Eleven v3 models, with automatic language detection. The Flash and Turbo models support fewer languages but are faster and cheaper.
