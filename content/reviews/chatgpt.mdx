ChatGPT is the Swiss Army knife of AI chatbots. It browses the web, generates images, writes and runs code, analyzes uploaded files, and speaks in natural conversation. No other AI tool bundles this many capabilities into one interface. The trade-off: writing quality trails [Claude](/reviews/claude), research accuracy trails [Perplexity](/reviews/perplexity), and the free tier is throttled when demand spikes. But if you want one AI subscription that covers every use case, ChatGPT is the most complete package.

## Key Features

- **Web Browsing** — ChatGPT searches the internet in real time and cites sources. Ask about today's news, current pricing, recent product launches, or anything that requires up-to-date information. The browsing experience is conversational: it reads pages, extracts answers, and follows up with you for clarification. Claude cannot do this. Gemini can, but often mixes sources less cleanly.
- **DALL-E 3 Image Generation** — Describe an image in natural language, ChatGPT creates it. No prompt syntax to learn. Say "a watercolor painting of a cat reading a book in a cozy library with warm lighting" and you get exactly that. You can refine through conversation: "make the cat orange," "change to nighttime." The integration with the chat interface means image creation feels like a natural part of the conversation, not a separate tool.
- **GPT Store** — Over 3 million custom GPTs built by the community. Need a travel itinerary planner? A coding tutor? A workout coach? A legal document reviewer? Someone has probably built a specialized GPT for it. Custom GPTs combine system prompts with uploaded knowledge files and specific capabilities. For teams, this means creating a GPT with your company's style guide, product catalog, and support docs, then sharing it with the whole team.
- **Code Interpreter (Advanced Data Analysis)** — Upload a CSV, Excel file, or JSON dataset, and ChatGPT writes and executes Python code to analyze it. It generates charts, runs statistical tests, cleans data, and explains findings in plain English. For analysts, researchers, and anyone who works with data but doesn't want to write pandas boilerplate, this feature alone justifies the $20/month subscription.
- **Voice Mode** — Real-time spoken conversation with natural pauses, intonation, and turn-taking. It feels like talking to a person, not a robot. Useful for brainstorming while driving, practicing language conversation, or getting quick answers without typing. The voice mode supports multiple languages and accents.
- **File Uploads** — Upload PDFs, images, spreadsheets, or code files. ChatGPT reads them, analyzes them, and answers questions. Upload a contract and ask "what are the termination clauses?" Upload a photo and ask "what plant is this?" Upload a spreadsheet and ask "what's the trend in Q3 revenue?"

## Our Take

ChatGPT is the default AI tool for a reason: it does everything reasonably well. The browsing feature answers questions no other major AI chatbot can (Claude is offline, Perplexity is search-only). DALL-E integration means you don't need a separate image generator. The GPT Store extends functionality in ways competitors haven't matched. And the $20/month Plus plan is priced identically to Claude Pro, making the comparison a question of strengths, not budget.

The writing is the weak spot. ChatGPT prose is competent but has a formulaic structure: opening summary, bullet points, concluding recap. It hedges excessively ("one should consider," "however, it bears mentioning"). For emails, reports, and creative work where voice matters, [Claude](/reviews/claude) produces more natural output. For research that requires cited sources, [Perplexity](/reviews/perplexity) has cleaner search results with better attribution.

The $200/month Pro plan is a head-scratcher. It gives you unlimited access to GPT-4o and o1 pro mode for complex reasoning tasks. Unless you're a researcher running intensive multi-step analysis daily, the $20 Plus plan gives you everything you need. The 10x price jump from Plus to Pro is the least justified pricing tier in AI chatbots.

## vs Claude, Perplexity, and Gemini

[Claude](/reviews/claude) writes better. Its prose is more natural, its reasoning deeper, its context window larger (200K vs 128K). But Claude cannot browse the web. If you need current information, ChatGPT or Perplexity are the only options. Many professionals use both: research in ChatGPT or Perplexity, write in Claude. [Perplexity](/reviews/perplexity) is a search engine with AI summarization — it finds and cites sources more cleanly than ChatGPT's browsing. But Perplexity is a research tool, not a general-purpose AI. You can't generate images, write code, or build custom GPTs. Use Perplexity for deep research, ChatGPT for everything else. ## FAQ

**Is ChatGPT Plus worth $20/month?**
Yes, for anyone who uses AI daily. The free tier is throttled during peak hours and limited to GPT-4o mini. Plus opens up web browsing, DALL-E, code interpreter, and file uploads — features that change how you work with AI in practical ways. The free tier is for testing; Plus is for working.

**Can ChatGPT browse the internet?**
Yes, on Plus and higher plans. ChatGPT searches the web in real time and cites sources. This is the feature Claude cannot match. Free tier users get limited browsing.

**Is ChatGPT better than Claude?**
ChatGPT is more versatile (browsing, images, GPT Store). Claude is better at writing and reasoning. The right pick depends on your primary use case. Many professionals use both.

**Can ChatGPT generate images?**
Yes, via DALL-E 3 integration. Describe what you want, ChatGPT creates it. Refine through conversation. This is included in Plus at no extra cost, making it the most affordable way to access DALL-E 3.

**Is my data safe with ChatGPT?**
OpenAI states that Plus/Team/Enterprise conversations are not used for model training by default. Free tier conversations may be used for improvement. Check OpenAI's [privacy policy](https://openai.com/policies/privacy-policy) and configure data controls in settings before sharing sensitive information.

**What's the difference between GPT-4o and GPT-4o mini?**
GPT-4o is the full model: more accurate, better reasoning, handles hard problems. GPT-4o mini is a lighter, faster version for simpler queries. Free tier uses mini; Plus gives you the full model. For casual questions, mini is fine. For professional work, you want the full GPT-4o.
