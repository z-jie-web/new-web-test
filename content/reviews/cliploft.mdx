## What ClipLoft Actually Does

ClipLoft is a browser-based AI ad generator. You start by either pasting a product URL (ClipLoft auto-extracts product details and writes a script) or uploading a manual script. You pick an AI actor from the 300+ library — they span ages, ethnicities, and presentation styles from casual TikTok energy to polished spokesperson delivery.

The actor delivers your script with VocalMatch lip sync. If you enable product compositing, you upload a white-background product photo, and ClipLoft inserts it into the scene — the actor appears to hold and gesture with your actual product.

ClipLoft then generates the video in multiple aspect ratios (9:16 for TikTok/Reels, 16:9 for YouTube, 1:1 for feed ads) at up to 4K resolution. But here is where it gets interesting: you can generate up to 40 variants simultaneously, each with a different hook. One variant opens with an unboxing angle. Another leads with a testimonial. Another starts with a problem-solution setup. In our test session, we generated 40 variants and had them ready to upload in under 4 minutes. This is built for creative testing — run all 40 against each other in Meta's dynamic creative optimization and scale the winners.

The entire process — from product URL to 40 variants — takes about 3 minutes. Export directly to Meta Ads Manager or TikTok Ads Manager, or download for manual upload.

**The AI script writer** generates ad copy from your product URL or a brief. It pulls from a hook library based on real winning DTC ad angles. Good for first drafts and batch testing. For hero campaigns where copy matters deeply, write your own script — the AI is serviceable but not great.
