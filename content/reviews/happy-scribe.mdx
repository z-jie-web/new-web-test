# Happy Scribe Review

Happy Scribe is a trusted transcription and subtitling platform for creators, educators, and media teams who need reliable caption generation, translation support, and accessible video distribution across markets.

## Key Features

- **Transcription**
- **Translation**

## Our Take

Happy Scribe is the transcription specialist — it does one thing and does it reliably. The transcription accuracy is among the best in the industry, and the translation support makes it valuable for multilingual content teams. It is not a video editor, and it does not try to be one — if you need caption styling or video editing alongside transcription, pair it with Descript or VEED. For media teams, journalists, and educators who need accurate transcripts and multilingual subtitles with minimal fuss, Happy Scribe is the gold standard.

## Pricing

Pay-as-you-go at $0.20/minute for transcription. Subscription plans start at $17/month for 2 hours of transcription. Team and enterprise plans with volume discounts.
