## What Happy Scribe Really Does

Happy Scribe converts audio and video files into text with two service tiers. **AI transcription** processes files in minutes at ~80-85% accuracy across 120+ languages, with speaker identification, timestamps, and an interactive editor for correcting the output. **Human transcription** delivers up to 99% accuracy with turnaround in hours, performed by professional transcribers who handle accents, overlapping speech, and poor audio quality that AI struggles with.

The interactive editor is where most users spend their time: synchronized audio playback alongside the transcript, click-to-navigate timestamps, speaker labeling, and a find-and-replace system for correcting consistent AI errors (names, technical terms, brand spellings). Export formats cover every major use case: Word, PDF, TXT for documentation; SRT, VTT, STL, XML, FCPXML, EDL for video subtitles and professional editing software; hard-coded subtitled video for direct publishing.

The 2026 **AI Notetaker** automatically joins Zoom, Teams, and Google Meet calls to record, transcribe, and summarize meetings. **AI Copilot** lets you ask natural-language questions across your transcript library, "find the part where we discussed Q3 budget" ,  useful for research teams and journalists managing large interview archives.

Subtitle generation supports 120+ languages with customizable styling, though the styling options are more basic than dedicated video editors like VEED or CapCut. The platform integrates with Dropbox, Slack, and major meeting platforms.

## Who Should Use Happy Scribe

**Happy Scribe is a strong fit if you:**
- Are a journalist, researcher, or legal professional who needs highly accurate transcripts
- Produce multilingual content and need subtitles in 120+ languages
- Want the option of human-reviewed transcription for critical projects
- Need professional export formats (FCPXML, EDL, STL) for video editing software

**Happy Scribe is not the best choice if you:**
- Need a video editor with transcription built in (use Descript)

- Want the cheapest possible AI transcription (Rev and Otter offer competitive alternatives)

## FAQ

**How accurate is Happy Scribe's AI transcription?** ~80-85% for clean single-speaker audio. Accuracy drops with multiple speakers, accents, and background noise. Human transcription achieves up to 99% accuracy.

**What languages does Happy Scribe support?** 120+ languages and dialects for both AI and human transcription.

**Can I edit transcripts after they are generated?** Yes, the interactive editor lets you correct errors with synchronized audio playback and find-and-replace.
