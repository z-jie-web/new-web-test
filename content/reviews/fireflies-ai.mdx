## Our Take

Fireflies.ai solves a real problem: nobody takes good meeting notes. Even the most diligent note-taker misses things because they are busy participating. Fireflies takes note-taking off your plate, it joins, records, transcribes, summarizes, and files the notes in your CRM while you stay in the conversation. If you run 10+ client calls a week, the time savings alone justify the $10/month Pro plan.

The AI Credits system is where the pricing gets slippery. Fireflies advertises "unlimited transcription" on Pro, but AskFred queries and advanced analytics draw from a credit pool that is not unlimited on any plan. A heavy user asking AskFred a dozen questions per call across 10 calls per week will burn through credits faster than expected. Credit packs cost $5 for 50, small money individually, but they add up fast for teams.

Transcription accuracy is good, not great. Clean audio with native English speakers? ~95% accurate. Heavy accents, technical jargon, or 4+ people talking over each other? Accuracy drops noticeably. Every AI transcription tool struggles with this, but Fireflies makes it worse by not offering human review as a fallback, what the AI spits out is your final transcript, no appeals process.

The bot-in-the-room problem is real. Some meeting participants find the "Fireflies.ai Notetaker" joining their call creepy or invasive. Most people get used to it after the first few minutes, but client-facing calls with first-time prospects are where the friction is highest. There is no "invisible mode" ,  the bot is always visible as a participant.

## What Fireflies.ai Really Does

Fireflies.ai is not a transcription tool you open. It is a bot that lives in your calendar. You connect your Google or Outlook calendar once, and Fireflies automatically joins every scheduled meeting as a named participant ("Fireflies.ai Notetaker"). It records audio, transcribes the conversation in real time with speaker labels, and delivers a structured summary to your inbox and Slack within minutes of the call ending.

**AskFred** is the feature that elevates Fireflies from "transcription tool" to "meeting intelligence platform." Fred is an AI assistant trained on your entire meeting history. You can ask it questions like "summarize every decision made about the website redesign across the last 6 meetings" or "what objections did Acme Corp raise during our last three calls?" Fred searches across transcripts, timestamps its answers, and links back to the original recordings. This is the reason to pick Fireflies over simpler transcription tools, the cross-meeting search capability.

**The AI Skills Store** (200+ templates) automates post-meeting workflows. A "Sales Call Scorer" template evaluates the rep's performance against a scorecard. A "Candidate Interview Summarizer" extracts skills, experience, and red flags. A "Follow-up Email Generator" drafts a summary email to all participants with action items already filled in. These are not gimmicks, they save 5-10 minutes of post-call work per meeting, which compounds to hours per week for heavy meeting users.

**Conversation Intelligence** (Business plan, $19/user/month) adds analytics on top: talk-time ratios to identify who dominates meetings, sentiment trends across customer accounts, topic frequency tracking, and team-level dashboards for coaching. This is the tier that makes Fireflies a sales enablement tool rather than just a note-taker.

## Who Should Use Fireflies.ai

**Fireflies.ai is a strong fit if you:**
- Run 10+ client or team calls per week and waste time on manual note-taking
- Work in sales and need CRM auto-logging with conversation analytics
- Manage a remote team and want a searchable knowledge base of every meeting
- Use Salesforce, HubSpot, or Pipedrive and want call notes auto-filed to contact records

**Fireflies.ai is not the best choice if you:**
- Transcribe offline audio files or interviews primarily (use [WhisperX](/reviews/whisperx) for offline, or Sonix for file-based workflow)
- Need near-perfect transcription accuracy and want human review (use Rev or Happy Scribe)
- Are a solo creator who does 2-3 meetings a week (the free tier covers this, but [Otter.ai](/reviews/otter-ai) has a better free plan)
- Find the idea of a bot joining calls uncomfortable, there is no way to make Fireflies invisible

## FAQ

**Is Fireflies.ai really free?** The Free plan is genuinely usable, 800 minutes of storage, basic AI summaries, and limited integrations. You will outgrow it. The 800-minute cap fills up in about 15 hours of meetings, a month's worth if you do 4 calls a week. After that, you either pay or lose access to old transcripts.

**How accurate is the transcription?** ~95% for clear single-speaker English. Drops to ~85-90% with accents, overlapping speech, or technical terminology. No human review option is offered, you cannot pay extra for a human to verify transcripts.

**Does the bot work on mobile?** Yes. Fireflies has iOS and Android apps that can record in-person meetings. The bot still joins video calls on desktop platforms automatically.

**Can I prevent Fireflies from joining certain meetings?** Yes. You can set calendar filters (only join meetings with specific keywords, or exclude recurring 1:1s) and manually remove the bot from individual events. The bot does not join meetings marked "private."

**Is my meeting data secure?** Fireflies is SOC 2 Type II compliant on Enterprise plans. Data is encrypted in transit and at rest. Free and Pro plans use shared infrastructure; Enterprise gets private storage. The bot records all audio by default, participants are notified, but some organizations disable auto-recording for compliance reasons.

**What happens if I cancel?** Your transcripts remain accessible, but the bot stops joining new meetings immediately. You can export your data before cancellation.
