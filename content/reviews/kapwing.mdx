# Kapwing Review

Kapwing includes AI subtitle tools, online editing, resizing, and content repurposing features, making it useful for distributed teams that need a collaborative browser workflow for social and marketing video assets.

## Key Features

- **Online Editor**
- **Team Workflow**

## Our Take

Kapwing is the team-first online video editor. Where VEED focuses on individual creator speed, Kapwing invests more in collaboration — shared workspaces, commenting, version history. The AI subtitle tools are solid, and the multi-format resizing makes it easy to repurpose one video across platforms. It is the best choice for distributed content teams that need to work on the same projects together. Solo creators may find VEED slightly more streamlined, but for teams, Kapwing's collaboration features tip the scale.

## Pricing

Free plan with watermarks. Pro at $16/month for watermark-free, 4K, and unlimited projects. Business at $50/month per user for teams with collaboration features.
