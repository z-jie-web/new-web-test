About AI Video Hub

AI Video Hub is built for users searching for the best AI video generators, subtitle platforms, face swap tools, and enhancement software. The site is designed to grow organic traffic first, then monetize responsibly with carefully placed advertising.

What this site is for

Discovery

We help overseas users find trending AI video tools faster through organized categories, practical descriptions, and editorial recommendations that reduce research friction.

Comparison

We break tools into meaningful use cases such as video generation, face swap, subtitles, and enhancement so visitors can compare products without digging through generic search results.

Retention

The site is structured to encourage deeper browsing through category pages, tool detail pages, internal links, and content blocks that increase topical depth and session duration.

How we review AI video tools

  • We look at the primary commercial use case of each product.
  • We consider how well the tool matches real creator, agency, or brand workflows.
  • We summarize whether the product is strongest for discovery, production, editing, localization, or enhancement.
  • We keep category language simple so users and search engines can both understand the site structure clearly.

Why the site is static

A static HTML structure keeps load times fast, simplifies Vercel deployment, and helps the project scale into a larger SEO site with category pages, tool pages, sitemap coverage, and future monetization layers.
